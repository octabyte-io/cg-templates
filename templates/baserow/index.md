Baserow looks and feels like a spreadsheet, but works like a database. Each table holds one kind of thing, such as customers, orders or equipment, and tables can be linked, so you can see every order a customer has placed from their row.

You can view the same data as a grid, a board of cards, a calendar or a gallery, and share forms so people can add rows without seeing the rest. A small events company, for example, could keep its venues, suppliers and bookings in linked tables, with a board view to follow each event from enquiry to invoice.

Cloudgate installs Baserow on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign up becomes the admin and invites the team. Baserow imports CSV and Excel files and Airtable bases, and has templates for common uses such as a CRM, a content calendar or a stock list.
