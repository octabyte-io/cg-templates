Baserow is an open-source no-code database that looks and feels like a spreadsheet but works like a database. Each table holds one kind of thing, such as customers, orders or equipment, and tables can be linked, so you can see every order a customer has placed straight from their row.

You can filter, sort and group your data, look at it as a grid or as a gallery of cards, and share forms so people can add rows without seeing the rest. A small events company, for example, could keep its venues, suppliers and bookings in linked tables and collect new enquiries through a form on its website.

Baserow imports Airtable bases from a share link, as well as CSV, Excel, JSON and XML files, and has ready-made templates for uses such as a CRM, a content calendar or a stock list. Its API and webhooks connect it to other apps, directly or through tools like Zapier, Make and n8n. There is no phone app, but it works in a phone's web browser. Board and calendar views and row comments are part of Baserow's paid plans.

Cloudgate installs Baserow on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to create an account becomes the admin and invites the rest of the team.
