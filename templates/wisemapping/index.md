WiseMapping is a self-hosted mind mapping app that runs in your web browser, a free alternative to MindMeister, XMind and MindManager for teams that want their mind maps on their own server. You build maps of topics and branches, add notes, links and icons, and share each map with the people who need it.

A café owner, for example, can plan a spring menu launch as one map, with branches for new dishes, suppliers, promotion and kitchen training, then invite the head chef to edit it and the front-of-house team to view it. If a change goes wrong, the map's history brings back an earlier version.

WiseMapping opens XMind, MindManager, FreeMind and Freeplane files, so existing maps can come with you, and it exports to PDF, images, Markdown and text. Maps can be made public and embedded in a website or help page. It works in any browser, on a computer or a phone, in 13 languages.

WiseMapping is free under its own licence, which asks that the "powered by WiseMapping" notice stays on screen. Cloudgate installs it on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, together with the database it needs, and gives it a secure web address.
