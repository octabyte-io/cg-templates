Ghost is a publishing tool for people who write for an audience: bloggers, journalists, coaches and small media businesses. You write a post once and Ghost puts it on your website and sends it to your subscribers by email.

Readers can sign up for free, or pay a monthly or yearly fee for members-only posts. Payments go through your own Stripe account, so the income is yours without a platform taking a share. A local food writer, for example, could publish free recipes on the website and send a paid weekly newsletter with a meal plan.

Cloudgate installs Ghost on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. You then pick a theme, set your publication's name and logo, and invite other writers. Ghost works with Stripe for payments and Mailgun for sending newsletters, and you can import posts and subscribers from Substack, WordPress and other platforms.
