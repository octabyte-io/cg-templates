Mini QR is a free, open-source, self-hosted QR code generator and scanner, and an alternative to paid tools such as QR Code Generator PRO and QR TIGER. You type a link or choose a code type, style the code with your colours, logo and a framed caption, and download it as PNG, JPG or SVG.

It covers the codes a small business prints most: a link to your menu or booking page, your Wi-Fi login for guests, a contact card, an email or text message, a place on the map, and a calendar event. A café could, for example, make one matching set of codes in its own colours for the menu, the guest Wi-Fi and its review page, then upload a spreadsheet to make a code for every table.

There are no accounts, the codes are made in your browser, and Mini QR stores nothing on the server. The codes are static: they keep working with no subscription, but you cannot change their link after printing or count scans. Mini QR works in any browser and can be installed like an app on a phone or computer.

Cloudgate installs Mini QR on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address; open it and start making codes.
