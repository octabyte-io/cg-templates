Koillection is a self-hosted collection manager for keeping track of physical collections of any kind, a free alternative to CLZ's collector apps. Books, records, DVDs, stamps, board games, trading cards or figures: you make a collection, add items with photos, and record the details that matter to you.

Each item can carry your own fields, such as artist, year, condition or the shelf it sits on, and templates let you reuse a set of fields. Tags work across collections, a wishlist keeps track of what you're still hunting for, and loans show who borrowed what. A record collector, for example, could catalogue every album with its pressing year and condition, keep a wishlist for record fairs, and share a public page of the collection with friends.

Koillection doesn't download details on its own. You type them in, or set up a scraper that copies them from a web page such as Discogs. It runs in any browser, including on phones, and several people can have their own accounts.

Cloudgate installs Koillection on your server with its database and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open it creates the admin account.
