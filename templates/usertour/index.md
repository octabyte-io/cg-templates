Usertour is open-source user onboarding software that you host yourself, and an alternative to Appcues, Userpilot, Userflow and UserGuiding. It lets your team build product tours, onboarding checklists, banners, hotspots, surveys and an in-app help center, and show them to the right people inside your own web app.

You build everything in a visual editor: point at a button in your app, write the tip, and set who sees it and when. Checklists tick themselves off as users complete each task, and reports show how many people start and finish every tour. A small software company could, for example, greet each new customer with a three-step tour of its dashboard, a checklist of first tasks, and a short satisfaction survey after their first week.

The free Community Edition is MIT-licensed and has no limit on users or sessions. Removing Usertour's small branding mark, single sign-on, two-step sign-in and an audit log need a paid licence. Usertour works in web apps, not native phone apps, and a developer adds its code snippet to your app once. It connects to Segment, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap, PostHog, HubSpot and Zapier.

Cloudgate installs Usertour on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, with its database, and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open it creates the admin account.
