faq:
  - q: Is ChurchCRM free?
    a: Yes, ChurchCRM is free, open-source church management software under the MIT licence. There is no paid edition and no fee per member; you pay only for the server it runs on.
  - q: Is ChurchCRM a good alternative to Planning Center or Breeze?
    a: Yes, for keeping members, groups, check-in, attendance and giving records, with no subscription. ChurchCRM does not take online donations, plan worship services or offer a phone app, so a church that relies on those may still need another tool.
  - q: Can ChurchCRM take online donations?
    a: No, ChurchCRM records gifts but does not process card payments. Your office enters donations on deposit slips, and ChurchCRM produces each family's giving statement for the tax year, to print or email.
  - q: Can I move my members from Breeze or Planning Center to ChurchCRM?
    a: Partly. Export your people as a CSV file from your current system, then use ChurchCRM's spreadsheet import, which lets you match each column to a ChurchCRM field. There is no one-click importer, so check a few records after the import.
  - q: How do I sign in to ChurchCRM the first time?

  - q: Does ChurchCRM have a phone app?

ChurchCRM is free, open-source church management software that you can self-host, a no-subscription alternative to Planning Center and Breeze. It keeps your members and families, groups, Sunday school classes, events, attendance, volunteers and giving records in one place in your web browser.

The church office can see who came on Sunday, follow up with first-time guests, print class lists and mailing labels, and email any group. The treasurer enters gifts on deposit slips, tracks pledges by fund and sends every family its giving statement at the end of the year. A small church, for example, could put a tablet at the children's ministry door for check-in, schedule its worship team for the month, and email each family its year-end statement in an afternoon.

Members can sign in to their own page to update their details, see the calendar and answer volunteer requests. ChurchCRM can import your members from a spreadsheet, sync with Mailchimp, and send email through any email sending service you connect. It has no phone app and does not take online donations.

Cloudgate installs ChurchCRM on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, with its database, and gives it a secure web address. Sign in as Admin, fill in your church's details, and add your members. Your congregation's records stay on your server.
