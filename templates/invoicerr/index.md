Invoicerr is free, open-source, self-hosted invoicing software for freelancers: quotes your clients sign online, invoices and receipts, as an alternative to FreshBooks or Zoho Invoice. It keeps your clients, documents and payments on your own server, with no monthly fee and no limit on clients or invoices.

A web designer, for example, could send a bakery a quote for a rebrand. The bakery opens the email, confirms a code and signs it, the designer turns the signed quote into an invoice in one click, and when the bank transfer arrives, records it and sends a PDF receipt. Invoices can also be downloaded as Factur-X, ZUGFeRD or XRechnung e-invoices.

Invoicerr does invoicing only: there are no card payments, time tracking, bookkeeping or phone apps, and it can't import from other tools. It is built mostly by one maker, who now works on it part-time and reviews other people's contributions, so new features arrive slowly. It works in any web browser, in 18 languages.
