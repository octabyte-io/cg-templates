MAZANOKE is a free, open-source image optimizer you can self-host that shrinks, resizes and converts pictures right in your web browser, a private alternative to TinyPNG and iLoveIMG. You drop in your images, choose a quality or a target file size, and download smaller files.

All the work happens on your own computer or phone, so your pictures are never uploaded, not even to your own server. It removes hidden photo details such as location, opens iPhone HEIC photos, and converts between JPG, PNG, WebP and ICO. There are no limits, no ads and no sign-up. A small online shop, for example, could shrink a batch of product photos to under 500 KB each and convert the iPhone shots to JPG before adding them to the store, without sending them to a tool website.

MAZANOKE does one job: it has no cropping, editing or watermarks. For PDFs and many other quick tasks, OmniTools is the broader choice. MAZANOKE works in any browser and can be installed on a phone or computer like an app, which keeps working offline.

Cloudgate installs MAZANOKE on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. There is nothing to set up and no login, so share the address only with the people who should use it.
