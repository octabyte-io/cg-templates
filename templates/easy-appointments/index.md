Easy!Appointments is a free, open-source, self-hosted appointment booking app, an alternative to Acuity Scheduling and Calendly for businesses with staff and set services. Customers pick a service, a member of staff, a day and a free time on your booking page, and book without creating an account.

Your team works from one calendar that shows every appointment, break and day off. You set up each service with its length and price, and each person's working hours. Staff and customers get an email when a booking is made, changed or cancelled. A hair salon, for example, could let clients book a cut or colour with the stylist they like, and see the whole day's appointments for every chair in one view.

It syncs with Google Calendar and other calendars, can add a Jitsi or Google Meet link, and can send each booking to n8n, Zapier or Make. It works in any browser on a phone or computer; there is no phone app. It does not take payments or send reminders.

Cloudgate installs Easy!Appointments on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, together with the database it needs, and gives it a secure web address. Open that address right after setup to create the admin account.
