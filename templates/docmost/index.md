Docmost is a free, open-source team wiki and Confluence alternative that you can self-host: a place where your team writes, organises and finds its handbook, how-to guides, policies and meeting notes. Pages live in spaces, one for each team or department, and can be nested inside each other.

Several people can edit the same page at once. Pages can hold checklists, tables, images, attachments and diagrams drawn with Draw.io, Excalidraw or Mermaid, and every page keeps a history of earlier versions. A bakery, for example, could keep its opening checklist, food safety rules and wholesale order process in one handbook space and send each new starter a single link.

You decide who sees what by putting people in groups and giving each group access to the spaces it needs. Docmost works in any web browser and imports Notion exports, Markdown and HTML files. The free edition is open source; single sign-on, two-step sign-in, audit logs, templates and the Confluence importer are part of Docmost's paid licences.

Cloudgate installs Docmost and its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open that address creates the owner account and names the workspace, with no outside sign-in service to set up.
