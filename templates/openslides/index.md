OpenSlides is free, open-source meeting software for general assemblies, AGMs, conferences and councils, and a self-hosted alternative to ElectionBuddy for votes held at a meeting. One web app holds the agenda, the motions and their amendments, the list of speakers, the votes and elections, and the big-screen view the room sees.

The chair runs the meeting from a laptop while members follow along, ask to speak and vote from their own phones or computers, in the room or from home. Votes can be secret, open or by name, with proxies and vote weights, and the results go straight onto the screen and into a PDF. A sports club, for example, can collect motions from its members before the annual meeting, let them propose amendments line by line, elect a new board by secret ballot on the night, and send everyone the results as a PDF.

OpenSlides works in any modern browser and in seven languages, including English, German, French and Spanish. Cloudgate installs it on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, with everything it needs, and gives it a secure web address. Sign in with the superadmin account and set up your first meeting; your members' details and votes stay on your server.
