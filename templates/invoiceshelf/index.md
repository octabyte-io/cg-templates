InvoiceShelf is free, open-source, self-hosted invoicing software for freelancers and small businesses: estimates, invoices, payments and expenses in one simple web app, as an alternative to FreshBooks, Zoho Books or the invoicing side of QuickBooks. It shows what each customer still owes, and it started as a fork of the Crater invoicing app.

A small design studio, for example, could send a yoga studio an estimate for a new website, turn it into an invoice, and record the first half when it is paid. The yoga studio sees the invoice and what is left to pay in its customer portal, and the studio's monthly hosting fee goes out as a recurring invoice.

InvoiceShelf does invoicing and nothing more: it keeps no double-entry books and has no bank feeds or time tracking. For full accounting, Bigcapital is the closer fit. The version Cloudgate installs gets security fixes until 1 September 2027. There is no phone app, but it works in any web browser.

Cloudgate installs InvoiceShelf on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. Open it straight away and finish the setup wizard to create your admin account and company.
