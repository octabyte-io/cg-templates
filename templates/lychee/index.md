Lychee is a free, open-source, self-hosted photo gallery for organising your photos in albums and sharing them with clients, family or the public, in place of a SmugMug, Pixieset or Flickr subscription. Your photos stay in their original quality on your own server, with no storage plan.

You upload photos and videos by dragging them into the browser, sort them into albums and sub-albums, and choose a cover, a header photo and a layout for each. An album can be public, protected by a password, or shared only with chosen user accounts, and you decide whether visitors may download. A wedding photographer, for example, could put each couple's photos in a password-protected album and send them the link, so they can browse a clean gallery and download every picture in full resolution.

Lychee shows each photo's camera details, puts geotagged photos on a map, and lets you tag many photos at once and rate them. It works in any web browser; there is no phone app yet. Watermarks, user groups and upload quotas come with the paid Supporter Edition.

Cloudgate installs Lychee and its database on your server and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open that address creates the admin account.
