faq:
  - q: Is Instatic free?
    a: Yes, Instatic is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no paid tier or locked features. You pay only for your server, plus your AI provider if you use the AI assistant with a paid service.
  - q: Is Instatic a good alternative to Webflow?
    a: Yes, if you want Webflow-style visual design, a content system and forms on your own server with no plan fees. Instatic publishes plain HTML you can host anywhere, but it is much younger than Webflow, with no shop, no visitor analytics and far fewer templates.
  - q: Is Instatic ready for a business website?
    a: Partly. Instatic is at version 0.0.x and its makers say features and workflows can still change before version 1.0, so it suits a simple site you are willing to keep up to date more than a site you never want to touch.
  - q: How do I sign in to Instatic the first time?
    a: Open your Instatic web address and a setup screen asks you to name your site and create the owner account. There is no default password; that first account is the admin.
  - q: Can I move my Webflow site to Instatic?
    a: Partly. Instatic can import a static site's HTML, CSS, images and fonts, such as Webflow's code export, and turns them into editable pages and styles. Wix and Squarespace have no such export, so those pages are rebuilt by hand.
  - q: Does the AI assistant cost extra?
    a: Only if your AI provider charges. Instatic uses your own key for Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter, or a free model you run yourself with Ollama, and the rest of Instatic works without any AI at all.
  - q: Does Instatic show visitor statistics?
    a: No, not yet. Instatic's dashboard shows your pages, media, storage and an activity log, and its makers list privacy-friendly visitor analytics as a next step on their roadmap.

Instatic is a free, open-source website builder and CMS you can self-host as an alternative to Webflow, Wix or Squarespace: you design pages on a visual canvas, manage posts and collections, and publish the site as plain HTML and CSS files. There is no monthly plan or fee per site, and the finished pages are standard files you can read, copy and host anywhere.

It suits designers, freelancers and small businesses who want precise control over layout, colours and type, a content system for posts and listings, and forms whose replies land in their own tables. A café could, for example, design its home page once, keep its menu in a simple table that fills a menu page, post news on its blog, and collect table booking requests through its own form.

Instatic is made by the team behind Core Framework and Motion.page. It is very new, at version 0.0.x, so features and workflows can still change between updates. The optional AI assistant works with Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter or a model you run yourself with Ollama.

Cloudgate installs Instatic on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives your editor and published site a secure web address.
