listmonk sends newsletters and email campaigns to your subscribers. You keep your contact lists in it, write emails in a visual editor, and send them now or on a schedule. Each campaign shows how many people opened it and clicked its links.

It suits businesses with large lists that send regularly and are tired of prices that rise with every new subscriber. A shop with 40,000 customers, for example, can send a monthly offer to all of them, or only to those who bought in the last year, for the cost of the emails alone.

Cloudgate installs listmonk on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. listmonk sends mail through an email sending service you choose, such as Amazon SES, Postmark, Mailgun or your own mail server, and you can add a sign-up form to your website so new subscribers join a list on their own.
