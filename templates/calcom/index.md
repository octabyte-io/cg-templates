Cal.com is an open-source scheduling tool that lets clients book meetings with you from a link, without the usual back-and-forth of emails. You set when you are available and what kinds of meetings you offer, and clients choose a free slot that suits them. The booking goes straight into your calendar.

It checks your existing calendars so a slot is only offered when you are really free, and it can add a Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams link to each booking. For teams, bookings can go to whoever is free or rotate between people. A physiotherapy practice, for example, could let patients book a first assessment online with any of its three therapists, and ask for their symptoms on the booking form.

Cal.com works with Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar, and booking pages work on any phone or computer. You can embed the booking page in your own website and send booking details to other tools through webhooks.

Cloudgate installs Cal.com with its database and cache on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open that address creates the admin account, then sets up their availability and meeting types and invites the team. Calendar and video connections are switched on once in the admin settings.
