Cal.com lets clients book time with you from a link, without the usual back-and-forth of emails. You set when you are available and what kinds of meetings you offer, and clients choose a free slot that suits them. The booking goes straight into your calendar.

It checks your existing calendars so a slot is only offered when you are really free, and it can add a Zoom, Google Meet or Teams link to each booking. For teams, bookings can go to whoever is free or rotate between people. A physiotherapy practice, for example, could let patients book a first assessment online with any of its three therapists.

Cloudgate installs Cal.com on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign in becomes the admin. Cal.com works with Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar, and you can embed the booking page in your own website.
