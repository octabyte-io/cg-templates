Memos is a free, open-source, self-hosted note-taking app for quick notes, and an alternative to Google Keep. You write short notes, ideas, links and checklists in Markdown, and each one lands on a private timeline you can search, filter by tag and look back on by day in a calendar.

Each memo can be private, shared with a Space of family or colleagues, or published on the web with its own link. You can attach photos and files, pin the notes you need often, and link related memos together. A freelance photographer, for example, could jot down shot ideas, client requests and kit checklists on a phone during the day, tag each with the client's name, and find them all that evening.

Memos works in any browser, on a computer or a phone; it has no official phone app. A web clipper for Chrome and Firefox and a Telegram bot let you save things to it in one step. Several people can have accounts on one Memos, each with their own notes.

Cloudgate installs Memos on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. It creates the administrator account for you: sign in as admin with the password Cloudgate generates. New sign-ups start turned off, so the admin adds anyone else.
