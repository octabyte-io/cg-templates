Horilla is free, open-source HR software (an HRMS) that you self-host to run hiring, attendance, leave, payroll and performance reviews in one place, much like BambooHR, Zoho People or Personio but with no fee per employee. Every module is included in the free version, and Cloudgate installs it on your own server.

Each employee has one record with their documents, shift and manager. Candidates move through your hiring stages and into onboarding, staff check in and request leave, and at the end of the month payroll turns contracts, allowances and deductions into payslips. Performance objectives, company assets, an HR help desk and exit checklists are part of it too. A chain of four cafés with forty staff, for example, could track who is on which shift at each site, approve holiday requests from one screen and generate everyone's payslips for the accountant.

Horilla has a free mobile app for Android and iPhone, connects to your company directory (LDAP) and imports employees from a spreadsheet. Its many modules mean some setup before your staff use it.

Cloudgate sets up Horilla with its database and a secure web address. On first visit you click Initialize Database, enter the password Cloudgate created and set up your admin account.
