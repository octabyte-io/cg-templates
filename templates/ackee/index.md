Ackee is a free, self-hosted website analytics tool and a simple alternative to Google Analytics and Fathom: it shows how many people visit your websites, which pages they read, where they came from and how long they stay, on one minimal dashboard. It sets no cookies and keeps visitors anonymous, so most sites can use it without a cookie banner.

It suits people who want a quick look at their numbers rather than a marketing suite. A bakery, for example, could see in a few seconds that its menu page is the most visited this week and that most new visitors arrive from Instagram, then track how many people click the "Order a cake" button.

Ackee works with any website: you paste one script line into the site, the same way you would add Google Analytics, and community plugins exist for WordPress and popular website frameworks. There is one login and no phone app, but the dashboard works in a phone's browser.
