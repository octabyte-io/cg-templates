Self-hosted Eigenfocus is a free project board with a time tracker built in, a simple alternative to Trello for one person who wants to plan tasks and record the hours they take in one app. Each project is a board of tasks that you drag from column to column, with notes, checklists, files, labels, due dates and comments.

You start a timer from the task you are working on, see your week on a timesheet, and download time reports by project or date as a spreadsheet file. A freelance baker, for example, could keep one board for a new seasonal menu and another for catering orders, then pull up exactly how long the recipe testing took.

The free edition is for one person: it has no user accounts, and it cannot import boards from Trello. Several users, roles, timeline views and custom fields come with the paid Essential and PRO editions. Eigenfocus is source-available, not open source, and runs in your web browser, including on a phone, with no separate apps.

Cloudgate installs Eigenfocus on your server, protects it with a password and gives it a secure web address. Your projects, files and hours stay on your server.
