solidtime is a free, open-source, self-hosted time tracker that records the hours you and your team spend on each client and project, works out what they're worth at your rates, and reports where the time went. It covers the time tracking people use Toggl Track, Clockify or Harvest for, on your own server with no per-user fee.

You start a timer when you begin a task, drag entries onto a week calendar, or fill in a weekly timesheet, and book the time to a client, project and task. solidtime adds up billable amounts at the rate you set for each person or project, and its reports can be saved, shared or exported. A small design studio, for example, could track every job for a local bakery and a gym, then export last month's billable hours per client to bill them.

There are free desktop apps and browser extensions, but no phone app; the web app works in a phone's browser. Built-in importers bring your Toggl, Clockify or Harvest history across. Invoicing needs solidtime's paid Business licence.

Cloudgate installs solidtime on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, together with its database, and gives it a secure web address. Sign in with your admin email and the password Cloudgate created, add your clients and projects, and start the timer.
