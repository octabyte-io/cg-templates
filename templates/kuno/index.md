Kuno is a free, open-source, self-hosted multilingual blog, and a lighter alternative to Ghost(Pro) or Medium for anyone who writes in more than one language. Each post, category and the site title keep their translations side by side, readers switch language from the menu, and every language gets its own web address that search engines can find. The reading and admin screens come in more than 70 languages.

A bakery in Montreal, for example, could post its winter opening hours and a sourdough recipe in English, use the built-in translation helper to draft the French version, correct it, and schedule both to go live on Monday morning.

You write in Markdown with a live preview, add pictures and videos from a media library, and see views, countries and devices in built-in stats. Kuno also offers an RSS feed and per-post search settings. Optional AI tools for summaries and search suggestions work with your own OpenAI, Gemini or Claude account. Kuno is a blog only: it has no newsletters, paid memberships or comments.

Cloudgate installs Kuno on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. You finish setup in your browser, and your posts stay on your server.
