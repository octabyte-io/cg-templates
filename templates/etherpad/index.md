Etherpad is a free, open-source online editor where your team writes the same document at the same time and sees every change live, a self-hosted alternative to Google Docs or Dropbox Paper for shared writing. Each person's words are highlighted in their own colour, and every change is kept, so the timeslider can replay how a document came together.

There are no accounts for writers. You open a pad, share its link, and people pick a display name and start typing; a chat panel sits beside the text for quick questions. A bakery, for example, could open a pad for each weekly team meeting, let everyone add their updates at once, and download the finished notes as a Word file or PDF.

The editor is deliberately simple: bold, italic, lists and indents out of the box, with plugins for headings, comments, tables and more, installed from the admin page. It works in any web browser, including on phones, but has no app.

Cloudgate installs Etherpad on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, together with its database, and gives it a secure web address. Anyone with that address can create pads, so share it with your team only, and sign in at the admin page with the password Cloudgate shows you.
