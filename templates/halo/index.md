faq:
  - q: Is Halo free?
    a: Yes, the Halo community edition is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, and self-hosting it costs only your server. The Halo team also sells paid Pro and Commerce editions with extras such as a phone app, automatic cloud backups, an online shop and paid themes and plugins; Cloudgate installs the free community edition.
  - q: Is Halo a good alternative to Squarespace or Wix?
    a: Yes, for a blog or a simple company website you want to own with no monthly plan. Halo has themes, plugins and comments built in, but no drag-and-drop page builder, fewer themes than Squarespace or Wix, and you keep it updated yourself.
  - q: Should I choose Halo or WordPress?
    a: Choose WordPress if you want the widest choice of English-language themes and plugins, a visual page builder or an online shop. Choose Halo if you mainly run a blog or small site and like its clean admin console, built-in comments and one-click backups.
  - q: Does Halo work in English?
    a: Yes, the admin console and the Setup page are available in English, as well as Chinese and Spanish. Many themes and plugins in Halo's app store, the official documentation and the community forum are mainly in Chinese, and a new site starts with a sample menu and About page in Chinese that you rename.
  - q: Can I move my WordPress or Ghost blog to Halo?
    a: Yes. Halo's free migration plugin imports posts, pages, categories, tags and comments from a WordPress or Ghost export, and also reads RSS feeds and Markdown files. Your theme does not come across, so choose a Halo theme and check each page after the import.
  - q: How do I sign in to Halo the first time?
    a: Open your site's web address and Halo shows a Setup page where you choose the language and site title and create the admin account with a username, email and password. There is no default login, so do this straight after setup; the admin console is then at /console.
  - q: What server size do I need for Halo?
    a: A small server with 1 GB of memory runs a typical Halo blog or company website. Choose a larger one if you expect a lot of visitors or install many plugins.

Halo is a free, open-source website and blog builder that you self-host as an alternative to Squarespace, Wix or Medium. You write posts and pages in a clean editor, choose a theme for the design, and add features with plugins from Halo's app store, all from one admin console.

It suits blogs, personal sites, portfolios and small company websites. A bakery, for example, could publish its opening hours, share recipes sorted into categories, announce baking classes and let customers leave comments, with no monthly fee to a website builder. Several people can write, each with their own account and role.

Comments, a media library and one-click backups are built in, and a free migration plugin imports posts from WordPress or Ghost. The admin console is in English; many themes and plugins, and Halo's own documentation, are written mainly in Chinese. The Halo team also sells paid editions with a phone app, automatic cloud backups and an online shop; the free community edition covers the website and blog.

Cloudgate installs Halo and its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open that address names the site and creates the admin account. You can later point your own domain name at it.
