faq:

    a: Yes, Dolibarr is free and open source under the GPL-3.0-or-later licence, and every one of its core modules is included. You pay only for your server; the paid extras are third-party add-ons from the DoliStore marketplace and hosted plans.

    a: Yes, if you want more than bookkeeping. Dolibarr goes wider than QuickBooks, with a CRM, quotes, orders, invoices, stock, purchasing and projects in one app. Its bookkeeping is simpler, though, with no automatic bank feeds, no payroll and no tax filing.

Dolibarr is already installed, with your company name and country set; first set your main currency under Home > Setup > Company/Organization, because it stays on euros otherwise.
  - q: Which parts of Dolibarr are switched on?
    a: In Dolibarr, third parties (customers and suppliers), products, stock, quotes, orders, invoices, banks and scheduled jobs are on from the start. You can switch on projects, accounting, HR, point of sale and the other modules at any time under Setup > Modules/Applications.
  - q: Can I import my data from QuickBooks or another tool?
    a: Partly. Dolibarr has no QuickBooks importer, but its import module reads CSV and Excel files, so you can export your customers, suppliers and products from your old software and import them.
  - q: Is there a Dolibarr phone app?

  - q: What server size do I need for Dolibarr?
    a: A small server is enough for most small businesses, because Dolibarr runs on modest hardware and its database grows by only about 1 MB for every 100 customers, suppliers or invoices. You can move to a bigger server later.

Dolibarr ERP & CRM is free, open-source, self-hosted business software that keeps your customers, quotes, orders, invoices, products and stock in one app, as an alternative to QuickBooks, Zoho Books, Xero or FreshBooks. It suits small businesses, freelancers and associations, and it is built from modules: you switch on only what you use.

A garden supply shop, for example, could send a community garden a quote it signs online, turn it into an order and an invoice, and watch its stock of pots and potting soil go down as it delivers. When the payment arrives, it is recorded against the invoice and the bank account. Later the shop could add purchasing, projects, point of sale or accounting without changing software.

Dolibarr covers more of the business than accounting apps such as Bigcapital, with a CRM, projects and point of sale, but its bookkeeping is simpler, with no bank feeds or payroll. The interface is dense and has many settings. It works in any web browser, and there is an Android app.

Cloudgate installs Dolibarr on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, with your company name and country, and gives it a secure web address. Sign in as admin, set your main currency first, and start adding customers.
