ClassicPress is a free, open-source content management system, forked from WordPress, that keeps the classic editor and leaves out the block editor. Self-hosted ClassicPress gives you the familiar WordPress admin for a business website or blog, with no monthly fee to Wix or Squarespace. You pick a theme, then write pages and posts in a word-processor style editor.

Most WordPress themes and plugins made for WordPress 6.2 or earlier work, as long as they do not need blocks, so contact forms, search engine tools and galleries are a plugin away. ClassicPress adds its own touches: categories and tags for your images, a view of where each file is used, and tools to clear out old post revisions. A bakery, for example, could run its menu, opening hours and a weekly news post on ClassicPress, with the owner and one editor each signing in to their own account.

If you already run WordPress, you can bring your posts across with the WordPress importer or switch the site over with the project's migration plugin. ClassicPress is run by volunteers, so its community is smaller than WordPress's.
