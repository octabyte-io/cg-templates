Nextcloud is a private cloud for your business: a place to store, sync and share files, with calendars, contacts and many other apps alongside. It works like Dropbox or Google Drive. Files you save in a folder on your computer appear on your phone and in the browser, and you can share them with a link.

You can add extra apps from its built-in app store, such as an office suite for editing documents in the browser, shared task lists, forms and video calls. A small accounting firm, for example, could keep each client's documents in a shared folder and send clients a password-protected link to upload their receipts.

Cloudgate installs Nextcloud on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open it creates the admin account. Nextcloud has desktop apps for Windows, Mac and Linux, phone apps for iPhone and Android, and syncs calendars and contacts with Outlook, Thunderbird and your phone.
