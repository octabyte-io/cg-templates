Umami counts the people who visit your website and shows where they came from, which pages they read and what devices they use. Everything fits on one clear dashboard, and you can switch between several websites from the same account.

It uses no cookies and does not follow visitors across other websites, so most sites can use it without a cookie banner. Agencies like it because they can track every client's website in one place and send each client a read-only link to their own stats. A freelancer, for example, could check which blog posts brought in enquiries this month in under a minute.

Cloudgate installs Umami on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. Sign in with the default admin account and change its password straight away, then add your first website. Umami works with any website, including WordPress, Shopify, Wix and Ghost.
