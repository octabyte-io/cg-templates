Umami is an open-source, privacy-focused web analytics tool that counts the people who visit your website and shows where they came from, which pages they read and what devices they use. Everything fits on one clear dashboard, and one Umami can track as many websites as you like.

It uses no cookies and does not follow visitors across other websites, so most sites can use it without a cookie banner. Agencies like it because they can track every client's website in one place and send each client a read-only link to their own stats. A freelance designer, for example, can check in under a minute which blog posts brought in enquiries this month, and share that page with the client instead of a monthly PDF.

Umami works with any website: you paste one line into the site's header, the same way you would add Google Analytics, including on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and Ghost. There is no phone app, but the dashboard works in a phone's browser.

Cloudgate installs Umami on a server you connect, together with the database it needs, and gives it a secure web address. Sign in with the default admin account, change its password straight away, then add your first website and copy its tracking line. Your statistics stay on your server.
