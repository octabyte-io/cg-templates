faq:
  - q: Is PrestaShop free?
    a: Yes, PrestaShop is free, open-source software (OSL-3.0 for the core, AFL-3.0 for its modules). You still pay for hosting and payment provider fees, many modules and themes on the PrestaShop Addons marketplace are paid, and the back office promotes PrestaShop's own paid services.
  - q: Is PrestaShop a good alternative to Shopify?
    a: Yes, if you want to own your shop rather than rent it. PrestaShop has themes, modules, payments and multi-store selling with no monthly platform fee, but you run the updates, backups and security yourself, and it has fewer ready-made apps than Shopify.
  - q: How do I sign in to the PrestaShop back office?
    a: Open your PrestaShop shop's web address followed by its back office folder, a random name chosen for security instead of /admin, and sign in with the email you entered during setup. Cloudgate lists the folder name and the generated password among the app's variables.
  - q: What do I need to do before my PrestaShop shop goes live?
    a: Three things. Delete the demo catalogue PrestaShop installs, with its sample t-shirts, mugs, posters and demo customer; add a payment module for the payment methods you want; and add your email sending details so customers get their order emails.
  - q: Can I move my products from Shopify to PrestaShop?
    a: Partly. PrestaShop imports products, categories, customers and addresses from CSV files, so you can export them from Shopify and import them. Moving everything, including past orders, needs a paid third-party migration tool.
  - q: Can PrestaShop sell in several languages and currencies?
    a: Yes, PrestaShop is translated into many languages and localized for many countries. One back office can run several stores, each with its own languages, currencies, VAT, payment methods and products.
  - q: What server size do I need for PrestaShop?
    a: A medium server, so Cloudgate asks for one. PrestaShop needs at least 512 MB of memory for PHP alone, and a shop with a large catalogue or many visitors runs better with more.

Self-hosted PrestaShop is a free, open-source e-commerce platform for running your own online shop, and an alternative to Shopify, BigCommerce and Wix eCommerce. You get a shop front with themes, a cart and checkout, and a back office for products, orders, customers, payments, shipping and taxes, with no platform fee on your sales.

It suits small and mid-sized shops, especially in Europe, that want to own their store and customer data. A clothing boutique, for example, could list its t-shirts in every size and colour, sell to France and Germany in two languages with the right VAT for each, and follow every order from payment to delivery slip.

PrestaShop grows with modules for payments, shipping, marketing and search engines. Some come with it; many more are on the PrestaShop Addons marketplace, where a lot of them are paid. Products, categories and customers can be imported from spreadsheets, and the shop and back office both work in a phone's browser.

Cloudgate installs PrestaShop and its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, creates your admin account and gives the shop a secure web address. Before you open, delete the demo catalogue, add a payment module and set up email sending so customers receive their order emails.
