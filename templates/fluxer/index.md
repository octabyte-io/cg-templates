Self-hosted Fluxer is free, open-source community chat that works like Discord: you create a community, split it into channels, give members roles and talk in direct and group messages, all on your own server. You can give everyone the extras Fluxer charges for on its own service, such as bigger uploads and animated avatars, at no cost.

A gaming group moving off Discord could import its Discord server template to recreate its channels, roles and permissions in one step, then share an invite link so members join again. Moderators can time out or ban people and check the audit log, and each community can have up to 500 custom emoji and 500 stickers.

Members use Fluxer in a web browser or in the official Windows, Mac, Linux and Android apps, which can connect to your server. This template is text chat only, without voice channels, video calls or screen sharing. Cloudgate installs Fluxer on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. This is the open-source Fluxer software running on your own server; it is not run by, or associated with, Fluxer Platform AB or fluxer.com.
