Vaultwarden is a password manager for you and your team. It keeps your passwords, passkeys, card details and secure notes in an encrypted vault, and fills in logins for you on websites and in apps. Nobody needs to remember dozens of passwords or keep them in a spreadsheet.

It works with the free, well-known Bitwarden apps for phones, computers and every major browser. You simply point the app at your own web address. Shared logins, such as the company's social media accounts or supplier portals, go into collections that you share with the right people. When someone leaves, you remove their access in one place.

Cloudgate installs Vaultwarden on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address, which the Bitwarden apps need. During setup you choose whether anyone can create an account. Vaultwarden imports passwords from 1Password, LastPass, Chrome and most other password managers.
