Invoice Ninja is self-hosted, source-available invoicing software for freelancers and small businesses: quotes, invoices, online payments, recurring billing, time tracking and a client portal, as an alternative to FreshBooks, Wave or Zoho Invoice. The self-hosted edition includes every Pro and Enterprise feature of the hosted plans.

A small design studio, for example, could send a cafe a quote for a new website, turn it into an invoice when the cafe approves it, and take a deposit by card from the invoice. The hours spent on the project go onto the final invoice from the timer, and the monthly hosting fee goes out as a recurring invoice charged to the cafe's card.

Invoice Ninja does invoicing, not accounting: it has no double-entry books, payroll or tax returns. Your clients see Invoice Ninja branding on their portal, PDFs and emails unless you buy a white-label licence. It has phone apps for iPhone and Android and desktop apps that connect to your server, and it imports from FreshBooks, Wave and Zoho Invoice.

Cloudgate installs Invoice Ninja on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. Sign in with the email you gave at setup and the password on the app's variables page, then add an email provider in the settings so your invoices reach clients.
