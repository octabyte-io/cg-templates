Uptime Kuma is an open-source uptime monitoring tool that checks your websites and online services around the clock and tells you the moment one stops working, so you hear about it before your customers do. You add a website by pasting its address and choosing how often to check it, as often as every 20 seconds.

When something goes wrong, alerts can go to email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Telegram, Discord, SMS and more than 90 other services. You can also publish a status page that shows customers which services are working and which are being fixed. An online shop, for example, can watch its storefront, payment page and booking system, get a phone alert within a minute if checkout breaks at night, and point customers to its status page while it is fixed.

Beyond websites, Uptime Kuma can check that a server answers, that a domain name still points to the right place, or that a page still shows a given word. It also warns you before a website's security certificate expires. There is no official phone app; the dashboard works in any browser.
