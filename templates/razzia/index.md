Razzia, formerly called Rahoot, is a free, open-source live quiz game you host yourself: a self-hosted alternative to Kahoot! for classrooms, training sessions, team meetings and quiz nights. The host shows each question on a big screen, players answer on their phones against the clock, and a leaderboard and podium crown the winner.

Players join from any browser with a six-digit game PIN or a QR code and a nickname, with no app or account. You write quizzes in the built-in editor, with single-answer, multiple-answer and number-guessing questions, pictures, video or audio, and points for each question. Group questions into rounds, or switch a quiz to survey mode to collect opinions with no scores. A trainer, for example, can open a workshop with a ten-question quiz about the company's products, keep the leaderboard for the end of each round, and look at the saved results afterwards to see which questions most people got wrong.

Razzia works in nine languages and takes your own name, colours and logo. It is made for live games run by a host, not homework or self-paced tests. Cloudgate installs Razzia on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, gives it a secure web address, and sets the manager password you sign in with.
