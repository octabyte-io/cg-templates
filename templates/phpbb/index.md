faq:
  - q: Is phpBB free?
    a: Yes, phpBB is free and open source under the GPL-2.0 licence, and there is no paid edition. You pay only for the server it runs on, with no fee per member or per post.
  - q: Is phpBB a good alternative to XenForo or vBulletin?
    a: Yes, if you want a classic forum with categories, topics, private messages, polls and detailed permissions without paying for a licence. phpBB looks more traditional than XenForo and has fewer built-in extras, but extensions add many of them.
  - q: Should I choose phpBB or NodeBB?
    a: Choose phpBB for a classic, well-proven bulletin board with a large library of extensions and styles. Choose NodeBB if you want a more modern forum where replies appear live and members can chat.

    a: No, phpBB has no official phone app. Its standard look adjusts to small screens, so members can read and post from any phone browser.

phpBB is free, open-source forum software that you can run self-hosted on your own domain, as an alternative to paid forum software such as XenForo or vBulletin, or to a Facebook group. It gives you a classic bulletin board: categories of forums, topics with replies, private messages, polls and file attachments, with detailed permissions and moderation tools.

An allotment society, for example, could keep committee news in a read-only announcements forum, let plot holders ask growing questions and swap seeds in their own forums, and run a poll to pick the date of the next work day. A committee-only forum can stay hidden from everyone else.

phpBB is built and supported by volunteers, and a large library of free extensions, styles and language packs lets you add features and change the look from the admin panel. There is no phone app, but the standard look works in any phone browser. Add your email settings so members can reset their passwords and get notified of replies.
