HortusFox is a free, open-source, self-hosted plant manager for house plants and gardens that a whole household can share, in place of plant-care apps such as Planta or Greg. It keeps your plants, their care, your tasks and your supplies in one place on your own server.

Each plant belongs to a location, such as the garden, the greenhouse or the living room. You note when it was watered, repotted or fertilised, how it is doing, and add photos and notes. Tasks with due dates, a shared calendar and an inventory of soil, fertiliser and tools sit alongside, and the dashboard shows which plants need attention and which tasks are overdue. A couple with a balcony garden, for example, could each mark plants as watered from their phones, see that the basil has pests, and check whether there is still potting soil before buying more.

HortusFox runs in any browser on a computer or phone and comes in twelve languages. It can identify plants from a photo with Pl@ntNet and show the weather, each with a free key from that service. It doesn't build a care schedule for you; you set your own tasks.

Cloudgate installs HortusFox on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. Sign in with your admin email and the password Cloudgate created.
