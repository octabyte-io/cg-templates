faq:
  - q: Is self-hosted Habitica free?
    a: Yes, the code is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, and you pay only for your server. The community build gives every account the subscription perks, lets you buy gems with gold instead of money, and lets you create Group Plans without paying.
  - q: Is this the official Habitica?
    a: No, Habitica's makers do not publish a self-hosted version. Cloudgate installs a community build of Habitica's own code, made by independent volunteers, so it is your own private copy and is not supported by Habitica.
  - q: Can I use my habitica.com account on my own Habitica?
    a: No, your own Habitica is separate from habitica.com, so its accounts, parties and challenges are not there and nothing moves across. You can export your task history from habitica.com for your records, then start fresh.
  - q: How do I sign in to Habitica the first time?
    a: Open your Habitica's address and sign up with a username, email and password; the first account becomes the admin. Once your family or group has joined, switch on invite-only sign-up in Cloudgate so only people you invite can create an account.
  - q: Can a family use Habitica for chores?
    a: Yes, a Group Plan gives the household one shared task board where you assign chores to one person, several people, or anyone. Everyone earns their own gold and experience for the chores they finish.
  - q: Does Habitica have phone apps that work with my own server?
    a: Not that we can confirm. Habitica's Android and iPhone apps are built for habitica.com, so use your Habitica's website in your phone's browser instead.
  - q: Do I need an email server for Habitica?
    a: No, Habitica works without one, but password resets and invitations by email are only sent if you enter your email provider's details. You can still invite people already on your server by their username.

Self-hosted Habitica is your own private copy of Habitica, the free, open-source habit tracker and to-do list that works like a role-playing game. Finishing your habits, daily tasks and to-dos earns your pixel-art avatar experience and gold, and skipping them costs it health, so keeping a routine starts to feel like playing.

It runs on a community build of Habitica's code, made by independent volunteers rather than Habitica's makers. Your copy has no link to habitica.com accounts, parties or paid gem purchases. Instead, every account gets the subscription perks, gems are bought with gold, and Group Plans are free. A family, for example, could set up a shared board where "feed the cat" belongs to one child and "take out the bins" goes to whoever does it first, with everyone saving gold for a reward the family chose, like picking Friday's takeaway.

Friends can form a party, chat and beat quest monsters by doing their own tasks. Habitica runs in any web browser, including on a phone, and you can export your data at any time.

Cloudgate installs Habitica on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, with the database it needs, and gives it a secure web address. Sign up first to become the admin, then invite your family or friends.
