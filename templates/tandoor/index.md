Tandoor Recipes is a self-hosted recipe manager and meal planner for people with large recipe collections, and a no-fee alternative to paid apps such as Paprika or Plan to Eat. It keeps your recipes, meal plan and shopping list in one shared space for family or friends, with more detail than most recipe apps: each step lists its own ingredients, and Tandoor can total nutrition or cost from what goes in.

Save recipes from websites by their address and sort them with tags and cookbooks. A family that cooks from a few hundred recipes, for example, could import its old Paprika collection, plan the week's dinners on the calendar, and send the ingredients to one list sorted by their supermarket that everyone ticks off live.

Tandoor runs in any browser and can be added to a phone's home screen; there is no app in the app stores. It imports from Paprika, Plan to Eat, Mealie and many other apps, and can use an AI provider you connect to read recipes from photos or PDFs. It is source-available and free to use on your own server.

Cloudgate installs Tandoor on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. Open that address right after setup to create your admin account, then invite your household.
