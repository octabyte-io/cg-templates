Silex is a free, open-source visual website builder you can self-host as an alternative to Webflow or Wix: you design pages by dragging blocks onto a canvas, and Silex publishes them as plain HTML and CSS. There is no monthly plan, no fee per site and no lock-in, because the published site is standard files that any web host can serve.

Silex suits designers, freelancers and small studios who want precise control over layout, fonts and colours without writing code. It does ask you to learn a few web design ideas, so a complete beginner may find Wix quicker. A freelance designer could, for example, build a bakery's five-page site with its menu, opening hours and a cake order form, check how it looks on a phone, and publish it in one click.

Silex is made by Silex Labs, a non-profit. Forms send their messages to a form service you choose, and there is no online shop checkout. You design on a computer in Chrome or Firefox.

Cloudgate installs Silex on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, puts a password in front of the editor, and gives the editor and your published website their own secure web addresses.
