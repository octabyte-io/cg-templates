  - q: Is Joomla free?
    a: Yes, Joomla is free and open source under the GNU GPL licence (version 2 or later), and there is no paid edition. Self-hosting it costs only your server; some third-party templates and extensions are paid.
  - q: Is Joomla a good alternative to Wix or Squarespace?
    a: Yes, if you want a site you fully control, with no monthly plan, several languages and detailed user permissions built in. Joomla has no drag-and-drop page builder of its own and takes longer to learn than Wix or Squarespace, and you keep it updated yourself.
  - q: Should I choose Joomla or WordPress?
    a: Choose Joomla if you need a site in several languages or detailed permissions for many editors and members, because both are built in. Self-hosted Joomla is a free, open-source content management system (CMS) for building company, club and community websites, and a self-hosted alternative to Wix and Squarespace. Editors write articles in a visual editor and sort them into categories, while templates set the look and menus decide what visitors see.

Joomla stands out for what is built in: sites in more than 70 languages, and detailed permissions that decide who can write, edit, publish or only read each part of the site. A hiking club with members on both sides of a border, for example, could publish its walks in English and German, keep route maps in a members-only area, and let walk leaders post news without being able to change the menus or design.

Thousands of extensions, most of them free, add features the core does not have. There is no official phone or desktop app; you work in the web browser.

Cloudgate installs Joomla and its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. It asks for your site name and admin email, then Joomla sets itself up: sign in at /administrator as admin with the password shown in Cloudgate, and a guided tour shows you around. You can later point your own domain name at it.
