faq:
  - q: Is Leantime free?
    a: Yes, the self-hosted version of Leantime is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence, and you pay only for your server. Some extras, such as custom fields, recurring tasks and whiteboards, are paid plugins, and the AI features are only in the paid Leantime cloud.
  - q: Is Leantime a good alternative to Asana or ClickUp?
    a: Yes, for small teams who want to-do boards, milestones, goals and time tracking in one simple app. Leantime has fewer automations and integrations than Asana or ClickUp, and custom fields and recurring tasks cost extra as plugins.
  - q: How do I sign in to Leantime the first time?
    a: There is no default login. Open your Leantime web address right after setup and fill in the install page with your name, email, password and company; that creates the admin account. Whoever opens the address first becomes the admin, so do it straight away.
  - q: Can I move my projects from Trello or Asana to Leantime?
    a: Partly. Leantime imports CSV files of to-dos, milestones, projects, users, ideas and goals, so you can export from your old tool and bring the work across. There is no one-click importer for Trello, Asana or other tools.
  - q: Is Leantime good for people with ADHD?
    a: Leantime is built with ADHD, dyslexia and autism in mind, with a simple layout and a My Work page that shows what is due next. It is aimed at people who are not trained project managers.
  - q: Does Leantime have a phone app?
    a: Yes, but the Leantime phone app is in beta, and connecting it to your own server needs a paid add-on. The web app works in a phone's browser without it.
  - q: What server size do I need for Leantime?
    a: A small server with 1 GB of memory is enough for Leantime and its database, because the app is light. You can move to a bigger server later as your team grows.

Leantime is open-source project management for people who aren't project managers: self-hosted Leantime puts to-do boards, milestone timelines, goals and time tracking in one simple app on your own server, in place of tools like Asana, monday.com, ClickUp or Trello. It is built with ADHD, dyslexia and autism in mind, so the screens stay calm and each person starts from a My Work page that shows what is due next.

To-dos can be viewed as a kanban board, a table, a list or a calendar, and grouped under milestones on a timeline. Goals track a number towards a target, timesheets record the hours spent, and canvases such as a Lean Canvas or SWOT help with planning. A small marketing agency, for example, could run each client campaign as a project, track the launch dates as milestones and log billable hours against each to-do.

Leantime sends notifications to Slack, Mattermost and Discord, exports calendars to other apps and imports work from CSV files. The phone app is in beta; the web app works in a phone's browser. Custom fields, recurring tasks, whiteboards and AI are paid extras.

Cloudgate installs Leantime on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, together with its database, and gives it a secure web address. Open that address right away to create your admin account.
