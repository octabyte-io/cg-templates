SplitPro is a free, open-source, self-hosted alternative to Splitwise for sharing expenses with friends, housemates, partners and travel groups. You add what you paid, split it the way you agreed, and SplitPro keeps a running balance of who owes whom, with no daily limit on how many expenses you add.

Each person has their own account, so balances follow friends across every group, just like Splitwise. Expenses can carry a receipt photo, a category and their own currency, and rent or bills can repeat on their own. Four housemates could keep one group for the house: whoever pays the electricity bill adds it from their phone, everyone sees their share straight away, and at the end of the month each person settles up with one payment.

SplitPro runs in any browser and can be added to a phone's home screen. You can bring over your friends and groups, with their balances, from Splitwise. Everyone signs in with a link sent by email, so you connect an email sending service when you install it.

Cloudgate installs SplitPro on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, sets up its database, and gives it a secure web address. Your shared expenses stay on your server.
