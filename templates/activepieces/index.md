Activepieces connects the online tools your business uses and does the repetitive work between them for you. You build a flow by choosing what starts it, such as a new form entry or a new email, and then adding the steps that should follow.

Each step is filled in from menus, so if you have used Zapier you will feel at home straight away. A small agency, for example, can have every new enquiry from its website added to a Google Sheet, posted in the team's Slack channel and answered with a friendly email, all within seconds and without anyone touching it.

Cloudgate installs Activepieces on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign in becomes the admin. Activepieces has ready-made steps for hundreds of services, including Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack, HubSpot, Notion and Stripe, and there is no limit on how many flows you run.
