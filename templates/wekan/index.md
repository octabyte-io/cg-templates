faq:
  - q: Is WeKan free?
    a: Yes, WeKan is free and open source under the MIT licence. A self-hosted WeKan has every feature, including the Admin Panel, with no limit on boards or members; the WeKan team charges only for its hosted service and for commercial support.
  - q: Is WeKan a good alternative to Trello?
    a: Yes, if your team wants Trello-style boards with more built in. WeKan has lists, cards, labels, checklists and due dates like Trello, plus swimlanes, calendar and Gantt views and automation rules, but its screen is busier and it has no phone apps or Power-Ups.
  - q: Can I import my boards from Trello into WeKan?
    a: Yes, WeKan imports Trello boards with their lists, cards, labels, checklists and comments. You can upload a board's JSON export from Trello, or connect a Trello API key and token to pick boards and bring their attachments too.
  - q: How do I sign in to WeKan the first time?
    a: Open WeKan's web address, choose Register and create an account with a username, email and password; the first account becomes the admin. Then untick Self-Registration under People in the Admin Panel, so only people you invite can join.
  - q: Does WeKan need an email server?
    a: No, WeKan works without one, and password sign-in needs no email. Add your email settings in the Admin Panel when you want WeKan to send board invitations, notifications and password resets.
  - q: Is there a WeKan phone app?
    a: No, WeKan has no app in the App Store or Google Play. It works in a phone's web browser, and you can add it to your home screen from Safari or Chrome so it opens like an app.

WeKan is a free, open-source, self-hosted kanban board and a long-running alternative to Trello. Your team plans work as cards in lists, splits a board into swimlanes, and adds labels, due dates, checklists, comments and files to each card.

The same board can also be shown as a table, a calendar, a timeline or a Gantt chart, and rules handle routine steps, such as adding a label when a card reaches Done. A bakery, for example, could keep one board of cake orders with lists for New orders, Baking, Decorating and Ready for pickup, and check the calendar view each morning to see what is due.

If you are leaving Trello, WeKan imports your boards, and it also reads exports from Jira, Asana and many other tools. It runs in the web browser, including on a phone, where you can add it to your home screen. WeKan offers more options than Trello, so its screens take a little longer to learn.

Cloudgate installs WeKan and its database on your server and gives it a secure web address. Register the first account to become the admin, then turn off open registration so only the people you invite can join.
