Didacta is a fair-code, self-hosted course platform for selling online courses and memberships with a member community, in place of Teachable, Thinkific, Skool or Kajabi. It is early beta software from VA360 LABS, a Spanish company, and its free Community edition runs on your own server.

You build courses from sections of text, video, PDF or quiz lessons, then fill your academy three ways: enrol students yourself, sell single courses, or sell memberships that unlock a group of courses. Payments go through your own Stripe account, and access is removed on its own when a member stops paying. Students get certificates, a community feed, messages, points and badges, and live classes through Zoom. A pottery teacher, for example, could sell a beginners' wheel-throwing course, offer a monthly membership with every course included, release a new lesson each week and host a live glazing class for members.

Didacta works in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, in the web browser. It can bring over courses and students from a WordPress site running LearnDash. The AI helpers are optional and need a paid key from an AI provider.

Cloudgate installs Didacta with its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. Your courses, students and sales records stay on your server.
