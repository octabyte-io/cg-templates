Vikunja is a free, open-source, self-hosted to-do list and task manager, an alternative to Todoist, Trello and Microsoft To Do that keeps your tasks on your own server. You organise tasks into projects and can see each project as a list, a Kanban board, a Gantt chart or a table.

Tasks get due dates, reminders, repeats, labels, priorities, subtasks, assignees and attachments, and you can add one in a single line with its date and label typed in. A bakery, for example, could plan a website relaunch on a Kanban board, check the timeline on the Gantt chart before launch day, and keep a repeating "order flour every Monday" task in the same app. Share a project with your team, or send a public link to someone without an account.

Vikunja works in any web browser. Its phone app covers only the basics, but calendar apps can show your tasks through CalDAV. You can bring tasks in from TickTick or a CSV file straight away, and from Todoist, Trello or Microsoft To Do after some extra setup.

Cloudgate installs Vikunja and its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. There is no default account: open the address and create yours, and once your team has joined, turn off open sign-up.
