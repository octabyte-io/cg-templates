Yamtrack is a free, open-source, self-hosted media tracker and an alternative to Trakt, MyAnimeList and Goodreads. It keeps one private record of the movies, TV shows, anime, manga, video games, books, comics and board games you have watched, read or played, with your scores, progress, dates and notes.

You search for a title, add it, and Yamtrack fills in the cover and details. Series are tracked season by season and episode by episode, and a calendar shows what is coming out next. A family could share one Yamtrack: each person keeps their own history, and together they build a "films to watch together" list and get a message in their group chat when the next episode of a show they follow comes out.

Yamtrack imports your history from Trakt, Simkl, MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, IMDb, Goodreads, HowLongToBeat and Steam, and exports it to a CSV file whenever you like. If you run Jellyfin, Plex or Emby, what you watch there is logged for you. It works in any browser, including on your phone.
