faq:
  - q: Is WordPress free?
    a: Yes. WordPress is free, open-source software under the GPL licence, so self-hosting it costs only your server. Some themes and plugins are paid, but thousands are free.
  - q: Is WordPress a good alternative to Wix or Squarespace?
    a: Yes, if you want full control and no monthly platform fee. WordPress has far more themes and plugins than Wix or Squarespace, and you can move your site to any host. In return you keep it updated yourself and have more settings to learn.
  - q: Can I move my site from Wix or Squarespace to WordPress?
    a: From Squarespace, yes; its export file can be imported into WordPress with the built-in importer. Wix has no export, so pages are usually rebuilt in WordPress, often with a theme that looks similar.
  - q: How do I self-host WordPress?
    a: Pick WordPress in Cloudgate's template list and press Set up. Cloudgate installs WordPress and its database on your server and gives it a secure web address, where you choose the site name and create your admin account.
  - q: Can I run an online shop with WordPress?
    a: Yes. Install the free WooCommerce plugin to sell physical products, services or downloads, and take payments through Stripe, PayPal and other providers.
  - q: Is my data private?
    a: Yes. Your pages, posts, customer details and form entries are stored on your own server. Plugins you add may send data to their own services, so check what each one does.
  - q: What server size do I need?
    a: A small server (1 GB of memory) runs a typical business website. Choose a larger one for a busy online shop or a site with many plugins.

WordPress is an open-source website builder and content management system that lets anyone create and run a website, blog or online shop. It powers everything from one-page sites for a local café to large online shops and news sites. You pick a theme for the design, then add pages, blog posts and images with a visual editor.

Plugins add almost any feature you can think of: contact forms, online shops with WooCommerce, appointment booking, newsletters, translations and search engine tools. A hairdresser, for example, could have a site with a price list, a photo gallery, online booking and a blog, without writing any code, and pay no monthly fee to a website builder.

WordPress works with thousands of services through plugins, including Stripe, PayPal, Mailchimp and Google Analytics, and the free WordPress mobile app lets you write posts from your phone. You can import posts from Blogger, Tumblr, Squarespace and other WordPress sites.

Cloudgate installs WordPress and its database on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address, with upload limits large enough for photos and themes. The first person to open that address chooses the site's name and creates the admin account. You can later point your own domain name at it and invite editors.
