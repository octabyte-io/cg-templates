LinkStack is a free, open-source, self-hosted link-in-bio page, an alternative to Linktree: one web page with your photo, name, a short bio and buttons to everything you want people to find. It is the page you put in the single link Instagram, TikTok and other profiles allow.

You add links from an editor that shows a live preview, with ready-made buttons for dozens of sites, plus headings, text, an email address, a phone number or a contact card. Themes change the look in a few clicks, and each link shows how many times it has been clicked. A bakery, for example, could link its cake orders, this week's bread menu, opening hours and social profiles from one page in its Instagram bio, and see which link people open most.

One LinkStack can also host pages for other people: turn on sign-ups and each person gets their own page, managed from an admin panel. It works in any browser on a phone or computer, and there is no app to install.

Cloudgate installs LinkStack on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, and gives it a secure web address. Open that address right after setup to run the short setup wizard and create your admin account.
