WBO (Whitebophir) is a free, open-source self-hosted whiteboard where several people draw on one large board at the same time, a simple alternative to Miro and Microsoft Whiteboard. Everyone sees each line, shape and note appear live, and every board is saved on your own server as you draw.

The tools are kept simple: a freehand pen, straight lines, rectangles, ellipses, text, an eraser and a choice of colours and sizes. Each board has its own web address, so you make one per client, lesson or project and send the link. A kitchen-renovation firm, for example, could open a board during a call, sketch the client's layout while they watch and add notes from the conversation, then come back to the same board after the site visit.

WBO is not a full Miro replacement: it has no sticky notes, templates, images or comments, and boards download only as SVG images. It has no accounts of its own, so this template asks for one shared login before anyone can open a board. It works in any browser, on tablets and phones too.

Cloudgate installs WBO on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. Cloudgate is not connected with WBO or its makers.
