Plausible shows you how many people visit your website, which pages they read, and where they came from, all on one simple page. You do not need training to read it: the numbers that matter are at the top, and you click any of them to dig in.

It uses no cookies and collects no personal data, so most websites can use it without a cookie banner. That keeps your site cleaner for visitors and keeps their data away from ad companies. A shop owner, for example, can see that a newsletter brought in 300 visitors on Tuesday and that 12 of them bought something.

Cloudgate installs Plausible on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to register becomes the owner and adds the website to track. Plausible works with any website, including WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and Ghost, and can import your past data from Google Analytics.
