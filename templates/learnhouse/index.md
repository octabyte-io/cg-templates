faq:
  - q: Is LearnHouse free?
    a: Yes, LearnHouse is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with no limit on courses or learners when you host it yourself. Payments, analytics, single sign-on, SCORM, live classes, messages and audit logs are part of a paid Enterprise licence.
  - q: Can I sell courses with self-hosted LearnHouse?
    a: No, not with the free edition. Selling courses through a store and checkout is an Enterprise feature when you host LearnHouse yourself, so the free edition suits free courses, school classes and staff training.
  - q: Is LearnHouse a good alternative to Teachable or Thinkific?
    a: Yes, if you want to run courses on your own server rather than sell them. LearnHouse gives you lessons, graded assignments, certificates and communities with no fee per learner, but taking payments needs its paid Enterprise licence.
  - q: Can I import my courses from Moodle or Canvas?
    a: Yes, LearnHouse imports course exports from Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, D2L Brightspace, Open edX and any Common Cartridge file, and turns quizzes into assignments or quiz blocks. It does not import from Teachable or Thinkific, and SCORM packages need the Enterprise licence.
  - q: How do I sign in to LearnHouse the first time?
    a: LearnHouse creates one admin account when it is installed. Sign in with the admin email you entered at setup and the password Cloudgate created for it, shown in Cloudgate, then invite your teachers and learners from the dashboard.
  - q: Does LearnHouse have AI features?
    a: Yes, optionally. LearnHouse can draft courses, help in the editor and answer learners' questions from your own course content once you connect an AI provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a local Ollama; the provider bills you for what you use.
  - q: What server size do I need for LearnHouse?
    a: LearnHouse needs at least 2 GB of memory, and the project recommends 4 GB and two processor cores. Video and file uploads use disk space, so plan for 20 GB or more.

LearnHouse is a free, open-source learning management system (LMS) for running online courses on your own server, a self-hosted alternative to Teachable, Thinkific and TalentLMS for schools, trainers and teams. You write lessons in a block editor much like a document, set assignments that are graded automatically or by hand, and award certificates when learners finish a course or a learning path.

Next to the courses, learners get discussion communities, podcasts and shared whiteboards, and you can release chapters on a schedule. A small company could, for example, move its staff onboarding into LearnHouse: one learning path of three short courses, a quiz at the end of each, and a certificate that the new hire and their manager can check online.

If your courses live in Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace or Open edX today, LearnHouse can import their exports. AI help for writing courses and answering learners is optional and uses your own AI provider. Selling courses, analytics, single sign-on and live classes are part of a paid Enterprise licence.
