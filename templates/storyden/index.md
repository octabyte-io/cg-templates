faq:
  - q: Is Storyden free?
    a: Yes, Storyden is free and open source under the MPL-2.0 licence, with no paid edition or member limit. You pay only for your server. Its optional AI tools use your own OpenAI or Anthropic account, which that provider bills you for.
  - q: Is Storyden a good alternative to Circle?
    a: Yes, if your community needs discussions and a shared knowledge base on your own domain without a monthly fee. Storyden pairs a forum with a wiki-style library, but unlike Circle it has no courses, events pages or paid memberships.
  - q: How do I sign in to Storyden the first time?
    a: Open your Storyden web address and register an account. The first account registered becomes the admin, so do it right after installing, before you share the address.
  - q: Can I import my existing forum into Storyden?
    a: No, Storyden has no tool to import a forum from Circle, XenForo, Discourse or other software. Most communities start fresh and keep the old forum readable for a while.
  - q: What size server does Storyden need?
    a: A small one. Storyden's documentation says it idles at about 80 MB of memory and that 1 GB is plenty for a small to medium community.
  - q: Is there a Storyden phone app?
    a: No, there is no phone app. Storyden works in any phone or computer browser, with a layout made for small screens.
  - q: Do I have to use Storyden's AI features?
    a: No, the AI features are optional and off until you turn them on. To use them, you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key in the admin settings.

Storyden is free, open-source, self-hosted community software that puts a discussion forum and a shared knowledge base in one place, as an alternative to paid community platforms such as Circle, XenForo or Invision Community. Members talk in threads, and the best of what they share is kept in a library of wiki-style pages that stays easy to find.

A car club, for example, could discuss weekend drives and repairs in the forum, keep a library of model guides and trusted garages with fields such as Region and Status, and let members suggest new entries for a moderator to approve. Shared links and collections help members gather useful finds.

Roles, permissions and moderation tools such as reports, warnings and word filters help you keep things friendly. Members can sign in with a password, a passkey, or a Google, GitHub or Discord account. Optional AI tools can suggest titles, tags and summaries if you connect your own AI provider. Storyden works in any browser; there is no phone app.

Cloudgate installs Storyden on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. Register right after setup: the first account becomes the admin.
