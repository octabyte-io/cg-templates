Paheko is free, open-source, self-hosted software for running an association: the member register, membership fees, email reminders and double-entry accounting made simple for volunteers, in one place. It is a no-subscription alternative to AssoConnect, and its interface is in French.

Formerly called Garradin, it has been developed since 2012 to cut the time a board spends on paperwork. A community bike workshop, for example, could keep its member list, see at a glance who has not renewed this year, email them a reminder, record each repair sale and grant, and hand the treasurer an income statement at the general meeting.

Members can sign in to update their own details, and the association can share documents and publish a simple website from the same tool. Extensions add a till, member cards, bookings, expense claims, volunteer time tracking and HelloAsso for online payments. The ready-made charts of accounts are for France, Belgium and Switzerland.

Cloudgate installs Paheko on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. Open the address straight after setup to create your association and administrator account.
