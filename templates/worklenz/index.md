Worklenz is a free, open-source, self-hosted project management tool, an alternative to ClickUp and Asana, that keeps projects, tasks, time tracking and team workload in one place. It suits agencies and service teams that need to know who is doing what, for how long, and whether a project stays on budget.

Your team plans work as tasks and subtasks and views them as a list, a board or a timeline. Everyone logs time on tasks with a timer, and the workload view shows who has spare capacity before you take on new work. Project finance tracks budgets, costs and billable time. A small design agency, for example, could plan each client's website as a project, log the hours spent on it and check at the end of the month which projects made money.

Worklenz works in any web browser, with no limit on the number of people. You can bring a task list over from ClickUp, Asana or a spreadsheet as a CSV file, and export a project to CSV at any time.

Cloudgate installs Worklenz on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect, together with its database and file storage, and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open that address signs up and creates the organisation, then invites the rest of the team. Sign-up stays open after that, so others who find the address can create their own separate organisations.
