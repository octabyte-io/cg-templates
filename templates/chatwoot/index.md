Chatwoot brings every customer conversation into one shared inbox. Messages from the chat window on your website, your support email address, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram all arrive in the same place, so nobody has to check five apps to see who is waiting for an answer.

Your team can assign conversations to each other, add private notes, and use saved replies for common questions. You can also publish a help centre with answers people can find on their own. An online shop, for example, can have two people answering chat and email during the day, with every past conversation with a customer in front of them.

Cloudgate installs Chatwoot on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign in becomes the admin, connects the channels and invites the team. Chatwoot has apps for iPhone and Android, and plugins for WordPress and Shopify.
