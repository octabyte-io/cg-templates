Chatwoot is an open-source customer support app that brings every customer conversation into one shared inbox. Messages from the chat window on your website, your support email address, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Telegram all arrive in the same place, so nobody has to check five apps to see who is waiting for an answer.

Your team can assign conversations to each other, add private notes and use saved replies for common questions. You can also publish a help centre with articles customers can find on their own, and see reports on response times and satisfaction ratings. An online shop, for example, can have two people answering chat and email during the day, with every past conversation with a customer in front of them.

Chatwoot has free apps for iPhone and Android and connects to other tools through webhooks, with ready-made integrations for services such as Slack and Dialogflow. The chat window is added to a website by pasting one snippet of code. To invite teammates and reply by email you need an email sending service, which you can fill in during setup.

Cloudgate installs Chatwoot, its database and its background workers on a server Cloudgate provides or one you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open that address creates the admin account, connects the channels and invites the support team.
