### Worklenz and ClickUp pricing

As of November 2026, ClickUp's pricing page shows these plans, per user a month, billed yearly:

- **Free Forever**: unlimited members, 60 MB of storage, 5 spaces, Gantt charts and time tracking as trials only.
- **Core**: $9, with unlimited storage and Gantt charts, custom fields, time tracking and 1,000 automations a month.
- **Business**: $17, adding workload, resource management, timesheets, advanced dashboards and Google sign-in.
- **Enterprise**: priced by ClickUp's sales team, with SAML single sign-on, audit logs and white labelling.

A Max plan at $100 per user a month adds ClickUp's fuller AI features. Extra AI credits cost $10 for 10,000.

The self-hosted Worklenz Community Edition has no licence fee and no user limit. Your cost is the server for Worklenz, its PostgreSQL database and its file storage. Worklenz also sells a cloud service, free for up to 5 members and 3 projects, then Pro at $6 and Business at $10 per user a month billed yearly ($8 and $12 monthly). Its self-hosted Business plan, which adds single sign-on, custom branding, advanced reporting and priority support, is priced by its sales team.

### Hosting and your data

ClickUp runs on ClickUp's own cloud. Worklenz Community Edition runs on a server you choose, so client projects, time logs and budgets stay with you. The trade-off is that you look after the server, its updates and its backups.

### Time tracking, workload and budgets

This is where Worklenz is strongest. Every task has a timer and manual time entry, and timesheets and time reports show where hours go. The workload view and scheduler show each person's capacity across projects, and project finance tracks budgets, costs and billable time. A client portal lets clients follow progress.

ClickUp covers the same ground across its plans: time tracking from Core, and workload, resource management and timesheets from Business.

### What ClickUp has that Worklenz lacks

- **Docs and whiteboards**: wikis, nested pages and a canvas that turns ideas into tasks.
- **Chat**: channels, direct messages and threads next to the work.
- **AI**: ClickUp Brain, with AI credits on every plan.
- **More views and automations**: dozens of views and automations with triggers, conditions and webhooks.
- **Integrations and apps**: native integrations such as Google Calendar, Figma and Zapier, plus iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux apps.
