### Didacta and Teachable pricing

As of November 2026, Teachable has no free plan, only a 7-day free trial:

- **Starter**: $29 a month billed yearly, or $39 monthly. Up to 5 published products and a 7.5% transaction fee.
- **Builder**: $69 a month yearly, or $89 monthly. Up to 10 products and a 0% transaction fee with Teachable Payments.
- **Growth**: $139 a month yearly, or $189 monthly. Up to 50 products, certificates and no Teachable branding.
- **Custom**: priced by Teachable's sales team.

Card fees apply on top: 2.9% + 30¢ for US cards and 3.9% + 30¢ for international cards.

Didacta's Community edition is free to self-host. You connect your own Stripe account and Didacta takes no commission, so your costs are the server and Stripe's card fees. Didacta Cloud, the hosted version, is free to start but keeps 5% of each sale, pays out weekly and does not let you connect your own Stripe.

### Courses, memberships and community

Both sell single courses and memberships with drip-fed lessons, quizzes and certificates. Didacta adds the community in the same app: a feed, chat rooms and direct messages, a resource library, points, streaks and badges, a list of members at risk of dropping out, and live classes run through Zoom. It also takes SCORM packages as lessons.

Teachable sells courses, coaching with scheduling, digital downloads and memberships, with upsells, cart recovery and an affiliate program on its paid plans.

### Licence, editions and maturity

Didacta is fair-code, not open source. Its Sustainable Use License lets you run it for your own academy; hosting it as a service for other people needs an agreement with VA360 LABS. A paid Enterprise licence adds single sign-on (SAML and OIDC), SCIM, white-label and several organisations on one server.

Didacta entered public beta in August 2026, so expect frequent updates and rough edges. Teachable is an established service with a support team on its Builder plan and up.

### Phone apps and running it

Teachable's free iPhone and Android student apps include offline downloads and push reminders. Didacta has no native app yet; courses, the community and messages open in the phone's browser.

Self-hosted Didacta needs PostgreSQL with pgvector and Redis, and you handle updates, backups and an email service. Teachable runs all of that for you.
