### Joomla and Wix pricing

As of November 2026, Wix has a free plan and four paid plans, priced per month in US dollars and billed yearly; monthly billing costs more:

- **Free**: a wixsite.com address, Wix branding, 500 MB of storage and no payments.
- **Light**: $17 a month ($24 monthly), with 2 GB of storage and 2 site collaborators.
- **Core**: $29 a month ($36 monthly), with 50 GB, payments, eCommerce, bookings and 5 collaborators.
- **Business**: $39 a month ($46 monthly), with 100 GB and 10 collaborators.
- **Business Elite**: $159 a month ($172 monthly), with unlimited storage and 100 collaborators.

Wix says prices and currency vary by location, and taxes are added on top.

Joomla has no licence fee and no paid edition. You pay for the server, a domain name and any paid templates or extensions. Joomla 6 needs PHP 8.3 or newer and MySQL, MariaDB or PostgreSQL; on Cloudgate a small server with 1 GB of memory runs a typical site.

### Languages and permissions

This is where Joomla stands out. Multilingual sites are part of the core, with over 70 language packs and links between the versions of each page. Its access control lists and nine default user groups decide who can read, write, edit or publish each part of the site, and two-factor sign-in is built in.

Wix covers the same needs differently. Its Wix Multilingual app can translate a site automatically into over 130 languages using word credits, sold in packs of 3,000 to 50,000 words, or you translate by hand. Wix has roles and custom roles for the people who manage a site, with 2 to 100 collaborators depending on the plan.

### Building pages

Wix has a drag-and-drop editor, over 2,000 templates and an AI builder that creates a site from a prompt. Joomla has no drag-and-drop builder of its own; page builders are extensions, often paid. Editors write articles in a visual editor, templates set the look, and menus and modules decide what appears where. There is more to learn, but it suits a structured site with many pages.

### Hosting and ownership

Wix's help centre says a Wix site needs to be hosted on Wix's servers; you can connect your own domain but not move the site. A Joomla site's files and database are yours to copy to any host. In return, you keep Joomla and its extensions up to date, and reliable password-reset emails need an email sending service.
