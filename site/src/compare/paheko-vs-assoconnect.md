### Paheko and AssoConnect pricing

As of November 2026, AssoConnect's plans are priced a month, including VAT, by the number of contacts in your base:

- **Paiement**: free, with no subscription, for collecting payments online.
- **Comptabilité**: €23 a month for the accounting tool.
- **Communication**: from €23 a month for up to 50 contacts.
- **Sérénité**: from €28 a month for up to 50 contacts, rising to €112 for 1,000 and €335 for 5,000. It covers members, accounting, payments, a website and email campaigns, with a 14-day free trial.
- **Expert**: from €109 a month for up to 300 contacts, adding 5 local sections, organisations as members and SEPA direct debit.

Paheko has no licence fee and no member limit. Your cost is the server. If you would rather not run it, the Paheko team hosts it at cost for €5 a year with unlimited members, plus a donation of your choice; it suggests about 0.3% of your yearly income.

### Members, fees and reminders

Both keep a member register. Paheko lets you choose the fields, tracks who has paid this year's fee and emails automatic reminders. Members can sign in to update their details, see their fees and download their data. Extensions add member cards, bookings, a till with stock, expense claims and volunteer time.

AssoConnect manages members, volunteers, donors and beneficiaries, with online forms for memberships, donations and events. Expert adds a shared base for local sections and organisations as members.

### Accounting

Both do the books for volunteers. Paheko uses double-entry accounting made for beginners, with an income statement, balance sheet, PDF export, analytical accounts by project and tax receipts for donations. AssoConnect's accounting includes bank reconciliation, financial statements in PDF and Excel and links to your payments, memberships and events.

### Payments, website and email

AssoConnect is ahead on payments. Card payments for memberships, donations, ticketing and a shop are built in, with cheques and cash recorded too, and Expert adds SEPA direct debit for regular donations.

Paheko has no card payments of its own; online payments go through its HelloAsso extension. Both send newsletters and email campaigns and publish a simple website. With Paheko on your own server, you set up email sending and look after backups yourself.

### Hosting and language

AssoConnect is a hosted service. Paheko runs on your own server, or on the Paheko team's hosting. Both are in French. Paheko's ready-made charts of accounts cover France, Belgium and Switzerland.
