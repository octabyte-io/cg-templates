### DailyTxT and Day One pricing

As of November 2026, Day One has three plans, priced per year:

- **Basic**: free, on 1 device, with 1 photo per entry, unlimited journals and end-to-end encryption.
- **Silver**: $49.99 a year, with unlimited devices, up to 30 photos or videos per entry, audio recordings with transcription and 25% off printed journals.
- **Gold**: $74.99 a year, adding AI features such as guided reflection and summaries, with daily limits, and 35% off printed journals.

DailyTxT has no plans and no paid edition. It is free under the MIT licence, with no limit on entries, users, photos or files per day. Your cost is the server, and DailyTxT is small enough for Cloudgate's smallest size.

### Where your diary lives

Day One keeps your journal in its own cloud, with end-to-end encryption, so your entries are encrypted on your device before they sync.

DailyTxT keeps your diary on a server you choose. Each person's entries and files are encrypted there with a key that only their password unlocks, so even the person who runs the server cannot read them through the app. The trade-off is that encryption happens on the server rather than on your phone, and you look after the server and its backups yourself. If you forget your password, only the backup keys DailyTxT shows you once can open your entries again.

### Apps and writing

Day One has native apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Apple Watch and Windows, and works in a browser too. You can write many entries a day across many journals, and add photos, videos, drawings and audio.

DailyTxT runs in the browser on a computer or phone, and you can install it to your home screen as a web app. It keeps one entry per day, written in Markdown with a live preview, and saves as you type. You can tag entries, search every word, tag and file name, pin places on a map with GPX tracks, save entry templates and read a whole month in a reading view.

### Photos, memories and export

Day One allows 1 photo per entry on Basic and up to 30 photos or videos per entry on paid plans, and has On This Day to look back at past entries and printed journal books.

DailyTxT lets you upload any files to a day, up to 500 MB each; photos appear in a gallery under the entry. It shows what you wrote on the same date in earlier years and your writing streaks, and exports your diary with its files to an HTML page you can open anywhere.

### Families and several users

DailyTxT lets several people keep separate diaries on one installation. Each account has its own encryption key, and the admin panel manages accounts but cannot open anyone's entries. It comes in 11 languages, including English, German, French, Chinese and Japanese.
