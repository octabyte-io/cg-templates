### Pascal Editor and Planner 5D pricing

As of November 2026, Planner 5D's pricing page shows these plans, in US dollars:

- **Free**: unlimited projects, about half of the furniture catalogue and no renders. You can buy one-time credit packs.
- **Premium**: $19.99 a month, or $59.99 billed yearly, with the full catalogue, unlimited HD renders, uploading a plan to turn it into 3D, and 100 AI credits a month.
- **Professional**: $49.99 a month, or $399.99 billed yearly, adding 4K renders, 360° panoramas and walkthroughs, 3D model uploads, custom textures, API access and more AI credits.
- **Team and Enterprise**: shared workspaces with roles, or a white-label planner, priced by Planner 5D's sales team.

The page also shows lower introductory prices for the first period. Teachers and professors can use Planner 5D free through its Education program.

Self-hosted Pascal Editor has no licence fee and no limits. Its server side is light, so Cloudgate runs it on a small server. Pascal's own hosted editor sells extras such as AI design runs paid with credits, which are not part of the self-hosted editor.

### Drawing rooms and buildings

Both tools let you draw a floor plan and see it in 3D. In Pascal Editor, you draw a room as a rectangle, a polygon or wall by wall, and each room gets its walls, floor, ceiling, name and area. Doors, windows, stairs, kitchens and hip, gable, flat, mansard or barrel roofs are building parts you adjust. You can work over several floors and a basement, add terrain, fences and terraces, and trace over a 3D scan or a picture of an old plan.

Planner 5D covers the same drawing in 2D and 3D. Its paid plans add AI that turns an uploaded plan into an editable design, an AI interior designer and automatic decor.

### Furniture, decoration and renders

This is where Planner 5D is ahead. Its full catalogue has over 8,000 furniture and decor items, and its renders let you set light, shadows and colours. Professional adds 4K renders, 360° panoramas, walkthroughs and mood boards, and Planner 5D can make a shopping list with cost estimates.

Pascal Editor has a smaller, searchable library of furniture and fittings and materials for walls, floors and roofs. You see the design in an interactive 3D view, but it makes no photo-quality images.

### File exports: IFC, OBJ, DWG and PDF

Pascal Editor exports the model as GLB, USDZ, OBJ, STL or IFC, makes 3MF and STL files for 3D printing, prints the floor plan to PDF, and can share a read-only 3D view by link. It also has a Blender add-on.

Planner 5D exports plans and renders to PDF, and its Professional users can export 2D drawings as DWG or DXF. Pascal Editor cannot open Planner 5D projects.

### Apps, hosting and team access

Planner 5D runs on the web, Windows, iPhone and Android, and keeps your projects in its cloud. Pascal Editor runs in a desktop browser on your own server, where everyone on your team signs in with one shared password and sees the same projects. There are no separate user accounts or roles.
