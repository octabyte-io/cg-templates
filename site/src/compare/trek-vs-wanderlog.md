### TREK and Wanderlog pricing

As of November 2026, Wanderlog is free to use and sells one upgrade, Wanderlog Pro. Wanderlog does not show its prices on a public pricing page. The US App Store lists Pro's annual plan at $39.99 a year, the price Wanderlog's own blog gives, and also shows other annual prices and monthly plans. According to Wanderlog's blog, Pro adds:

- offline maps and directions (the free version keeps your itinerary offline);
- route optimization for each day's plan;
- exporting a trip to Google Maps;
- scanning your Gmail for reservation emails.

TREK has no paid tier. It is free under the AGPL-3.0 licence, every feature is in the self-hosted version, and your cost is the server, where a small one is enough.

### Planning together

Both tools let a group edit one trip live. TREK adds a group chat, shared notes and polls for deciding together, guests who need no login, and a read-only link where you choose what it shows. Places, notes and bookings are dragged between days, and the map shows each day's route and travel times. Wanderlog invites tripmates by email or link and syncs edits live too.

### Bookings, costs and packing

Wanderlog reads reservations from emails you forward to it, and Pro can scan Gmail. TREK has no address to forward to: you upload confirmations as emails, PDFs, wallet passes or photos, it reads them and you check each one before it is saved. Both track a budget and split bills. TREK handles several payers and currencies, equal or custom shares and settle-up suggestions, and adds packing lists and to-dos with templates and people assigned to each item.

### Guides, places and offline use

Wanderlog is stronger for deciding where to go: it has travel guides, recommendations, hotel search and an AI assistant. TREK has no guides or hotel booking; it searches places and addresses with OpenStreetMap and its own index, with no API key, and shows a 16-day weather forecast. TREK's iPhone, iPad and Android apps and web app open trips and maps with no signal at no charge, where Wanderlog keeps offline maps for Pro.

### Hosting and your data

Wanderlog keeps your trips, bookings and travel dates on its servers. TREK keeps them on a server you choose, which you look after, though Cloudgate installs TREK and gives it a secure web address.
