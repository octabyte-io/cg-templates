### Swing Music and Spotify pricing

As of November 2026, Spotify's US site shows these plans, each billed monthly:

- **Free**: Spotify's ad-supported service.
- **Premium Individual**: $12.99 a month, after a free month for new subscribers.
- **Premium Duo**: $18.99 a month for two people at the same address.
- **Premium Family**: $21.99 a month for up to six people at the same address.
- **Premium Student**: $6.99 a month for eligible students.

Premium removes ads, lets you download songs and streams at up to 24-bit/44.1 kHz. Individual, Duo and Family include 15 hours of audiobooks a month.

Swing Music's server and web player are free. Its optional paid licence, as of November 2026:

- **Pro**: $4.99 a month billed monthly, or $49.99 billed yearly.
- **Lifetime**: $99, paid once.

Prices are before tax, and there is no free trial. The licence adds recommendations and mixes, secure share links, transcoding, classical music browsing and up to 3 swingmx.com web addresses. Your other cost is the server and the disk your music needs.

### Your own music or Spotify's catalogue

Spotify streams a catalogue of over 100 million tracks in 184 markets. You play almost anything, but you rent it, and you lose access when you stop paying. Spotify's apps can also play files stored on your device as local files.

Swing Music plays only what you upload. New songs appear after a scan, which you start in the settings or set to run on its own.

### Swing Music's web player and library

Swing Music's web player is built to feel like a modern streaming app, and it does extra work on your files:

- **Tidy tags**: it cleans up messy song tags and groups album versions such as Deluxe or Remaster.
- **No duplicates**: it hides duplicate songs and keeps the best-quality copy.
- **Folder view**: browse music the way it sits on disk.
- **Collections**: group whole albums and artists on one page.
- **Stats**: your top artists, albums and songs over a week, month or year, plus Last.fm scrobbling.

Spotify's player has what a catalogue makes possible: recommendations based on your taste and playlists made for you, free on every plan.

### Swing Music and Spotify phone apps

Spotify has official apps for iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux. Swing Music has a free official Android app that pairs by scanning a QR code. It has no iPhone app, and it does not support Subsonic, so other music apps can't connect; on an iPhone you use the web player in the browser.
