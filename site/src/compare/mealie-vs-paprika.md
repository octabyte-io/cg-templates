### Mealie and Paprika pricing

As of November 2026, Paprika 4 is free to download on every platform, and one plan covers all your devices:

- **Free**: up to 50 recipes, one grocery list and one cookbook, with no cloud sync.
- **Standard**: free for anyone who bought an earlier Paprika, with unlimited recipes, cloud sync and more lists and cookbooks.
- **Premium**: $14.99 a year at its introductory price in the US, or $9.99 a year for earlier buyers, with a one-week free trial. Prices vary by region.

Premium adds family accounts, recipe scanning, social media import, nutrition, calendar feeds and Alexa. Before Paprika 4, each Paprika app was a separate one-time purchase.

Mealie has no plans and no recipe limit. Your cost is the server it runs on, plus an AI provider only if you turn on the AI imports.

### Apps and offline use

This is where Paprika is ahead. It has apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows, an Apple Watch app, and its apps work offline.

Mealie is a web app. It works in any browser and can be added to a phone's home screen so it opens like an app, but there is nothing to install from the App Store or Google Play, and it has no offline mode.

### Sharing with your household

Mealie is built for sharing. Everyone you invite gets their own sign-in to the same recipe collection, and Mealie can split a server into households that share recipes but keep their own meal plans and shopping lists.

In Paprika, sharing one collection with family needs Premium. A family account holds up to six people, who share the owner's recipe collection and subscription.

### Recipes, meal plans and shopping lists

Both save recipes from a website's address, scale servings, plan meals on a calendar and turn recipes into a grocery list sorted by aisle. Mealie can pick recipes for the plan by tag or category, publish the plan as a calendar feed and send it to other tools through webhooks. With an AI provider you connect, it reads recipes from photos, pasted text or cooking videos.

Paprika adds a pantry with expiry dates, a cook mode with voice commands, menus, Apple Watch timers and nutrition from USDA data in Premium. Its Premium plan also scans cookbook pages and imports recipes from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
