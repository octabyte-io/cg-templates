### Leantime and Asana pricing

As of November 2026, Asana has these plans:

- **Personal**: free, for one or two people, with list, board and calendar views.
- **Starter**: $10.99 per user a month billed yearly, or $13.49 billed monthly. Adds timeline and Gantt views, forms, unlimited rules and reporting dashboards.
- **Advanced**: $24.99 per user a month billed yearly, or $30.49 billed monthly. Adds goals, portfolios, workload, approvals and time tracking that compares estimated and actual time.
- **Enterprise** and **Enterprise+**: priced by Asana's sales team, with SAML sign-in, SCIM, audit logs and data residency.

The self-hosted Leantime has no licence fee and no user limit; your cost is the server, and a small one with 1 GB of memory is enough. Some extras, such as custom fields, recurring tasks and whiteboards, are paid plugins. Leantime also sells its own cloud, Leantime Pro, at $8 per user a month billed yearly or $10 billed monthly, and that is where its AI features are.

### Views and planning

Both put tasks on boards, lists and calendars. Leantime shows the same to-dos as a kanban board, a table, a list or a calendar, with subtasks and dependencies, and lays milestones out on a Gantt-style timeline. Each person starts from a My Work page that groups their to-dos by due date.

Asana's free plan has list, board and calendar views. Timeline and Gantt views, with task dependencies, start at Starter.

### Goals, time and planning tools

Leantime builds in things Asana keeps for higher plans. Goals track a number from a start value towards a target, timesheets record hours against each to-do as billable or not, and canvases such as a Lean Canvas, Business Model Canvas or SWOT help with planning. Docs, wikis, files and screen recordings sit with the work.

In Asana, goals and portfolios are on the Advanced plan. The free plan tracks time only through integrations, and Advanced compares estimated with actual time.

### What Asana has that Leantime lacks

- **Automation and forms.** Asana includes unlimited rules and forms from Starter, and branching forms on Advanced. Leantime has neither.
- **Portfolios and workload.** Asana's Advanced plan plans team capacity across projects.
- **Integrations.** Asana connects to Slack, Google Drive, Zoom and over 100 other apps, plus Salesforce, Tableau and Power BI on Advanced. Leantime sends notifications to Slack, Mattermost and Discord and exports calendars.
- **Phone and desktop apps.** Asana has apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows. Leantime's app is in beta and needs a paid add-on to connect to your own server.
- **AI.** Asana includes AI requests on paid plans. Leantime's AI features are only in its paid cloud.

### Hosting: your own server or Asana's cloud

Asana runs only in its own cloud, with a choice of data region on Enterprise+. The self-hosted Leantime runs on a server you choose, so your projects, files and timesheets stay with you; the trade-off is looking after the server, its updates and backups. Leantime also offers an on-premise Enterprise edition with single sign-on, priced on request.
