### LedgerSMB and QuickBooks pricing

As of November 2026, QuickBooks Online's US pricing page lists five plans, priced per month with no annual contract:

- **Free**: $0, for 1 user, with 2 invoices a month and 1 connected bank.
- **Simple Start**: $38 a month, for 1 user.
- **Essentials**: $85 a month, for 3 users.
- **Plus**: $140 a month, for 5 users, adding inventory tracking, budgets and project profitability.
- **Advanced**: $340 a month, for 25 users.

Intuit was offering 70% off for the first three months when we checked. Payroll is extra, from $50 a month plus $7 per employee.

LedgerSMB has no licence fee and no paid edition. A small server with 1 GB of memory is enough for a small business. If you want help, consultants listed on LedgerSMB's support page offer paid support and hosting.

### Double-entry accounting in both

Both keep double-entry books with a chart of accounts, invoices, bills, payments, a trial balance, an income statement and a balance sheet.

LedgerSMB is built for people who know accounting. Every invoice, payment and stock movement posts straight to the general ledger, you can have a second person approve entries before they post, and it has year-end closing, budgets and fixed assets. It comes in 45 languages with 29 national charts of accounts.

QuickBooks Online aims at owners who are not accountants: its paid plans include automated bookkeeping and connect to your bank to bring in transactions.

### Orders, stock and several companies

LedgerSMB includes the parts of a small ERP on every install:

- Quotations that turn into sales orders and invoices, and the same for purchases.
- Parts, services and assemblies in several warehouses, with shipping and receiving.
- Timecards that turn into invoices, with projects and departments compared against budgets.
- Several currencies, and as many companies as you need on one install.

In QuickBooks Online, inventory tracking and project profitability start on the Plus plan, and batch invoices and custom user permissions on Advanced.

### What QuickBooks has that LedgerSMB lacks

- **Bank feeds.** QuickBooks connects to your bank. LedgerSMB imports statement files instead.
- **Payroll.** QuickBooks sells it as an add-on. LedgerSMB has none.
- **Phone apps and a dashboard.** QuickBooks Online has apps for iPhone and Android. LedgerSMB runs in a web browser and has no dashboard.
- **Connected apps.** Intuit says QuickBooks integrates with over 800 business apps. LedgerSMB has no app marketplace.
- **A modern interface.** LedgerSMB's screens are older and denser, and use terms such as AR, AP and GL.
