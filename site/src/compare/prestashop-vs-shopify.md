### PrestaShop and Shopify pricing

As of November 2026, Shopify has four main plans, shown in US dollars:

- **Basic**: $39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly, with an extra 2% per sale if you use a payment provider other than Shopify Payments.
- **Grow**: $105 a month, or $79 billed yearly, with 5 staff accounts and a 1% fee for other providers.
- **Advanced**: $399 a month, or $299 billed yearly, with 15 staff accounts, live carrier shipping rates and a 0.6% fee for other providers.
- **Plus**: from $2,300 a month, with up to 9 free expansion stores.

Shopify Payments' online card rate runs from 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic to 2.25% + 30¢ on Plus. Shopify offers 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months on most plans.

The self-hosted PrestaShop download has no licence fee and takes no cut of your sales. You pay for the server, your payment provider's card fees and any paid modules or themes from PrestaShop Addons. PrestaShop also sells a Hosted offer, run for you.

### Hosting and ownership

Shopify is a hosted service: hosting, a custom domain, SSL, security and updates come with every plan.

PrestaShop 9 runs on PHP 8.1 or later with MySQL or MariaDB, on a server you choose. Your products, orders and customer data stay there, and you can change any theme, module or line of code. You look after updates, backups and security yourself.

### Catalogue, orders and several shops

PrestaShop is a dedicated shop engine. Products have photos, stock and combinations such as size and colour. Orders move through statuses with invoices and delivery slips, and you see abandoned carts. One back office runs several shops, each with its own products, languages, currencies, payments and tax rules, which suits selling across Europe with the right VAT for each country.

Shopify includes storefront translation, local currencies and Shopify Markets on every plan, with up to 9 free expansion stores on Plus.

### Apps, modules and themes

The Shopify App Store lists over 16,000 apps and its theme store has hundreds of themes. Shopify also includes email and SMS marketing, over 200 reports and discounted shipping labels on every plan.

PrestaShop comes with the Hummingbird theme and modules for payments, shipping and marketing. Many more modules and themes are on PrestaShop Addons, and a lot of them are paid. Bank transfer and cheque are built in; cards and PayPal come through payment modules.

### Phones and in-person selling

Shopify's phone app for iPhone and Android manages orders and stock and takes in-person payments with Tap to Pay, and Shopify sells POS Pro at $89 a month per location. PrestaShop's shop and back office work in a phone's browser.
