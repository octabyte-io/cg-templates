### Wealthfolio and Sharesight pricing

As of November 2026, Sharesight's pricing page shows four plans in US dollars:

- **Free**: 1 portfolio, 10 holdings (closed ones count) and basic support.
- **Starter**: $7 a month billed yearly, or $9.33 monthly, for 1 portfolio and 30 holdings.
- **Standard**: $18 a month billed yearly, or $24 monthly, for 4 portfolios and unlimited holdings.
- **Premium**: $23.25 a month billed yearly, or $31 monthly, for 10 portfolios and unlimited holdings.

Wealthfolio's core app is free, with no limit on accounts or holdings; self-hosted, your cost is the server. Its makers sell an optional Wealthfolio Connect subscription for automatic broker sync, encrypted device sync and cloud backups: Basic at $2.99 a month, Essentials with broker sync at $7.99 and Duo for two people at $12.99, or $29, $79 and $129 a year. Their FAQ says self-hosting with manual imports needs nothing else.

### Getting your trades in

Sharesight imports trades from more than 200 brokers, reads trade confirmation emails, takes spreadsheet files and adds dividends, reinvestment plans and share splits for you.

Self-hosted Wealthfolio, as Cloudgate installs it, has no link to your broker. You add trades by hand or import your broker's CSV file, matching its columns once per account. Prices come from Yahoo Finance by default, and you can add providers such as Alpha Vantage or Finnhub with your own key.

### Tax reports

Sharesight works out capital gains and taxable income to local tax rules, such as Australia's ATO and Canada's CRA, and you can share reports with your accountant. Wealthfolio makes no tax reports. It lets you choose a cost basis method (FIFO, average, LIFO or HIFO), but the tax return itself stays with you or your accountant.

### More than a portfolio

Sharesight is built around investment portfolios. Wealthfolio covers more of your money: add your home, car, loans and mortgage for a full net worth, sort spending into categories and budgets, set saving goals, plan retirement or early retirement, and get a plan to rebalance towards a target mix.

### Hosting, privacy and phone apps

Sharesight is an online service with read-only iPhone and Android apps. Its team runs everything, and support is included.

Wealthfolio keeps your data on your server and has no analytics or tracking in the app. Self-hosted, it has one password for one person, and you look after updates and backups. Wealthfolio's iPhone and Android apps keep their own data, so with a self-hosted copy you open its address in your phone's browser instead.
