### Storyden and Circle pricing

As of November 2026, Circle's plans, in US dollars a month, billed yearly and before tax:

- **Professional**: $89, with a 2% transaction fee, 3 admins and 10 moderators.
- **Business**: $199, with a 1% fee, adding workflows, APIs and white-labelling.
- **Scale**: $419, with a 1% fee, adding AI agents, a knowledge engine and custom single sign-on.
- **Circle Plus**: priced by Circle's sales team, with a 0.5% fee and branded phone apps.

Every plan allows unlimited members, and Professional, Business and Scale have a 14-day free trial.

Storyden has no licence fee, no paid edition and no fee on payments. Your cost is the server, and a small one is enough: its docs say it idles at about 80 MB of memory. Its optional AI tools use your own OpenAI or Anthropic account, which that provider bills you for.

### A forum with a knowledge base

Storyden puts discussion and shared knowledge side by side. Members start threads in categories, reply, quote, tag and react. Next to the forum is a library: a tree of wiki-style pages with fields such as Status or Author, shown side by side in a table. Members can suggest new pages or edits, and moderators accept them before they go live. Shared links are saved and searchable, and threads and pages can be gathered into collections.

Circle is built around selling access. Next to discussion spaces it has courses, live events and streams, payments and a custom domain on every plan, with AI tools on top.

### Members, moderation and sign-in

Storyden has Admin, Member and Guest roles, or roles you create, each with its own permissions and badge. Moderators handle reports, give private warnings, keep staff notes, suspend accounts and block words. Members sign in with a password, a passkey, or a Google, GitHub or Discord account.

Circle gives 3 to 10 admins and 10 to 100 moderators depending on the plan, and adds AI moderation on Scale.

### Phone apps and maturity

Circle has iPhone, Android and desktop apps on every plan. Storyden has no phone app; it works in any browser with a layout made for small screens.

Storyden is a young project that is still changing, so check the release notes before each update. Circle is a managed service, with onboarding and priority support on its higher plans.
