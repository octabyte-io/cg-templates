### GPT Researcher and ChatGPT deep research pricing

As of November 2026, deep research comes with ChatGPT's plans, and OpenAI's pricing page gives each plan a different amount:

- **Free** ($0): limited deep research.
- **Go** ($8 a month): limited deep research.
- **Plus** ($20 a month): expanded deep research, with limits.
- **Pro** (plans at $100, $200 or $500 a month): maximum deep research.
- **Business** ($20 per user a month billed yearly, or $25 monthly) and **Enterprise**, which is priced by OpenAI's sales team.

GPT Researcher has no plan and no subscription. Each report costs only the AI and search usage it takes on your own keys. The project's figures, with OpenAI models, are about $0.12 for a standard report and about $0.40 for a Deep Research run. Search is free with DuckDuckGo, or Tavily has a free plan. You also pay for the server.

### Privacy and hosting

ChatGPT deep research runs in OpenAI's cloud, and your questions and reports live in your ChatGPT account. GPT Researcher runs on your own server, so questions and reports stay with you. Your questions and the pages it reads still go to the AI provider you connect, unless you use a local model.

### How the research works

Both read many sources and write a long report with citations. GPT Researcher splits your question into smaller research questions, reads 20 or more sources and writes a report of over 2,000 words. You choose a quick summary, a detailed report or Deep Research, which follows each lead further, and one of more than a dozen tones, such as objective or analytical. You can limit research to chosen websites.

ChatGPT deep research can search the public web or specific sites, use files you upload, and read connected apps such as Google Drive or SharePoint, depending on your plan.

### AI models and search engines

ChatGPT deep research uses OpenAI's models only. GPT Researcher uses OpenAI by default, and also works with Anthropic, Google Gemini, Azure OpenAI, Mistral, Groq and local models through Ollama. For search it supports Tavily, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Google and academic sources such as arXiv, and it can draw on MCP sources. The project says it is tested most on OpenAI's models.

### What ChatGPT has that GPT Researcher lacks

- **No setup.** ChatGPT works in the browser and its apps with nothing to install or configure.
- **Phone apps.** ChatGPT has mobile apps. GPT Researcher works in a browser only.
- **Connectors.** ChatGPT deep research can read your connected work apps.
- **Accounts.** ChatGPT gives each person an account. GPT Researcher has none of its own, so on Cloudgate everyone shares one sign-in.
