### ChiefOnboarding and Enboarder pricing

Enboarder shows no prices. As of November 2026, its pricing page says "We don't have one-size-fits-all pricing" and asks you to get a quote. It sells three packages, Intelligent Onboarding, AI-Assisted Employee Journeys and New Hire Enablement, with add-ons for US compliance and integrations.

ChiefOnboarding is free, with every feature and no limit on new hires. As of November 2026 its makers list:

- **Free**: €0, self-hosted, with community support on GitHub.
- **Support**: €249 a month, billed yearly, self-hosted with priority email support.
- **Managed hosting**: run for you by ChiefOnboarding, with the price on request.

Self-hosted, your cost is the server, a PostgreSQL database, S3-compatible storage if you want file uploads, and Twilio if you want text messages.

### Channels: Slack and email, or Teams and WhatsApp too

ChiefOnboarding gives each new hire a web portal and, if you use Slack, a Slack bot; both have every feature. It also sends email through your email service and text messages through Twilio. It does not work with Microsoft Teams, and Slack needs a Slack app you create yourself.

Enboarder reaches people by email, text, WhatsApp, Slack, Microsoft Teams and phone calls. Its journeys run in the mobile browser with no app to install and no login, which suits frontline staff without a work email, and they come in over 90 languages.

### Building the onboarding plan

Both start before day one and time each step around the start date. In ChiefOnboarding you build sequences of to-dos with forms, resources and short courses, introductions, badges and admin tasks for HR, IT or the buddy. Each new hire can have their own time zone, and offboarding works the same way.

Enboarder adds AI: a journey builder, an AI assistant that answers new hires' questions, and generated 30-60-90 day ramp plans. It sends e-signature documents through DocuSign, Adobe Sign or Dropbox Sign and tracks them.

### Integrations and reports

ChiefOnboarding's integrations import staff from BambooHR or Google, and create accounts in Google Workspace, Azure AD, Asana, Zendesk and other tools on the day you choose. You can add your own through its manifests and webhooks.

Enboarder connects to HR and applicant tracking systems, with its own connectors for Workday, SAP SuccessFactors and BambooHR, plus APIs, webhooks and SFTP. Its dashboards track ramp progress, attrition, time to productivity and no-show rate, where ChiefOnboarding shows each new hire's timeline.
