### TeamMapper and MindMeister pricing

As of November 2026, MindMeister's pricing page lists four plans, per user a month and excluding VAT, with prices that depend on your region:

- **Free**: up to 3 mind maps and 500 Meister AI credits.
- **Personal**: $6.50, with unlimited maps, file exports and 7 days of version history.
- **Pro**: $10.50, adding Microsoft Office exports, unlimited version history and several admins.
- **Business**: $15.50, with all-time history and SAML single sign-on through sales.

You can pay yearly or every six months, and MindMeister says this saves up to 18%.

TeamMapper has no licence fee, no paid edition and no limit on maps or people. You pay only for the server.

### Accounts or links

MindMeister gives each person an account and keeps the maps there, with team members and admins. TeamMapper has no accounts at all. You start a map and share either a view-only link or an edit link, by URL or QR code. That makes it fast for a room full of people, but anyone with the edit link can change the map, and there are no per-person permissions or comments.

### Editing together

Both let several people work on one map at once and see each other's changes live. In TeamMapper you add nodes with colours, bold or italic text, pictures and web links, with undo and redo and keyboard shortcuts. MindMeister adds comments, a presentation mode, templates, Meister AI to build or refine a map from a prompt or a document, and links to MeisterTask, Google Drive and Microsoft Teams.

### How long your maps are kept

This is the biggest difference. TeamMapper deletes a map nobody has changed for a set number of days, 30 by default, and you choose the number when you install it. It suits short-lived maps, not an archive. MindMeister keeps maps in your account, with version history that runs from 7 days on Personal to all-time on Business.

### Hosting and apps

MindMeister is a cloud service with iPhone and Android apps on every plan. TeamMapper runs on your own server, so maps stay with you, and works in any browser, including a phone's, in eight languages. It has no phone or desktop app.
