### WordPress and Wix pricing

As of November 2026, Wix has a free plan and four paid plans. Prices are per month in US dollars, billed yearly; monthly billing costs more:

- **Free**: a wixsite.com address, Wix branding, 500 MB of storage and no way to take payments.
- **Light**: $17 a month ($24 monthly), with 2 GB of storage and a free domain for the first year.
- **Core**: $29 a month ($36 monthly), with 50 GB, payments, basic eCommerce and bookings.
- **Business**: $39 a month ($46 monthly), with 100 GB and standard eCommerce.
- **Business Elite**: $159 a month ($172 monthly), with unlimited storage and advanced eCommerce.

Wix says prices and currency vary by location, and taxes are added on top.

Self-hosted WordPress has no licence fee and no plans. Your costs are the server, a domain name and any paid themes or plugins you choose. WordPress asks for PHP 8.3 or newer, MariaDB 10.11 or MySQL 8.4, and HTTPS; on Cloudgate a small server runs a typical business site.

### Which WordPress this is

WordPress comes in two forms. This page is about **self-hosted WordPress**: the free, open-source software from wordpress.org, which Cloudgate installs on your own server. **WordPress.com** is a separate hosted service with paid plans, and works more like Wix.

### Building and editing your site

Wix gives you a drag-and-drop editor, over 2,000 templates and an AI builder that creates a site from a prompt. Everything comes from Wix, including apps in its App Market.

WordPress uses a theme for the design and the block editor for pages and posts. The wordpress.org directory lists 8,792 free themes, and its plugin directory adds forms, bookings, newsletters, translations and search engine tools. Paid themes and plugins are sold elsewhere, and you can install any of them.

### Hosting, ownership and lock-in

Wix runs your site on its own servers, and its help centre says a Wix site cannot be hosted anywhere else. You can use your own domain name, but not move the site.

A self-hosted WordPress site is yours: its files and database can be copied to any host. The trade-off is upkeep. You keep WordPress, your theme and your plugins up to date, and sending email from the site needs an email plugin and a sending service.

### Shops and phone apps

On Wix, selling starts on the Core plan. On WordPress, the free WooCommerce plugin adds products, a cart and checkout, with payments through Stripe, PayPal and others.

Both have phone apps for iPhone and Android. The Wix app runs your dashboard, store, blog and bookings; the WordPress app lets you write and publish posts.
