### Easy!Appointments and Acuity Scheduling pricing

As of November 2026, Acuity Scheduling has three plans with prices, in US dollars before tax:

- **Starter**: $16 a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly, for 1 staff or location calendar.
- **Standard**: $27 a month billed yearly, or $34 monthly, for up to 6 calendars.
- **Premium**: $55 a month billed yearly, or $69 monthly, for up to 36 calendars.

Enterprise, with unlimited calendars, is priced by Acuity's sales team. Every plan has a 7-day trial with no card needed, and there is no free plan.

Easy!Appointments has no plans. It is free under the GPL-3.0 licence, with no limit on staff, services or bookings. Your cost is a small server and an email service. Booking pages show "Powered By Easy!Appointments" unless you buy a white-label licence from its maker.

### Booking and calendars

Both give clients a public booking page where they pick a service, a staff member, a day and a free time, without creating an account. In Easy!Appointments, staff work from one calendar that shows every appointment, break and day off, and roles split the work between admins, providers and secretaries.

Easy!Appointments syncs both ways with Google Calendar and other CalDAV calendars, and can add a Jitsi or Google Meet link. Acuity Scheduling connects to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and iCloud, Zoom, QuickBooks, Mailchimp and Zapier. Easy!Appointments can send each booking to n8n, Zapier or Make.

### Payments, reminders and forms

This is where Acuity Scheduling does more:

- **Payments** through Stripe, Square, PayPal or Venmo, on every plan.
- **Email reminders** and custom **intake forms** on every plan.
- **Text reminders**, a waitlist, packages, memberships and gift certificates from Standard.
- An AI booking assistant, a HIPAA business associate agreement and no Acuity branding on Premium.

The free Easy!Appointments app has none of these. It sends emails when a booking is made, changed or cancelled; its maker offers reminders, texts and payments only as paid custom work.

### Apps and hosting

Acuity Scheduling runs in Squarespace's cloud. Its app for iPhone and iPad lets the business manage staff calendars, bookings and payments on the go.

Easy!Appointments runs on your own server, so your clients' details stay with you. It works in any browser on a phone or computer, with no phone app.
