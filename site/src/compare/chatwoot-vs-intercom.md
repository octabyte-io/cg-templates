### Chatwoot and Intercom pricing

As of November 2026, Intercom has three plans, priced in US dollars per seat a month and billed yearly:

- **Essential**: $29 a seat, shown at $19 for new customers (35% off). Fin, the Messenger chat, a shared inbox with ticketing, reports and a public help centre.
- **Advanced**: $85 a seat. Adds multiple team inboxes, the workflow builder, round-robin assignment and 20 free Lite seats.
- **Expert**: $132 a seat. Adds single sign-on, HIPAA support, SLAs and multibrand help centres.

On top of seats, Fin costs $0.99 per outcome, WhatsApp and SMS are charged per message, and Copilot, an AI assistant for your agents, is $29 a seat a month billed yearly. Paying monthly costs more than paying yearly. There is a 14-day free trial.

Chatwoot Community Edition has no licence fee and no limit on agents. Your cost is the server: on Cloudgate, Chatwoot gets a medium server. Chatwoot also sells self-hosted Premium Support at $19 an agent a month (Captain AI, voice calls, custom branding) and an Enterprise Edition at $99 (adds SSO and SLA policies). Chatwoot Cloud starts free for 2 agents and runs from $19 to $99 an agent a month.

### Hosting and your data

Intercom runs only in its own cloud, in the US, the EU or Australia. Regional hosting in the EU or Australia is for new contracts on Advanced and Expert plans.

Chatwoot runs on a server you choose, so conversations, contacts and attachments stay with you. Messages still pass through the services you connect, such as WhatsApp or Facebook, and you look after the server's updates and backups.

### Shared inbox and channels

Both give your team one shared inbox. Chatwoot brings in website chat, email, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, LINE and SMS, and lets you assign conversations, leave private notes, use canned replies and labels, and track response times and satisfaction ratings.

Intercom includes live chat, support email and in-app chat on every plan, with ticketing in the inbox. WhatsApp, SMS and phone are paid per use.

### AI and automation: Fin and Captain

This is where Intercom leads. Fin is an AI agent that learns from your support content, answers across channels and hands over to a person, and you pay only when it resolves a conversation. Advanced and Expert add a visual workflow builder and SLAs.

Chatwoot Community Edition is built for support led by people. It has automation rules, assignment and canned replies, but no AI agent. Captain AI, Chatwoot's AI assistant, is in the paid self-hosted plans and Chatwoot Cloud.

### What Intercom does better

- **AI agent.** Fin answers customers first, on every plan.
- **Workflows.** A visual builder, SLAs and multiple team inboxes.
- **Outbound.** Product tours, surveys and in-app messages as add-ons.
- **Managed service.** No server to run, with regional data hosting.
