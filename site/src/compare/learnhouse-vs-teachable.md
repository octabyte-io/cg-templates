### LearnHouse and Teachable pricing

As of November 2026, Teachable has no free plan, only a 7-day free trial:

- **Starter**: $29 a month billed yearly, or $39 monthly, with up to 5 products and a 7.5% transaction fee.
- **Builder**: $69 a month yearly, or $89 monthly, with up to 10 products and 0% transaction fees with Teachable Payments.
- **Growth**: $139 a month yearly, or $189 monthly, with up to 50 products, certificates and no Teachable branding.
- **Custom**: priced by Teachable's sales team.

Card fees apply on top, from 2.9% + 30¢ for US cards.

LearnHouse Community Edition is free, with unlimited courses and members; you pay for the server. Its Enterprise Edition is a yearly licence for your own servers, with no price on the page. LearnHouse Cloud costs $564 a year (Standard), $1,716 (Pro) or $3,828 (Business), with unlimited members and 0% platform fees on payments.

### Selling or teaching

This is the main difference. Teachable is built to sell: checkout, global tax handling, upsells, cart recovery and affiliates, plus coaching, digital downloads and memberships.

LearnHouse is built to teach. Self-hosted, payments, discount codes, affiliates, analytics, single sign-on, SCORM and live sessions are all Enterprise features. The free edition suits free courses, school classes and staff training.

### Course building and assessment

LearnHouse writes lessons in a block editor with video, PDFs, quizzes, flip cards, maths and code. It has graded assignments marked automatically or by hand, learning paths, drip-fed chapters, discussion communities and certificates with a public page. AI help is optional, using your own AI provider.

Teachable has quizzes, drip content and certificates, which start on the Growth plan, and AI help for building courses.

### Phone apps, imports and running it

Teachable's free iPhone and Android student apps include offline downloads. LearnHouse Cloud lists a mobile app on its plans, but the self-hosted free edition has none; learners use the browser.

LearnHouse imports from Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard, Brightspace, Open edX and any Common Cartridge, and exports courses as zip files. Self-hosted, it needs at least 2 GB of memory, with 4 GB and 2 cores recommended, and you handle updates and backups.
