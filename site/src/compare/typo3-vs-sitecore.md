### TYPO3 and Sitecore pricing

As of November 2026, Sitecore shows no prices on its website. Its products, now led by the SitecoreAI platform, are sold through its sales team and partners: you request a demo and get a quote. Sitecore says AI features in SitecoreAI are included rather than sold as add-ons.

TYPO3 has no licence fee for any number of sites, editors or visitors. The costs are the server, which can start small with about 1 GB of memory, and the people: most organisations have an agency or developer build the design and content types, and some buy a support contract from an agency or TYPO3's paid extended support.

### Hosting and updates

TYPO3 runs on your own server, so your content and visitor data stay with you, and you plan updates. TYPO3 13 is a long-term support release with free updates until 31 December 2027, and paid extended support until 2030.

Sitecore's lead product, SitecoreAI, is a cloud SaaS service that Sitecore updates for you. Its older Sitecore XP and XM products can run in your own cloud or Sitecore's.

### Content, sites and languages

Both are built for large websites. TYPO3 organises every page in a tree, runs several sites and languages from one installation, and lets editor groups see and change only certain pages, content types and fields. Workspaces hold changes as drafts, show them beside the live page and send them through review stages. SEO fields, forms, redirects, a link checker and scheduled tasks are included, and extensions add news, events and more.

Sitecore offers a drag-and-drop page editor with live preview, unlimited sites and brands, AI translation of whole sites with review, approval workflows and brand guardrails, forms, and a digital asset library. Developers can use Next.js, Angular or .NET.

### Personalisation, testing and AI

This is where Sitecore goes further. Its editor includes point-and-click audience targeting, real-time personalisation from visitor signals such as location and behaviour, and A/B and multivariate tests. SitecoreAI also lists over 20 AI agents that draft, tag and translate content, plus AI search.

TYPO3 has none of these built in. A TYPO3 site that needs them looks to extensions or outside analytics and testing tools, which means more setup work and more parts to look after.
