### LimeSurvey and SurveyMonkey pricing

As of November 2026, SurveyMonkey's plans for one person are:

- **Basic**: free, with unlimited surveys, 10 questions per survey and a limited number of responses, and no data exports or skip logic.
- **Standard**: $149 a month billed monthly, with 1,000 responses a month, data exports and skip logic.
- **Advantage**: $468 billed yearly ($39 a month), with 15,000 responses a year.
- **Premier**: $1,668 billed yearly ($139 a month), with 40,000 responses a year, advanced logic, multilingual surveys and crosstabs.

Team plans start at 3 users and are billed yearly: Team Advantage at $30 and Team Premier at $92 per user a month, with Enterprise by quote. Paid plans charge $0.15 for each response over the limit.

LimeSurvey Community Edition has no licence fee and no limit on surveys, responses or admin accounts. Your cost is the server, and a small one is enough for most surveys, plus an email server for invitations. LimeSurvey's makers also sell a hosted LimeSurvey Cloud, from $39 a month billed monthly with 1,000 responses a month.

### Question types, logic and quotas

LimeSurvey has more than 30 question types, including arrays, dual scales, dates, file uploads and equations. Every survey can use skip logic, randomisation and quotas, which limit answers from groups of people and can close the survey for a group once its quota is full. Each survey can run in several languages at once.

SurveyMonkey keeps skip logic for Standard and up, and advanced logic, block randomisation and multilingual surveys for Premier.

### Inviting people and exporting results

LimeSurvey can send each person on a participant list a personal invitation link with reminders, or you share one public link or a QR code. It exports every answer to CSV, Excel, PDF, Word, HTML, SPSS, R and Stata.

SurveyMonkey sends surveys by link, email, text or social media, and its paid plans add exports, AI analysis, statistical significance and, on Premier, crosstabs.

### What SurveyMonkey has that LimeSurvey lacks

- **A respondent panel.** SurveyMonkey Audience lets you pay for answers from people who match your target group.
- **Phone apps.** SurveyMonkey has iPhone and Android apps for building, sending and checking surveys.
- **Ease of use.** Its builder and templates are quicker to learn than LimeSurvey's admin area.
- **A managed service.** SurveyMonkey runs the servers and sends the emails for you.
