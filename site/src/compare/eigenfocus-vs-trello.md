### Eigenfocus and Trello pricing

As of November 2026, Trello has four plans, in US dollars:

- **Free**: up to 10 collaborators and 10 boards per Workspace, files up to 10 MB and 250 automation runs a month.
- **Standard**: $5 per user a month billed yearly, or $6 billed monthly. Unlimited boards, custom fields and 1,000 automation runs a month.
- **Premium**: $10 per user a month billed yearly, or $12.50 billed monthly. Adds calendar, timeline, table, dashboard and map views and unlimited automation runs.
- **Enterprise**: $17.50 per user a month billed yearly ($210 a user a year), with organisation-wide controls and single sign-on.

The Eigenfocus free edition has no fee and no limit on projects or tasks. Its paid editions are bought once, not by subscription: **Essential** at $139 adds team invites, permissions, custom types and statuses and a REST API, and **PRO** at $489 adds Grid and Timeline views, custom fields, project templates and single sign-on. Eigenfocus Cloud starts at $15 a month for up to 5 people.

### Time tracking

This is the main reason to pick Eigenfocus. You start a timer from the task you are working on, or add time by hand. A weekly timesheet shows your hours for each day, and a time report totals them by project, label and date range and downloads as a CSV file.

Trello's plans do not include a time tracker. Teams that need one add a Power-Up, and some partner Power-Ups charge their own fee.

### One person or a team

The free edition of Eigenfocus has no user accounts. Anyone who signs in with the shared password works as the same person, so there are no assignments or private projects. That is fine for a freelancer and a poor fit for a team, which needs Essential or PRO.

Trello is made for sharing. Every plan has members, assignees, comments and an activity log, and the Free plan allows 10 collaborators per Workspace.

### Boards and views

Both put work on boards of columns you drag cards across, with labels, due dates, checklists, files and comments. Eigenfocus adds a list of every task in a project, starter layouts for things like a sales pipeline or bug tracking, and a Focus Space with a work-and-break timer.

Trello's Premium plan adds calendar, timeline, table, dashboard and map views. In Eigenfocus, timeline and grid views are in the PRO edition.

### Apps, automation and hosting

Trello has iPhone and Android apps on every plan, automation built into every board, and Power-Ups for calendars, integrations and more. Eigenfocus has no phone apps, automation or Power-Ups; it opens in a web browser, including on a phone.

Trello runs only in Atlassian's cloud. Eigenfocus runs on your own server, so your hours and files stay with you, and you look after its updates and backups.
