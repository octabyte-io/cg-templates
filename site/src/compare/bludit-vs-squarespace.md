### Bludit and Squarespace pricing

As of November 2026, Squarespace has four plans, shown in US dollars:

- **Basic**: $19 a month billed yearly ($228 a year) or $25 billed monthly, with 2 contributors and a 2% fee on online store sales.
- **Core**: $29 a month billed yearly ($348 a year) or $39 billed monthly, with unlimited contributors and no store transaction fee.
- **Plus**: $49 a month billed yearly ($588 a year) or $65 billed monthly.
- **Advanced**: $99 a month billed yearly ($1,188 a year) or $139 billed monthly.

Each plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and there is no free plan. Yearly plans include a free domain for the first year.

Bludit has no licence fee. Bludit PRO, four extra plugins for page history (Time Machine), one-click backups, galleries and white labelling, comes with any Patreon tier from $2. On Cloudgate, Bludit runs on the smallest server size.

### Hosting and how your site is stored

Squarespace runs only on its own hosting, and you pay the plan for as long as the site is online.

Bludit runs on a server you choose and needs only PHP 8.0 or later. It keeps your pages, users and settings as JSON files instead of in a database, so the site is light and a backup is a copy of its folder. You look after updates and backups yourself, where Squarespace does that for you.

### Editing and design

Squarespace starts from a designed template. Its Fluid Engine editor lets you drag blocks into place and resize them on a grid, with a separate layout for phones.

Bludit is simpler. You write pages and posts in a visual editor with a formatting toolbar, much like a word processor, or switch to Markdown. You can save drafts, schedule posts and pin one to the top of the home page. The layout comes from the theme: Bludit bundles four, and there is no drag-and-drop builder.

### What Squarespace has that Bludit lacks

- **A shop.** Squarespace sells products, digital content and memberships on its plans. Bludit has no shop.
- **Templates and drag-and-drop pages.** Bludit has far fewer themes and no page builder.
- **A phone app.** The Squarespace app for iPhone and Android edits site copy, uploads photos and handles orders and payments. Bludit has no app.
- **Backups and page history included.** In Bludit these are PRO plugins for Patreon supporters.
