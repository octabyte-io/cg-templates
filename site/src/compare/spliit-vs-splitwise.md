### Spliit and Splitwise pricing

As of November 2026, Splitwise is free on iPhone, Android and the web, with a paid upgrade called Splitwise Pro. Splitwise's Pro page lists what Pro adds:

- Unlimited expenses each day, so free accounts have a daily limit
- Card transaction import (US only)
- Currency conversion at current exchange rates
- Charts and graphs, and expense search
- Receipt scanning and itemization
- Default split settings for a group
- Early access to new features and no ads

Neither the Pro page nor the subscribe page shows a price, so check the app before you subscribe.

Spliit is free, with no paid edition, no ads and no limit on groups or expenses. Its makers describe it as "Open Source. Forever Free." Your cost is the server; Spliit is one light web app and a database, so a small server is enough. Its official instance at spliit.app is free too, paid for by donations.

### Accounts or a group link

Splitwise gives everyone an account. You add friends and groups, and Splitwise keeps a balance with each friend across all your groups.

Spliit has no accounts. You create a group, send its link, and whoever opens it picks which participant they are and can add or edit expenses. It is quick for a trip or a shared flat, but share each link only with the people in that group. In a self-hosted Spliit, anyone who knows your address can also create new groups.

### Splitting, settling and tracking

Both tools cover the core job: log who paid, split it and see who owes whom.

- **Spliit** splits evenly, by shares, by percentage or by exact amounts. Balances show the fewest payments to settle up, with a "Mark as paid" button. Each expense has a date, category and notes, rent and bills can repeat daily, weekly or monthly, and the stats tab shows total spending over time. Search and currency conversion are included, and you can export a group as CSV or JSON.
- **Splitwise** splits equally, unequally, by percentage or by shares, simplifies debts and records cash or online payments. It has recurring expenses, categories, offline mode and over 100 currencies. Search, charts and conversion are part of Pro.

Spliit can attach receipt photos and read them with AI, but that needs separate file storage and an AI provider key, so the Cloudgate template leaves it off.

### Phone apps and hosting

Splitwise has official apps for iPhone and Android and runs only as its own service.

Spliit works in any browser and installs on a phone's home screen. Its official iPhone app, for iOS 26 or later, can open groups on your own Spliit next to groups on spliit.app. There is no Android app. Self-hosted, your group's spending stays on your own server.
