### Collabora Online and Microsoft 365 pricing

As of November 2026, Microsoft lists these prices per user a month in US dollars, in effect from July 1, 2026. Prices vary by country and currency.

- **Business Basic**: $7, with Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook on the web, Teams, a 50 GB mailbox and 1 TB of OneDrive per user.
- **Business Standard**: $14, adding the desktop apps.
- **Business Premium**: $22, adding protection against phishing and ransomware and device management.

Collabora Online has three options:

- **CODE**: free, for home use, testing and small teams, with rolling updates.
- **Collabora Online for Business**: €3, £2.60 or $3.40 per user a month for up to 99 users, adding long-term support, signed security updates and a service agreement.
- **Collabora Online for Enterprise**: for 100 users or more, priced by Collabora's sales team.

With CODE, your cost is the server. You also need a file app such as Nextcloud or Seafile, which is free.

### Editors, not a whole suite

Microsoft 365 bundles the Office apps with Outlook email, Teams chat and meetings, and OneDrive storage. Collabora Online is only the editors: documents, spreadsheets, presentations and drawings. It has no accounts or storage of its own, and people open files from Nextcloud or Seafile with their usual login.

Inside those editors, several people work on one file at once, with comments and tracked changes. Spreadsheets have formulas, pivot tables, filters, charts and protected cells, and slides can be presented from the browser with speaker notes.

### Office file formats

Collabora Online is built on LibreOffice. It saves .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files in the same formats, so colleagues on Microsoft 365 can keep working on them. It also opens OpenDocument files and Visio diagrams. Complex Office files can look slightly different from how they look in Microsoft Office, so check important layouts after moving.

### Hosting and devices

Microsoft 365 runs in Microsoft's cloud. Business Standard and Premium also install the desktop apps on up to five PCs or Macs, five tablets and five phones per user.

Collabora Online runs on your own server and needs about 2 GB of memory. It edits in any modern browser, including a phone's. CODE has no desktop app; Collabora's Enterprise plan adds a desktop version and mobile support. The trade-off is that you look after the server and its updates, and that Collabora says CODE is not recommended for production; the free edition also shows a welcome screen after updates and asks for feedback now and then.
