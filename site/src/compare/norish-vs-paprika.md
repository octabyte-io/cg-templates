### Norish and Paprika pricing

As of November 2026, Paprika 4 is free to download on every platform, and one plan covers all your devices:

- **Free**: up to 50 recipes, one grocery list and one cookbook, with no cloud sync.
- **Standard**: free for anyone who bought an earlier Paprika, with unlimited recipes and cloud sync.
- **Premium**: $14.99 a year at its introductory price in the US, or $9.99 a year for earlier buyers, with a one-week free trial. Prices vary by region.

Premium adds family accounts, recipe scanning, social media import, nutrition and calendar feeds.

Norish has no plans and no recipe limit. Your cost is the server, plus an AI provider only if you import from videos, photos or text. Norish needs a database and a browser service for reading recipe pages, so on Cloudgate it gets a medium server.

### Live sharing for the household

Norish is built around one shared kitchen. When one person adds a recipe, plans a dinner or ticks off milk, everyone else sees it straight away. The grocery list can skip what is already in your pantry, and the meal calendar can show up in your own calendar app.

In Paprika, sharing one collection needs Premium. A family account holds up to six people, who share the owner's recipe collection and subscription, and changes sync between them.

### Apps and offline use

Paprika has apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows, and an Apple Watch app, and they work offline.

Norish has no app in the App Store or Google Play. It is built for the phone's browser and works offline there, syncing your changes when you reconnect. Add it to the home screen and it opens like an app.

### Importing and cooking

Both save recipes from a website's address, scale servings and have a cook mode that keeps the screen on. Norish also switches between US and metric in one tap, shows each step with its own ingredients and timers, and, with an AI provider you connect, turns YouTube, Instagram or TikTok videos, photos of cookbook pages or pasted text into recipes.

Paprika's Premium plan scans cookbook pages and imports from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube without a provider of your own. Paprika also has pantry expiry dates, menus, voice commands in cook mode, Alexa and nutrition from USDA data.

### Beta or mature

Norish is young, and every release so far is labelled beta. It works day to day, but features change, so keep backups. Paprika has been sold for years, and Paprika 4 keeps all your Paprika 3 data.
