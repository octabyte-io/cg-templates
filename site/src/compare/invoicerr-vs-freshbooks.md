### Invoicerr and FreshBooks pricing

As of November 2026, FreshBooks has four plans, shown in US dollars a month:

- **Lite**: $23, for up to 5 billable clients.
- **Plus**: $43, for up to 50 billable clients, adding proposals, retainers, double-entry accounting reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access.
- **Premium**: $70, with unlimited clients and project profitability.
- **Select**: priced on request, with two team accounts included and lower card and ACH fees.

Each extra team member costs $11 a month on any plan. FreshBooks often shows a discount for the first months and offers 10% off when you pay yearly. There is a 30-day free trial.

Invoicerr has no licence fee, no plan and no limit on clients, invoices or users. Your cost is the server it runs on and the mail service it sends through.

### Quotes, invoices and e-invoices

Both tools cover the everyday work of billing clients. Invoicerr sends a quote by email; the client opens the link, confirms an 8-digit code and signs, with no account. A signed quote becomes an invoice in one click. Invoicerr also has recurring invoices, partial payments with PDF receipts, revenue statistics and branded PDFs.

Its clearest lead is e-invoicing. Any Invoicerr invoice downloads as Factur-X, ZUGFeRD, XRechnung, UBL or CII, which matters for businesses in France and Germany. FreshBooks has estimates with deposit requests, recurring invoices, automatic payment reminders and late fees.

### What FreshBooks has that Invoicerr lacks

- **Online payments.** FreshBooks clients can pay by card, ACH, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Invoicerr shows your payment details, and you record each payment.
- **Time tracking and projects.** FreshBooks includes both on every plan.
- **Accounting.** Plus and higher add double-entry reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access.
- **Phone apps.** FreshBooks has iPhone and Android apps. Invoicerr runs in a phone's browser.
- **Support.** FreshBooks offers phone and email support. Invoicerr is built mostly by one maker who now works on it part-time, so changes come slowly. A version 2 with more features is in alpha testing, but Cloudgate installs the stable 1.4 line.

### Hosting and your data

FreshBooks runs only in its own cloud, so it looks after servers, updates and backups. Invoicerr runs on a server you choose, so your clients, quotes and invoices stay with you. In return, you keep the app updated and backed up, and you add a mail server so it can send documents.
