### KitchenOwl and AnyList pricing

As of November 2026, AnyList's apps are free, and the free plan covers creating and sharing lists. AnyList Complete is billed yearly:

- **Individual**: $9.99 a year.
- **Household**: $14.99 a year.

Complete adds meal planning, unlimited recipe imports from websites (the free plan allows five), recipe photos, item prices, list folders, premium themes, cloud backup of photos and the meal plan, and priority support.

KitchenOwl has no plans: the meal plan, recipes, expenses and apps are all free. Your cost is the small server it runs on.

### Shared grocery lists

Both put the shared list first. In AnyList and in KitchenOwl, everyone in the household adds and ticks off items, and the list updates for everyone straight away. AnyList groups items by category, and you can add them with Siri. KitchenOwl lets you keep a list for each shop, gives each item an icon, amount and category, and learns the order you usually tick things off.

### Meal plans and recipes

This is the main price difference. AnyList's meal planning is only in AnyList Complete, where it has a calendar, a queue for recipes you plan to cook soon, ideas from your own collection and reusable templates, and it can show the plan in Google Calendar or other calendar apps.

KitchenOwl includes a weekly meal plan for free, with recent and suggested dishes to choose from. Both save recipes from a web address and add their ingredients to the list in one go, though AnyList's free plan stops at five web imports.

### Apps

AnyList has apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Android, a web app, and browser extensions for saving recipes. KitchenOwl has official apps in the App Store, Google Play and F-Droid, desktop builds and a web app, all connected to your own server. KitchenOwl's offline support is partial: the list stays on hand when you lose signal.

### Shared costs and maturity

KitchenOwl also keeps the household's shared spending: after a shop, enter what it cost and split it between household members, with a balance for each person and a monthly overview by category. AnyList Complete tracks item prices and a running total as you shop, and lets you compare prices between stores.

AnyList is mature software. KitchenOwl says it is still in development, so expect occasional rough edges.
