### WiseMapping and MindMeister pricing

As of November 2026, MindMeister's pricing page lists four plans, per user a month and excluding VAT, with prices that depend on your region:

- **Free**: up to 3 mind maps and 500 Meister AI credits.
- **Personal**: $6.50, with unlimited maps, file exports and 7 days of version history.
- **Pro**: $10.50, adding Microsoft Office exports, unlimited version history and several admins.
- **Business**: $15.50, with all-time history and SAML single sign-on through sales.

You can pay yearly or every six months, and MindMeister says this saves up to 18%.

WiseMapping has no paid plan. Every feature is included, with no limit on maps or users, and you pay only for the server.

### Editing together

MindMeister is built for live teamwork: the whole team works on the same map at once, leaves comments and sees who changed what. WiseMapping lets you share each map with colleagues as owner, editor or viewer, but locks a map while one person edits it, so changes are never overwritten and nobody edits side by side. For a team that maps alone and shares the result, the lock rarely gets in the way; for a live brainstorm with several people typing at once, MindMeister fits better.

### Mapping features

Both draw mind maps in the browser. WiseMapping has seven themes, light and dark mode, notes, links, icons, emoji and images on topics, arrows between ideas, an outline view and find-in-map, in 13 languages. Maps can be made public and embedded in a website.

MindMeister adds a presentation mode, Meister AI to build a map from a prompt or an uploaded document, and links to MeisterTask, Google Drive and Microsoft Teams.

### Files in and out

WiseMapping opens XMind, MindManager, FreeMind, Freeplane and OPML files and exports to PDF, images, Markdown, text, FreeMind and Freeplane on every map. On MindMeister, the free plan has no file exports, Personal adds them, and Pro adds Microsoft Office exports.

### Hosting and apps

MindMeister is a cloud service with iPhone and Android apps on every plan. WiseMapping runs on your own server with a database, and works in any browser, including a phone's, but has no phone app. The same code runs WiseMapping's free hosted service at wisemapping.com.
