### Libredesk and Help Scout pricing

As of November 2026, Help Scout has four plans. The prices below are the ones its pricing page shows with annual billing, which it says saves 16%:

- **Free**: 5 users, 1 inbox and 1 Docs site, with 10 saved replies and 30 days of reports.
- **Standard**: $25 per user a month, with multiple inboxes, live chat and SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, one basic SLA policy and basic workflows.
- **Plus**: $45 per user a month, adding advanced workflows, two advanced SLA policies, a customer portal and round-robin routing.
- **Pro**: $75 per user a month, adding unlimited workflows and SLA policies, single sign-on and HIPAA compliance.

The Envoy AI agent is $0.75 per resolution, and extra inboxes cost $10 a month on annual billing.

Libredesk is free, with no paid tiers or add-ons; its makers say every feature is included. Your cost is a small server, plus your AI provider's bill if you turn on AI.

### Channels and the shared inbox

Both give your team one shared inbox where conversations are assigned, tagged and answered, with private notes for colleagues.

Libredesk brings email, a live chat widget for your website and WhatsApp (through a Meta business account) into that inbox in its free app. You assign conversations by hand, by rules or by each agent's capacity, and use saved replies (macros), custom statuses and snoozing. Help Scout covers email on every plan and adds live chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger from Standard.

### SLAs, ratings and reports

Libredesk sets response-time targets and warns you before one is missed, sends a satisfaction survey when a conversation closes, and reports on response and resolution times, ratings and agent activity. Help Scout gives one basic SLA policy on Standard and more on Plus and Pro, and keeps reports for 30 days on Free, 2 years on Standard and all time on Plus and Pro.

### Help center and AI

Help Scout's Docs knowledge base comes with every plan. Libredesk's help center is a searchable, multilingual knowledge base, still marked beta.

Help Scout includes AI drafts and summaries on every plan. In Libredesk, AI is optional and in beta: connect a provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic or a local Ollama model to draft replies, summarize conversations and let an assistant answer chat from your help center.

### Apps, integrations and hosting

Help Scout is a managed cloud service with free iPhone and Android apps and a directory of over 100 integrations. Libredesk runs on your own server with a PostgreSQL database, so conversations stay with you and you look after updates and backups. It works in any web browser and has no phone apps.
