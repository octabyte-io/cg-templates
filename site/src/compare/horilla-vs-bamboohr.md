### Horilla and BambooHR pricing

As of November 2026, BambooHR has three plans, priced per employee a month for companies with more than 25 employees:

- **Core**: $10 per employee a month (the page's FAQ says from $10.25), with employee records, hiring and onboarding, time off, reports and workflows.
- **Pro**: $17 per employee a month (from $17.50 in the FAQ), adding 360° reviews, one-to-ones and recognition.
- **Elite**: $25 per employee a month, adding compensation planning and salary benchmarks.

Companies with 25 or fewer employees pay a flat $250, $425 or $650 a month. Payroll, Benefits Administration and Time & Attendance are add-ons with no prices on the page.

Horilla's self-hosted edition is $0, with unlimited users and companies. Your cost is the server. If you would rather not run one, Horilla Cloud is $7 per user a month, billed monthly, and Horilla quotes Enterprise and support plans on request.

### Payroll: payslips or US payroll

Horilla's payroll module is in the free edition. You set contracts, allowances, deductions and tax rules, then generate payslips each month, with loans, reimbursements and leave encashment. It calculates pay; your bank and accountant still pay staff and file taxes.

BambooHR's Payroll add-on runs direct deposit and federal and state tax filing, and its Benefits Administration add-on handles open enrolment. Both are for US employees only.

### HR features side by side

Both tools keep employee records, handle time off with approvals and balances, and run hiring with job postings and candidates. Horilla also includes, at no extra cost:

- **Attendance and shifts**: check-in and check-out, overtime approval and punches from biometric devices. BambooHR sells shift scheduling and time clocks as an add-on.
- **Onboarding and offboarding**: checklists for each new starter and each leaver.
- **Assets and a help desk**: track the equipment each person holds and answer HR questions as tickets.
- **Performance**: objectives and key results, feedback and one-to-ones. BambooHR keeps 360° reviews and one-to-ones for Pro and above.

### Hosting, setup and support

BambooHR is a cloud service with nothing to install, free iPhone and Android apps, and a marketplace of over 125 partner integrations.

Horilla runs on your own server, so staff records and salaries stay with you, and you look after updates and backups. It has many modules and settings, so plan time to set up your company before staff use it, and its screens are busier than BambooHR's. Help comes from the community forum unless you pay Horilla for support. It connects to your company directory (LDAP) and has a free Android and iPhone app.
