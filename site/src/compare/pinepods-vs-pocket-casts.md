### PinePods and Pocket Casts pricing

As of November 2026, the Pocket Casts apps are free on phones, the web and the desktop. Since March 2025, the web player and the Mac and Windows apps need no subscription, and a free account syncs your progress, queue and subscriptions.

**Pocket Casts Plus** costs $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year in US dollars for new subscribers, before local taxes. It adds folders, Smart Shuffle, bookmarks, Apple Watch and Wear OS apps, 20 GB of cloud storage for your own files, and extra themes and icons. A higher Patron plan adds more.

PinePods has no paid tier, and its phone apps are free. Your cost is the server. PinePods uses little memory, so a small one is enough; plan the disk for the episodes you download.

### Where your listening lives

Pocket Casts keeps your subscriptions, queue and history in your Pocket Casts account, on its servers. PinePods keeps them on your own server, and each person in a household or team gets their own library, queue, settings and stats. The trade-off is that you look after the server and its backups.

### Apps on every device

Both have apps nearly everywhere. PinePods runs in the browser, as desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux, and as free iPhone and Android apps with CarPlay and Android Auto. Your place in an episode follows you between them.

Pocket Casts covers more places: iPhone and iPad, Android, the web, Windows and Mac, Apple TV and Android TV, Android Automotive, Sonos and Alexa, with Apple Watch and Wear OS in Plus.

### Listening features

PinePods has a queue, saved and favourite episodes, smart playlists that fill themselves, chapters, transcripts, audio and video episodes and listening stats. Its built-in gPodder sync server keeps AntennaPod, Kasts and other gPodder apps in step.

Pocket Casts has folders, bookmarks and Smart Shuffle in Plus, and lets you cast to Chromecast from its phone apps.

### Finding new shows

Pocket Casts has search and human-curated recommendations. PinePods searches Podcast Index and iTunes, using the PinePods project's public search service, but has no curated charts or editor picks.
