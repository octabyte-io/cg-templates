### Drawnix and XMind pricing

As of November 2026, XMind has three plans on its pricing page:

- **Free**: up to 10 online maps and 5 slides in Pitch, 3 days of version history and basic themes.
- **Pro**: $4.92 a month, up to 50 online maps with basic collaboration, attachments, custom themes and exports to PNG, SVG, PDF, Markdown and Office formats without a watermark.
- **Premium**: $8.25 a month, unlimited collaborative maps, real-time collaboration, Gantt charts, tasks and 30 days of version history.

Those prices are in US dollars and shown with the yearly option, which XMind says saves up to 50% against paying monthly. One subscription covers up to 5 computers and 5 phones or tablets.

Drawnix has no plans and no fee. It is free under the MIT licence, and your only cost is a very small server.

### Mind-map features

XMind is built for mind mapping. Besides mind maps, it offers timelines, matrices and Zones, a choice of themes, a Pitch mode that turns a map into slides, a Zen mode for focus, and AI that builds a map from a sentence, a file or a link.

Drawnix is a whiteboard that makes mind maps. You get a mind map with coloured branches on an endless board, next to flowcharts, shapes, freehand drawing, text and images. It has no outline view, presentation mode or templates library.

### Starting from text

Both tools can start from an outline. Drawnix turns a pasted Markdown list into a mind map and Mermaid code into a flowchart you can keep editing. XMind links to converters for Markdown, Doc and OPML, and its AI can draft a map from your words.

### Apps, files and collaboration

XMind runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android, plus a web app. It keeps files on your device, and its paid plans add cloud maps you can work on together; Premium has real-time collaboration.

Drawnix runs in any browser, including on a tablet. Each browser keeps one board, saved as you work, and nothing is stored on the server. Save File keeps a .drawnix copy; you can also export a PNG, JPG or SVG picture. Only one person works on a board, and it cannot open .xmind files.

### Hosting and privacy

Drawnix runs on your own server, with no sign-in, and your boards stay in your browser. XMind is a product you install or use on the web, with an account for its online maps.
