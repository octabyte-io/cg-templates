### HortusFox and Planta Premium pricing

As of November 2026, Planta's own Premium page shows no prices. Planta is free to download with limited access, and Premium is bought in the app as a monthly, three-month or yearly subscription. Prices vary by region. On the US App Store, Planta lists its Premium Plant Care subscriptions from $7.99 for one month, $17.99 to $19.99 for three months and $17.99 to $47.99 for twelve months, without saying which offer applies to whom.

HortusFox has no plans and no prices. It is free and open source under the MIT licence. Your cost is the server; on Cloudgate, HortusFox runs on a small one. Plant identification and the weather each use a free key from Pl@ntNet or OpenWeatherMap that you add yourself.

### Care schedules and reminders

This is the biggest difference. Planta builds a care schedule for each plant. Planta says its watering reminders use over 30 parameters, along with local climate and weather data, and the free version includes a watering schedule. Planta's help centre says fertilising and other care schedules, Dr. Planta, plant identification, the light meter and recommendations come with Premium.

HortusFox does not suggest a schedule. You note when each plant was watered, repotted or fertilised, its light, humidity and health, and you create your own tasks with due dates. Overdue tasks always show on the dashboard. Email reminders also need an email account for sending and a scheduled job, which take extra setup.

### Sharing with your household

Planta's Care Share lets you share care schedules with family and friends.

HortusFox is built for sharing. Everyone in the household signs in to the same workspace and sees the same plants, tasks, calendar and supplies. The admin decides who else is an admin, and a group chat helps you agree who waters what. The dashboard shows which plants are sick or have pests, and which locations have a plant in danger.

### Phone apps and your data

Planta is a phone app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and your plants are kept in Planta's account. HortusFox runs in any browser and can be added to a phone's home screen, but has no app-store app. It runs on your own server, so your photos and notes stay with you, and it can export backups of your plants, tasks, inventory and calendar. You look after the server and its updates.
