### Ghostfolio and Sharesight pricing

As of November 2026, Sharesight's pricing page shows four plans in US dollars:

- **Free**: 1 portfolio, 10 holdings (closed ones count), 1 custom group and basic support.
- **Starter**: $7 a month billed yearly, or $9.33 monthly, for 1 portfolio and 30 holdings.
- **Standard**: $18 a month billed yearly, or $24 monthly, for 4 portfolios and unlimited holdings.
- **Premium**: $23.25 a month billed yearly, or $31 monthly, for 10 portfolios and unlimited holdings.

Higher plans add reports such as future income, contribution analysis and multi-period reports, labels and the Xero integration. A Business plan for professionals is priced on request.

Self-hosted Ghostfolio has no licence fee and no limit on holdings or portfolios. Your cost is the server. Ghostfolio also sells Premium, a hosted service with professional market data, but the self-hosted app does not need it.

### Getting your trades in

This is the biggest practical difference. Sharesight imports trades from more than 200 brokers, reads trade confirmation emails, takes spreadsheet files and adds dividends, reinvestment plans and share splits for you.

Ghostfolio has no link to your broker or bank. You enter each buy, sell and dividend, or import a CSV or JSON file in Ghostfolio's own format. Prices come free from Yahoo Finance and CoinGecko, and you can enter your own for anything they miss.

### Tax reports

Sharesight works out capital gains and taxable income to local tax rules, such as Australia's ATO and Canada's CRA, and you can share reports with your accountant. Ghostfolio makes no tax reports, so it suits tracking and planning rather than the tax return itself.

### Analysis and planning

Both show performance with dividends included and break your money down by asset, currency and region. Ghostfolio adds benchmarks such as the S&P 500, an X-ray check that flags risks like too much in one holding, and a FIRE calculator. Sharesight keeps diversity, exposure and drawdown reports for its paid plans.

### Hosting and phone apps

Sharesight is an online service: nothing to install, and its team runs it. Its iPhone and Android apps are read-only, so you add trades on the website.

Ghostfolio runs on a server you choose, so your portfolio stays with you, and you look after updates and backups. It has no app store app but installs on a phone's home screen from the browser, with a Zen mode that hides amounts.
