### GlobaLeaks and EthicsPoint pricing

As of November 2026, NAVEX's main pricing page shows no prices: it says pricing "is tailored to your organization" and asks you to request a quote, with up to 30% off when you bundle NAVEX products. A separate NAVEX page lists **EthicsPoint Essentials** from $1,980 a year, for businesses with 250 employees or fewer. Essentials includes web and phone reporting around the clock, simple case management, preconfigured workflows, standard dashboards and US-based support.

GlobaLeaks has no licence fee and no paid edition, whatever the number of staff or reports. Your cost is the server: the project asks for 2 processor cores, 1 GB of memory and 20 GB of disk. You also do the setup, updates and backups that NAVEX does for EthicsPoint.

### How people report

EthicsPoint takes reports by web, phone and mobile, 24 hours a day, named or anonymous, from staff, contractors and suppliers. Its Nira for Intake assistant talks with reporters in their own language, with a live agent when needed.

GlobaLeaks takes reports through a web form only. Reporters answer your questionnaire, attach files and choose whether to give their name. Afterwards they get a 16-digit access code to read your questions, reply and add evidence, with no account. GlobaLeaks does not log IP addresses and suggests the Tor Browser for extra protection.

### Handling cases

GlobaLeaks gives recipients a list of reports with labels, statuses, reminders and expiry dates, search and export. A custodian role decides who may see a reporter's identity, conflict-of-interest settings keep a report away from the person it names, and retention rules delete old reports.

EthicsPoint goes further for large teams: configurable workflows, automatic assignment, approvals and escalations, bulk reassignment, role-based access and a log of who viewed or edited each case. Its full product adds AI help with investigations, Power BI dashboards, incident benchmarks and links to HR, risk and policy systems.

### Hosting and security

EthicsPoint is hosted by NAVEX, which looks after the servers, encryption and support. GlobaLeaks runs on a server you choose, so the most sensitive reports in the organisation stay with you. Reports are stored encrypted, and the project publishes its security audits, run since 2011, in full.

### Size and limits

GlobaLeaks suits small and mid-sized organisations: its spam protection accepts only a few new reports an hour, and every screen keeps a small "Powered by GlobaLeaks" line. The project says more than 30,000 organisations use it, from media to public agencies and companies. EthicsPoint is built for compliance teams of any size, with Essentials aimed at smaller businesses.
