### Tymeslot and Calendly pricing

As of November 2026, Calendly has four plans, priced in US dollars per seat a month:

- **Free**: one event type, one connected calendar and one-on-one meetings.
- **Standard**: $10 a seat a month billed yearly, or $12 billed monthly. Unlimited event types, reminders, payments and removing Calendly's branding.
- **Teams**: $16 a seat a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly. Adds round-robin, routing forms and Salesforce.
- **Enterprise**: from $15,000 a year, for 50 seats or more, with single sign-on and phone support.

Self-hosted Tymeslot has no licence fee and no paywall. Your cost is the server, and on Cloudgate Tymeslot gets a small server with 1 GB of memory, plus the email sending service you choose at setup.

Tymeslot also has its own cloud, priced in euros. Cloud Free costs €0 with unlimited bookings and a small Tymeslot footer on booking pages. Cloud Pro costs €9 a month or €90 a year and adds group bookings, payments (with a 0.5% platform fee), custom questions and removing the branding. Enterprise has no public price.

### One person or a team

This is the main difference. Tymeslot is built for one person's booking pages: a coach, a consultant or a therapist. It has no round-robin or collective team events.

Calendly covers both. One person can use the Free plan, group events with several hosts start on Standard, and round-robin, routing forms and admin-managed team events are on Teams.

### Booking pages, polls and approvals

Tymeslot's booking pages come in four themes in your own colours, and guests book in their own language and time zone with no account. You can keep several named schedules with breaks and buffers, hold a booking until you approve it, let several guests book one slot, and run a poll so a group can vote on a time. Confirmations and reminders carry a link to cancel or reschedule.

Calendly has meeting polls and one-off meetings on every plan, and adds reminders, cancellation policies, custom confirmation pages and payments from Standard.

### Calendars and video

Tymeslot syncs both ways with Google Calendar, Outlook and Microsoft 365, Exchange, iCloud and any CalDAV calendar such as Nextcloud. It creates rooms on Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Jitsi, MiroTalk, kMeet and Nextcloud Talk, and webhooks or Slack and Telegram messages tell your other tools about new bookings.

Calendly connects Google, Outlook and Office 365 calendars and Zoom, Google Meet and Teams with no setup, and has webhooks on every plan. Paid plans add HubSpot, Mailchimp and Zapier; Teams adds Salesforce.

### What Calendly does better

- **Team scheduling.** Group events, round-robin and lead routing.
- **Setup.** No server, email service or app key to arrange.
- **Phone app.** Calendly's mobile app is on every plan.
- **Support.** 24/7 email support on every plan, and chat support from Standard.
