### Habitica and Habitify pricing

As of November 2026, Habitify has two plans:

- **Free**: up to 3 habits, with sync across your devices.
- **Premium**: $2.49 a month billed yearly, or $59.99 once for a lifetime licence. It adds unlimited habits, advanced reminders (habit stacking and location-based), an off mode for breaks, Apple and Google calendar links, Apple Health, and an API with Zapier and IFTTT.

Self-hosted Habitica has no fee. The community build that Cloudgate installs gives every account a subscription that never needs renewing, lets you buy gems with gold instead of money, and lets anyone create a Group Plan. Your cost is the server: on Cloudgate, Habitica runs on a medium server with its database.

### A game or a tracker

Habitica works like a role-playing game. You have three kinds of tasks: Habits you tap plus or minus on, Dailies on your own schedule, and To-Dos with checklists. Finishing them earns your pixel-art avatar experience and gold. Missed dailies and bad habits cost it health. You spend gold on gear or on rewards you set yourself, pick a class at level 10, and raise pets into mounts.

Habitify is a straightforward habit tracker. It shows streaks, a calendar of your history and a yearly completion view, and it groups habits into morning, noon and night. It adds mood tracking, a built-in timer and notes for reflection.

### Families, friends and groups

This is where Habitica stands out. Friends form a party of up to 30 people, chat, and beat a quest boss by doing their own tasks. A Group Plan gives a household or team one shared board, with chores assigned to one person, several people or whoever does it first. On your own server, Group Plans are free.

Habitify has challenges with friends and monthly challenges with leaderboards, but it is built around one person's habits.

### Apps and devices

Habitify has apps for iPhone, Android, Mac and Apple Watch, and a web app, all synced through Habitify's cloud. It links with Apple Health and Google Fit.

Self-hosted Habitica runs in any web browser, including on a phone. Habitica's official phone apps are built for habitica.com, so we cannot confirm they work with your own server.

### Your data

Self-hosted Habitica keeps your tasks, parties and chat on your server, under the GPL-3.0 licence, and you can export your data at any time. It is a community build made by independent volunteers, not by Habitica's makers, and it is separate from habitica.com accounts.

Habitify keeps your data in its cloud, behind an optional privacy lock, and lets you export it to CSV or a full backup file.
