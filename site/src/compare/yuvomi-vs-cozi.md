### Yuvomi and Cozi pricing

As of November 2026, Cozi has three plans, priced in US dollars:

- **Free**: the shared calendar, to-do and shopping lists and the recipe box, with ads.
- **Cozi Gold**: $39 a year. No ads, month view on phones, calendar search, up to three reminders per event, notifications when the calendar changes, a birthday tracker, shopping mode and priority email support.
- **Cozi Max**: $79 a year. Everything in Gold, plus AI tools that turn emails into calendar events, create recipes and plan meals.

One subscription covers the whole family on every device, and subscriptions started on Cozi's website begin with a 2-week free trial.

Yuvomi has no price and no paid tier. Your cost is the server, and a small one is enough: its makers state 256 MB of memory as the minimum, and it runs as one app with its own built-in database.

### Family calendars and sync

Both give a family one shared calendar. Cozi imports events from Google, Outlook and Apple calendars and from iCal links, refreshing about once an hour, and can share a read-only feed of its own events. Cozi's FAQ notes that neither direction is two-way sync.

Yuvomi syncs both ways with Google calendars and CalDAV calendars such as iCloud and Nextcloud, subscribes to calendar links and imports calendar files. Connecting Google or Outlook takes a few extra setup steps.

### Chores, meals and money

Yuvomi puts more of the household in one app:

- **Meals to shopping**: drag recipes into the week, and one tap puts every ingredient on the shared list, sorted by aisle.
- **Chores and rewards**: a finished chore pays points that children spend in a reward catalogue a parent approves.
- **Money**: income, expenses, subscriptions and shared costs, with who owes whom.
- **Wall mode**: any tablet becomes a kitchen display with today's plan, the weather and timers.

Cozi has shared to-do lists, chore lists for each family member in its phone apps, a recipe box, and AI meal planning on Max.

### What Cozi does better

- **App-store apps** for iPhone and Android.
- **Nothing to run**: Cozi hosts the service and keeps it up to date.
- **Support from a company**, with priority email support on Gold and Max, while Yuvomi is maintained by one developer and changes often.
