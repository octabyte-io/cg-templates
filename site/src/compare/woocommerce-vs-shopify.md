### WooCommerce and Shopify pricing

As of November 2026, Shopify shows these plans in US dollars:

- **Basic**: $39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly. Online card rates with Shopify Payments are 2.9% + 30¢.
- **Grow**: $105 a month, or $79 a month billed yearly, with 5 staff accounts. Online card rates are 2.7% + 30¢.
- **Advanced**: $399 a month, or $299 a month billed yearly, with 15 staff accounts. Online card rates are 2.5% + 30¢.
- **Plus**: from $2,300 a month, on a 1- or 3-year term.

If you take payments through a provider other than Shopify Payments, Shopify adds a fee on each sale: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced and 0.2% on Plus. A new shop gets a 3-day free trial, then $1 a month for 3 months.

WooCommerce has no plan and no fee on sales. You pay for your server, your payment provider's card fees, and any paid extensions or premium theme you choose.

### Hosting and upkeep

Shopify is a hosted service: Shopify runs the servers, the checkout, security and updates, and you sign in and sell. You cannot run Shopify on your own server.

WooCommerce runs on your own WordPress site. Your products, orders and customer details stay with you, and you can export the site and move it to another host. In return you keep WordPress, WooCommerce and your plugins up to date and backed up, and you set up an email sending service so order emails go out.

### Store features

Both cover the core of an online shop: products with variants, a cart and checkout, orders and refunds, stock, discount codes, shipping zones, tax and sales reports. Both have a mobile app for running the shop.

The difference is where the extras come from. In WooCommerce, subscriptions, bookings and memberships are paid extensions, and payments come through plugins for Stripe, PayPal and others. Shopify includes Shopify Payments and its own POS for selling in person, and leaves the rest to apps.

### Themes, apps and control

Shopify's App Store lists over 16,000 apps, and its theme store has free themes and paid ones shown from $150 to $380. Apps and themes run inside Shopify's rules.

WooCommerce can use any WordPress theme or plugin, and its code is open under the GPL, so a developer can change anything. Your shop and your website or blog live in the same place, which suits a business that already has a WordPress site.
