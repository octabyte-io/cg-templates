### Tandoor and Paprika pricing

As of November 2026, Paprika 4 is free to download on every platform, and one plan covers all your devices:

- **Free**: up to 50 recipes, one grocery list and one cookbook, with no cloud sync.
- **Standard**: free for anyone who bought an earlier Paprika, with unlimited recipes and cloud sync.
- **Premium**: $14.99 a year at its introductory price in the US, or $9.99 a year for earlier buyers, with a one-week free trial. Prices vary by region.

Premium adds family accounts, recipe scanning, social media import, nutrition and calendar feeds.

Tandoor Recipes is free on your own server, with every feature included; you pay for the server and, if you turn on AI, your own AI provider. Tandoor's team also runs a hosted service, billed monthly: Free for one member and 10 recipes, Basic at €1.99, Standard at €3.49 for three members and Premium AI at €4.99 for ten.

### Recipe detail

Tandoor is the more detailed of the two. Each step of a recipe lists just the ingredients it needs, servings scale with fractions or decimals, and search finds recipes even when you misspell a word. Give your ingredients properties such as calories or price, and Tandoor totals them for each recipe.

Paprika keeps recipes simpler, with categories, cookbooks, a pantry with expiry dates and a cook mode with voice commands. Its Premium plan works out nutrition from USDA data.

### Apps and offline use

Paprika has apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows, plus an Apple Watch app, and they work offline.

Tandoor runs in the browser and is made for phones too. Add it to the home screen and it opens like an app, but there is no app in the App Store or Google Play, and only recently opened recipes are kept for offline use.

### Sharing, meal plans and shopping

Tandoor keeps recipes, the meal plan and the shopping list in one space you share with family or friends. The meal plan can be filled by hand or with the auto-planner and added to your calendar, and the shopping list is sorted by supermarket and updates live for everyone shopping.

Paprika plans meals by day, week or month and sorts grocery lists by aisle or recipe. Sharing one collection needs Premium, with family accounts for up to six people.

### Licence

Paprika is proprietary. Tandoor's code is public under the AGPL-3.0 with the Commons Clause, which forbids selling it as a service. That makes it source-available rather than open source, though it is free for you to run at home.
