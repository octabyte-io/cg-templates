### MeTube and 4K Video Downloader pricing

As of November 2026, 4K Video Downloader Plus has four plans, with prices shown in dollars:

- **Starter**: free, with permanent access to basic features and quantity limits. It needs no card and has no trial period.
- **Lite**: $15 a year, billed yearly, for the main features.
- **Personal**: $25 one-time, for lifetime access to the main features, for personal use.
- **Pro**: $45 one-time, shown as a discount from $60, for all features and a commercial use permit.

A 7-day free trial of the paid features is available, and downloading private content is a paid feature.

MeTube has no licence fee and no paid edition. Your cost is a small server and the disk space your downloads use, because every file stays on the server until you delete it.

### A web page or a desktop app

MeTube runs on your server as one web page. Anyone with the password can open it in a browser on a computer or phone, paste a link and watch the shared queue, with progress, speed and time left. Files are saved on the server, sorted into folders per playlist or channel, and you download each one from the Completed list. MeTube has no accounts of its own, so everyone shares one page and one set of files.

4K Video Downloader Plus is an app you install on each Windows, macOS or Linux computer, with a separate Android app. Files go straight to that device.

### What each one downloads

Both save single videos, whole playlists and whole channels, with subtitles. MeTube is a web page for yt-dlp, the widely used open-source download tool, so it supports YouTube and hundreds of other sites. You choose video or audio, the quality and format, a start and end time to keep a clip, or a split by chapters. Subscriptions check a channel or playlist on a schedule and save each new upload.

4K Video Downloader Plus names YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, SoundCloud, Facebook, Twitch and Bilibili, saves video as MP4 or MKV up to 8K and audio as MP3, M4A or OGG, and downloads subtitles in over 50 languages. Its Smart Mode remembers your settings, and an in-app proxy reaches content your network blocks.

### Updates when video sites change

Video sites change often, so any downloader needs updates to keep working. With 4K Video Downloader, that means the app on each computer. MeTube follows yt-dlp, and when a site change stops downloads, installing a newer version of the Cloudgate template fixes it for everyone who uses the page.
