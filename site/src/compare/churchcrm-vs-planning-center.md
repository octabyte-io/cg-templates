### ChurchCRM and Planning Center pricing

As of November 2026, Planning Center sells each product as its own monthly subscription, in US dollars, with no contract, setup fee or cancellation fee:

- **People** (the member database and reports): free, with no limit.
- **Check-Ins**: free for 10 check-ins on a Sunday, then $15 a month for 30, $32 for 75, $69 for 200, $115 for 500, $179 for 1,000 and $239 for unlimited.
- **Services**: free for 5 team members, then $15 a month for 20, $32 for 50, $69 for 150, up to $239 for unlimited.
- **Groups**: free for 15 group members, then $15 a month for 75, $32 for 200, $69 for 500, up to $239 for unlimited.
- **Giving**: free for 10 donations a month, then $15 for 75, up to $239 for unlimited, plus processing fees of 2.15% + $0.30 per card gift and $0.30 per bank transfer in the US.
- **Registrations** and **Calendar** room booking follow the same $15 to $239 steps. Texting costs $0.02 per message.

A church with 200 people at Sunday check-in, 75 people in groups and 50 on worship teams would pay $69 + $15 + $32, or $116 a month, before giving fees.

ChurchCRM has no licence fee and no paid edition. Your cost is the server. ChurchCRM needs PHP 8.4, MySQL 8.4 or MariaDB 10.11, and at least 256 MB of memory for PHP, 512 MB or more recommended. On Cloudgate it runs on a small server.

### Hosting and your members' data

Planning Center is a cloud service: your people, attendance and giving records are kept on its servers, and it looks after updates and backups.

ChurchCRM runs on a server you choose, so member, family and giving records stay with you. The trade-off is that you look after the server, its updates and backups. The code is public under the MIT licence.

### What Planning Center has that ChurchCRM lacks

- **Worship planning.** Planning Center Services builds service plans with songs, people and notes, keeps a song library with keys and arrangements, imports from SongSelect and adds rehearsal tools. ChurchCRM has none of this.
- **Online giving.** Planning Center Giving takes card, bank and text gifts. ChurchCRM only records gifts the office enters.
- **Phone apps.** Planning Center has iPhone and Android apps: Church Center for members, and Planning Center, Services, Check-Ins, Music Stand and Headcounts for staff and volunteers.
- **Workflows and forms.** People has multi-step workflows and forms that add people to them.
- **Integrations.** Planning Center lists Mailchimp, SongSelect, Zapier, ProPresenter and others, and has a documented API.

### What ChurchCRM covers

ChurchCRM keeps people and families with custom fields, groups and Sunday school classes, events with a check-in kiosk, volunteer scheduling, a member portal, and pledges, deposits and year-end giving statements. It can sync with Mailchimp and send email through a sending service you connect. It is translated into 49 locales. Every feature is in the free version, with nothing kept for a paid plan.
