### Razzia and Kahoot! pricing

As of November 2026, Kahoot! sells two families of plans, priced per host ("you need one license per presenter") and billed yearly only. Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT.

For work, Kahoot! 360 Pro plans set the number of players per game:

- **Pro Start**: $15 a month ($180 a year), 50 players.
- **Pro Standard**: $25 a month ($300 a year), 200 players.
- **Pro Plus**: $39 a month ($468 a year), 1,000 players.
- **Pro Max**: $79 a month ($948 a year), 2,000 players.
- **Pro Ultra**: $79 a month ($948 a year), 5,000 players, with onboarding required.

For teachers, Kahoot!+ Bronze is $3 a month (50 players), Silver $7 (100), Gold $10 (200) and Kahoot! One $19 (800), billed yearly. Several of these prices are shown as discounts on the page. Free options exist: Kahoot! 360 Express hosts 15-minute sessions for up to 15 players, and the free Kahoot! Go plan covers the basics for teachers.

Razzia has no licence fee. One small server runs it for every host who knows the manager password.

### The live game

Both play the same way. The host shows each question on a big screen, players join with a game PIN, answer on their phones against the clock and watch a leaderboard, with a podium at the end. Razzia players join in a phone browser with a six-digit PIN or a QR code and a nickname, with no account.

### Questions and quiz writing

Razzia questions have two to four answers, a time limit, points and an optional picture, video or audio clip. They can take one answer, several answers or a number, where players score by how close they get. You can group questions into rounds or switch a quiz to survey mode.

Kahoot! offers quiz and true or false questions on every plan, adds puzzle and poll on paid plans, and keeps slider, type answer, word cloud, open-ended and brainstorm for higher ones. It has a question bank and templates, and its AI can build a quiz from a topic, a web page or a PDF.

### What Kahoot! has that Razzia lacks

- **Self-paced games**: assign a kahoot as homework or training to finish in players' own time.
- **AI and a quiz library**, so you don't start every quiz from a blank page.
- **Reports, courses and certificates** on higher business plans.
- **Integrations** with Zoom, Microsoft Teams and PowerPoint.
- **Phone apps** for iPhone and Android, and separate accounts for each host.
