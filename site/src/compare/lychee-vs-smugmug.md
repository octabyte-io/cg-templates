### Lychee and SmugMug pricing

As of November 2026, SmugMug has four plans, each shown as a monthly price billed yearly, in US dollars:

- **Direct**: $20 a month, with unlimited photo storage, client galleries, digital delivery and Lightroom integration.
- **Portfolio**: $23.50 a month, adding print and download sales, website templates, watermarks and order fulfilment.
- **Pro**: $37 a month, adding several price lists, coupons and packages, and client favourites with download limits.
- **Enterprise**: no price shown; you contact SmugMug.

RAW file storage is an add-on at $10 a month for up to 1 TB, then $6 a month for each extra TB. SmugMug offers a 14-day free trial.

Lychee has no licence fee. Your cost is the server and its disk. The optional Supporter Edition costs €5 a month and adds statistics, user groups, upload quotas, secured image links and watermarks. A Pro add-on on top of it adds a webshop.

### Hosting: your own server or SmugMug's cloud

SmugMug is a hosted service: it stores and serves your photos, with unlimited photo storage on every plan, and you never run a server.

Lychee runs on a server you choose, so your photos stay with you in their original quality. Storage is limited only by your disk, and you look after the server, its updates and its backups yourself.

### Sharing galleries with clients

Both tools do the core job of delivering photos. In Lychee you put photos in albums and sub-albums, pick a cover, header photo and layout, and make an album public, protected by a password or shared only with chosen accounts. Visitors can download single photos or a whole album as a ZIP at full resolution, if you allow it. Each photo can show its camera details, and geotagged photos appear on a map.

SmugMug has password-protected galleries, custom visibility settings and website templates, and its higher plans let clients pick favourites.

### What SmugMug has that Lychee lacks

This is where SmugMug goes further:

- **Print sales.** Portfolio and Pro sell prints and downloads, with order fulfilment by SmugMug's labs. Lychee has no print lab.
- **Proofing.** SmugMug Pro has client favouriting with download limits. Lychee has no proofing.
- **Apps and plugins.** SmugMug has iPhone and Android apps and plugins for Lightroom and Luminar. Lychee has no phone app yet and works in a phone's browser.
