### SerpBear and Semrush pricing

As of November 2026, Semrush's SEO plan costs $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly, for 5 websites and 500 keywords tracked daily. Starter is $199 a month ($165.17 billed yearly) with the same tracking limits and more research tools. Pro+ is $299 a month ($248.17 billed yearly) for 15 websites and 1,500 keywords. Advanced, with 5,000 or more keywords, is priced by sales. There is also a free plan with one demo project and 10 reports a day, and extra users cost from $45 a month.

SerpBear has no plans. The software is free; your costs are the server and the scraping service that runs each Google lookup. You choose how often to check, daily, weekly or monthly, and how deep to look, which sets that cost.

### What each one tracks

SerpBear tracks Google only. For each keyword you pick desktop or mobile and a country, and it keeps the current position, the best position and a history chart.

Semrush Position Tracking covers Google, Bing, Baidu, ChatGPT Search and Google AI Mode, on desktop, tablet or mobile, down to postal-code level. It updates daily, compares you with up to 20 competitors per project, and flags search features such as featured snippets and AI Overviews.

### Limits

SerpBear has no limit on websites or keywords. The practical limit is what you want to pay the scraping service.

Semrush caps tracking by plan: 500 keywords on SEO and Starter, 1,500 on Pro+, and 5,000 or more on Advanced.

### Beyond rankings

Semrush is a full suite: keyword research, backlink analysis, site audits, competitor research, content tools and local SEO. SerpBear does none of that. It adds two Google connections instead: Search Console, for real clicks and impressions per keyword, and Google Ads, for keyword ideas and search volumes.

### Reports and team

SerpBear has one admin login. It emails daily, weekly or monthly changes, exports keywords as CSV and has an API for reporting tools, but no client reports or team accounts. Semrush sends weekly email reports, exports to Excel, CSV, Google Sheets and PDF, and sells extra users.
