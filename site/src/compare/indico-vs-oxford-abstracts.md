### Indico and Oxford Abstracts pricing

As of November 2026, Oxford Abstracts shows a free plan and three packages, each a one-off cost per event:

- **Basic**: free, for small events and meetings, with unlimited submissions, review and decisions, a built-in email tool, delegate registration and online support.
- **Abstract management**: £650 ($890, €790), adding custom forms and emails, abstract books, reports and showing reviews to submitters.
- **Standard conference**: £1,690 ($2,290, €1,990), adding a program builder, live and on-demand content and custom branding.
- **Professional conference**: £2,490 ($3,450, €2,990), adding a poster gallery, attendee chat, sponsors and exhibitors, a website builder for 12 months and certificates.

Delegate registration on any plan costs 2.5% of registration revenue, excluding VAT, capped at £3,750 per event. Add-ons include multi-stage submissions and symposia at £620 ($850) each.

Indico has no price at all. It is MIT-licensed with no paid edition, so every event costs the same: the server it runs on. Cloudgate gives it a medium server with 2 GB of memory.

### Abstracts and reviewing

Both tools cover a call for abstracts and review. Oxford Abstracts gives every plan unlimited submissions, reviewer assignment and an online reviewer workspace, and keeps custom review and decision forms, abstract books and reports for its paid packages.

Indico has a call for abstracts with tracks, abstract reviewing and a separate paper reviewing workflow, and it creates a book of abstracts as a PDF. There are no plan limits on any of these.

### Registration, timetable and badges

Both let you design a registration form and take payments. Oxford Abstracts adds ticket levels, early-bird timeframes and coupon codes, and takes payments through Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net or bank transfer.

Indico takes registration fees by bank transfer, PayPal or Stripe into your own account, with no share of your revenue. It has a drag-and-drop timetable, a badge and ticket editor, and a check-in web app for the door. Oxford Abstracts keeps its program builder for the Standard and Professional packages, and name badges for Professional.

### Virtual events and extras

Oxford Abstracts is the fuller choice for an online or hybrid conference. Its higher packages include live and on-demand content, Zoom scheduling, a poster gallery, event chat, sponsors, exhibitor space and an event website.

Indico focuses on the event itself and what comes after. It connects to Zoom for meetings, keeps every event's slides, papers and minutes in a lasting archive, and has room booking for meetings and equipment.

### Hosting, data and support

Oxford Abstracts hosts the platform and gives every plan helpdesk support and online help files. Its pricing page lists event data access for one year.

Indico runs on your own server, so submissions, reviews and registrations stay with you for as long as you keep them. You look after the server and its backups, and help comes from Indico's documentation and community forum.
