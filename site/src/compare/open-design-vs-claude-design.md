### Open Design and Claude Design pricing

As of November 2026, Claude Design is part of every Claude plan, and its use counts against the plan's limits. You can pay for extra usage past them.

- **Free**: $0, with limited usage.
- **Pro**: $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month billed yearly.
- **Max**: $100 a month, with five times Pro's usage.
- **Team**: Standard seats are $25 per seat a month billed monthly, or $20 billed yearly.
- **Enterprise**: $20 per seat a month billed yearly, with a 20-seat minimum, plus usage at API rates.

Open Design has no licence fee or plan. You pay for the server and for the AI provider whose key you add, which bills you per use. Its makers also sell optional hosted AI credits, which you don't need.

### Hosting and AI models

Claude Design runs only in Anthropic's cloud and uses Claude models. Anthropic announced it running on Claude Opus 4.7.

Open Design runs on your own server, and its makers also offer desktop apps for macOS and Windows. It has no AI model of its own: you paste a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Azure OpenAI or any OpenAI-compatible service. Your projects and keys stay on your server, but each brief goes to the provider you choose. A small server is enough, because the AI work happens at the provider.

### What each makes, and how you export

Both turn a written brief into a design you refine by chatting. Claude Design makes designs, prototypes, slides, one-pagers, pitch decks, landing pages and campaign assets. It exports to Canva, PDF, PowerPoint and standalone HTML, and packs a finished design into a bundle for Claude Code.

Open Design makes web pages, clickable prototypes, slide decks with speaker notes, and images. It exports HTML, PDF, PowerPoint, Markdown and ZIP. Designs are real web pages, so developers can take the HTML into their own project.

### Brand style and teamwork

Claude Design reads your codebase and design files to build a design system, and teams can keep more than one. Designs are private, viewable by link or editable by colleagues inside your Claude organization.

Open Design comes with more than 150 design systems, and you can add your own colours, fonts and style. The template Cloudgate installs has one shared password for the workspace, with no separate user accounts, so give it only to people you trust.
