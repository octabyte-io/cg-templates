### Dolibarr and QuickBooks pricing

As of November 2026, QuickBooks Online's US pricing page lists five plans, priced per month with no annual contract:

- **Free**: $0, for 1 user, with 2 invoices a month and 1 connected bank.
- **Simple Start**: $38 a month, for 1 user.
- **Essentials**: $85 a month, for 3 users.
- **Plus**: $140 a month, for 5 users, adding inventory tracking and project profitability.
- **Advanced**: $340 a month, for 25 users.

Intuit was offering 70% off for the first three months when we checked. Payroll costs extra, from $50 a month plus $7 per employee.

Dolibarr has no licence fee and no user limit, and every core module is in the free release. You pay for the server; Dolibarr runs on a small one. The paid extras are third-party modules from the DoliStore marketplace and hosted plans from providers such as DoliCloud.

### Features: a business suite or an accounting app

QuickBooks Online is accounting software. It sends invoices, pays bills, tracks expenses, connects to your bank and builds reports, and the higher plans add inventory, budgets and project profitability.

Dolibarr is built from about 100 modules you switch on as needed:

- **Customers and sales**: a CRM, opportunities, quotes customers can sign online, orders, contracts and tickets.
- **Products and stock**: a catalogue, warehouses, barcodes, lots and serial numbers, purchasing and shipments.
- **Billing**: invoices, credit notes, recurring invoices, payments and bank accounts.
- **More of the business**: projects and timesheets, leave and expense reports, point of sale and a website builder.

Running several companies in one Dolibarr needs a third-party module.

### Bookkeeping, banks and payroll

This is where QuickBooks is stronger. QuickBooks connects to your bank, its paid plans include automated bookkeeping, and Intuit sells payroll as an add-on.

Dolibarr has a double-entry accounting module, but bookkeeping takes more manual work. There are no built-in bank feeds: you record payments against invoices and bank accounts, and DoliStore add-ons can import statement files. There is no payroll. Its tax rules are strongest for Europe, so check what your country needs, such as e-invoicing.

### Hosting and mobile apps

QuickBooks Online runs only in Intuit's cloud, with apps for iPhone and Android.

Dolibarr runs on your own server, needing PHP and a MariaDB, MySQL or PostgreSQL database, or with a hosting provider. On Android, the DoliDroid app opens your Dolibarr; on iPhone, you use the web browser. The interface is dense and has many settings, so expect some time to set it up.
