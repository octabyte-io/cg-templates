### Typecho and Medium pricing

As of November 2026, Medium's help centre lists two memberships, in US dollars:

- **Medium Member**: $5 a month or $50 a year, for unlimited reading of member-only stories without ads.
- **Friend of Medium**: $15 a month or $150 a year, with the same benefits, and four times more of your reading time paid to the writers you read.

Writing and publishing on Medium is free, and readers without a membership can read a few member-only stories. Prices can vary by country.

Typecho has no fee and no paid edition. You pay for the server, and a small one is enough, because Typecho is a light PHP app with a MariaDB database.

### Ownership and your web address

On Typecho, the blog is yours: your domain, your design, your posts and your readers' comments, all on your server. Nobody else's rules decide who sees a post or whether it sits behind a paywall.

On Medium, your stories live on Medium's site and design. Members can set up a custom domain for a profile or publication, but the platform, the reading experience and the paywall rules are Medium's.

### Readers and earnings

Medium's main advantage is its audience. Stories can be recommended to Medium's own readers, and members can join the Partner Program, which pays writers from members' reading time. To join, you need a membership, a published story, a complete profile and a bank in a country where payouts are offered; payouts generally need at least $10.

Typecho has no built-in audience or payments. Readers find you through search, RSS feeds and social media, and every one you gain follows your own site.

### Writing, design and features

Typecho gives you a Markdown editor with a toolbar and preview, categories and tags, pages, comment moderation, themes, plugins and RSS feeds. Editors and contributors can have their own logins, and XML-RPC lets you post from blogging apps. The trade-off is the community: much of it, and many themes and plugins, are in Chinese, and the admin starts in Chinese until you switch it to English.

Medium gives you a clean editor, publications for groups of writers, and responses from readers, with a fixed design you cannot change much. It has apps for iPhone and Android.
