### Monica and Dex pricing

As of November 2026, Dex has three plans, priced per user in US dollars:

- **Premium**: $12 a month billed yearly, $16 billed quarterly or $20 billed monthly, with LinkedIn sync for up to 2,500 connections, email and calendar sync and all the apps.
- **Professional**: $20 a month billed yearly, $27 quarterly or $34 monthly, with up to 9,000 LinkedIn connections, Outlook, mail merge, the API and Zapier.
- **Team**: $40 per user a month, billed monthly, adding CRM integrations and single sign-on.

Premium and Professional have a 7-day free trial that needs a card.

Self-hosted Monica is free with every feature, and its makers say it always will be. Your cost is a small server. Monica's own hosted service is paid, and its registration is closed.

### Dex syncs contacts, Monica is typed in

This is the biggest difference. Dex pulls people in from more than 15 sources, including LinkedIn, Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, WhatsApp, iMessage, Facebook and Instagram, merges duplicates and builds one timeline of your meetings, calls, emails and messages.

Monica connects to none of these. You add each person and note yourself, or import a vCard file. In return, Monica never reads your email or social accounts, and its makers say it will never be a social network or scan your data.

### What Monica and Dex remember

Monica is built for private life: how you met, partners, children and pets, calls, activities, gifts, debts, tasks, documents and a simple journal, with birthday and stay-in-touch reminders. Dex adds notes, tags, groups and custom fields, keep-in-touch reminders, briefs before meetings and updates when connections change roles.

### Monica and Dex on your phone

Dex has web, iPhone, Android, macOS and Windows apps and a Chrome extension. Monica is a web app that works in a phone's browser, and syncs contacts and birthdays to your phone's address book and calendar over CardDAV and CalDAV.
