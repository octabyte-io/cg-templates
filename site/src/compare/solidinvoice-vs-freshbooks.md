### SolidInvoice and FreshBooks pricing

As of November 2026, FreshBooks has four plans, shown in US dollars a month:

- **Lite**: $23, for up to 5 billable clients.
- **Plus**: $43, for up to 50 billable clients, adding proposals, retainers, double-entry accounting reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access.
- **Premium**: $70, with unlimited clients and project profitability.
- **Select**: priced on request, with two team accounts included and lower card and ACH fees.

Each extra team member costs $11 a month. FreshBooks often shows a discount for the first months, offers 10% off when you pay yearly, and has a 30-day free trial.

Self-hosted SolidInvoice has no licence fee and no limit on clients, invoices or users. Your cost is the server, plus the usual fees Stripe or PayPal charge on each payment. SolidInvoice also sells a hosted cloud: a free plan for 3 active clients and 5 invoices a month, then Solo at $9, Pro at $19 and Business at $39 a month.

### Getting paid online: cards, PayPal and reminders

This is where the two are closest. In SolidInvoice, each invoice email carries the PDF and a link where the client views and pays it, through your own Stripe or PayPal account. Unpaid invoices are marked overdue on their own, and reminders go out on the schedule you set. Recurring invoices can be paused at any time.

FreshBooks takes card, ACH, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments, sends automatic reminders and can add late fees. Its Select plan lowers card and ACH fees.

### What FreshBooks has that SolidInvoice lacks

- **Expenses and time tracking.** FreshBooks tracks both, with projects and budgets on every plan. SolidInvoice has none.
- **Accounting.** Plus and higher add double-entry reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access. SolidInvoice keeps no books.
- **Proposals and retainers.** These come with FreshBooks Plus.
- **Phone apps.** FreshBooks has iPhone and Android apps. SolidInvoice runs in a phone's browser.
- **Languages.** SolidInvoice's interface is in English only.

### Hosting and your data

FreshBooks runs only in its own cloud and handles servers, updates and backups. Self-hosted SolidInvoice runs on a server you choose, so your clients and invoices stay with you, and you look after updates and backups. One install can run several companies, with user roles and two-step sign-in.
