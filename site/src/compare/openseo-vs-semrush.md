### OpenSEO and Semrush pricing

As of November 2026, Semrush's SEO and AI search plans are:

- **Free**: one demo project and 10 reports a day across the core tools.
- **SEO**: $139 a month, or $117.33 a month billed yearly, for 5 websites and 500 keywords tracked daily.
- **Starter**: $199 a month, or $165.17 a month billed yearly, adding fuller keyword research and competitor tools, MCP access and AI visibility tracking.
- **Pro+**: $299 a month, or $248.17 a month billed yearly, for 15 websites, 1,500 keywords, historical data and content optimization.
- **Advanced** and **Enterprise**: priced by Semrush's sales team.

Extra users cost from $45 a month each, and paid plans come with a 7-day free trial.

OpenSEO has no plans. The software is free; you pay DataForSEO, its data provider, for every keyword search, rank check, backlink lookup and audit. OpenSEO's guide says new DataForSEO accounts get $1 of test credit and the smallest top-up is $50. If you would rather not self-host, OpenSEO's own hosted version starts at $10 a month and adds 28% to the data cost.

### Data: a big index or pay per lookup

Semrush runs its own databases. It says they hold over 28.8 billion keywords and 43 trillion backlinks across 142 country databases, with historical data on its higher plans.

OpenSEO has no data of its own. Everything comes from DataForSEO when you ask for it, so a quiet month costs little and a busy one costs more. The app shows nothing until you add a DataForSEO API key, and it keeps far less history than Semrush.

### Rank tracking

Semrush Position Tracking checks rankings daily in Google, Bing, Baidu, ChatGPT Search and Google AI Mode, on desktop, tablet or mobile, down to postal-code level, and compares you with up to 20 competitors per project. The entry plans cap tracking at 500 keywords.

OpenSEO tracks Google on desktop or mobile, daily, weekly or monthly, and each check is paid to DataForSEO, so cost rather than a plan sets how many keywords you follow. It also has a local grid that shows where a business ranks on Google Maps around an area.

### Research, audits and AI visibility

Both cover the everyday SEO work: keyword volume, difficulty and intent, a domain's ranking keywords and top pages, backlinks and referring domains, and a technical site audit. OpenSEO's audit crawls up to 10,000 pages and sorts 32 kinds of issues by severity.

Both also track how AI assistants mention your brand, and both connect to AI assistants through MCP. Semrush adds tools OpenSEO does not have: content writing and optimization, keyword cannibalization reports, local listings management, and traffic and advertising research.

### Hosting and team

Semrush is a cloud service; there is no self-hosted edition. OpenSEO runs on your own server, needs at least 4 GB of memory, and keeps your projects there. Teammates join by invitation link at no extra cost, where Semrush charges for each additional user.
