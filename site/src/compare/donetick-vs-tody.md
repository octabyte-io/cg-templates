### Donetick and Tody pricing

As of November 2026, Tody has three levels, priced yearly:

- **Free**: the core cleaning plan for one person, with no ads. You can assign and rotate chores on this plan too.
- **Premium**: $9.99 a year, adding more than one home and a Focus Timer.
- **Premium+**: from $25 a year, priced by household size: Duo (2 members) $25, Family (up to 6) $40 and Team (up to 12) $80. It adds the shared live plan on everyone's phone and FairShare.

Every paid level has a 30-day free trial.

Donetick has no premium plan. Every feature, including sharing with any number of household members, is free. Your cost is a small server.

### Phone apps or the browser

Tody is made for phones: it has apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and a polished look with its dust-bunny mascot, Dusty.

Donetick is built for the browser first. It works on any phone, tablet or computer and can be added to a phone's home screen. Its Android app is an early download from the project's releases, and its iPhone app is an alpha test version on TestFlight.

### Rooms or due dates

Tody splits your home into rooms and small tasks. Each task has its own interval, and a colour shows how far it has gone through that interval: green means it can wait, red means it is overdue.

Donetick lists chores by when they are due, such as overdue, today and later. You add chores in plain words, and it fills in the day, time and repeat. Labels such as Kitchen or Bathroom group chores by room, and subtasks reset each time a repeating chore is done.

### Sharing and fairness

Both can rotate chores between people. In Donetick, a repeating chore can pass in turn, at random or to whoever has done the fewest, and finished chores earn points with charts of who did what. A dashboard view on a kitchen tablet lets anyone tap their name to tick off a chore.

In Tody, the whole household seeing the same live plan on their own phones is part of Premium+. That plan adds FairShare, a monthly target for each member, and a monthly race against Dusty with a leaderboard.

### Reminders and smart home

Tody reminds you in the app. Donetick sends reminders through Telegram, Discord or Pushover, and its official Home Assistant integration shows each person's chores as a to-do list. It also supports sign-in through OIDC providers such as Authentik or Keycloak.
