### ONLYOFFICE and Microsoft 365 pricing

As of November 2026, Microsoft lists these prices per user a month in US dollars, in effect from July 1, 2026. Prices vary by country and currency.

- **Business Basic**: $7, with Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook on the web, Teams, a 50 GB mailbox and 1 TB of OneDrive per user.
- **Business Standard**: $14, adding the desktop apps.
- **Business Premium**: $22, adding protection against phishing and ransomware and device management.

ONLYOFFICE Docs comes in three editions:

- **Community Edition**: free under the AGPL-3.0 licence, recommended for up to 20 users, with community support.
- **Enterprise**: from $33 per connection a year, for at least 50 connections, with an admin panel, scaling to many servers and paid support. A lifetime licence is also sold, with three years of updates.
- **Developer**: for building the editors into your own product, priced by ONLYOFFICE's sales team.

With the Community Edition, your cost is the server. You also need a file app such as Nextcloud or Seafile, which is free.

### Editors, not a whole suite

Microsoft 365 bundles the Office apps with Outlook email, Teams chat and meetings, and OneDrive storage. ONLYOFFICE Docs is only the editors. It has no accounts or storage of its own, and people open files from Nextcloud or Seafile with their usual login.

Inside the editors, several people work on one file at once, with comments, a chat beside the document, tracked changes and version history. Spreadsheets have formulas, pivot tables, charts and conditional formatting. Slides have transitions, animations and a presenter mode. ONLYOFFICE also edits PDFs and fills in forms, and supports plugins and macros.

### Office file formats

ONLYOFFICE Docs saves .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files as they are, with no conversion, so colleagues on Microsoft 365 can keep working on them. It also opens older formats such as DOC, XLS and PPT, and OpenDocument files. Complex Office files can look slightly different from how they look in Microsoft Office.

### Hosting and devices

Microsoft 365 runs in Microsoft's cloud. Business Standard and Premium also install the desktop apps on up to five PCs or Macs, five tablets and five phones per user.

ONLYOFFICE Docs runs on your own server, and ONLYOFFICE recommends 4 GB of memory. Its free Desktop Editors for Windows, macOS and Linux connect to Nextcloud and Seafile. In the Community Edition, files open read-only in a phone's browser. The trade-off is that you look after the server and its updates, and the Community Edition has no admin panel: settings live in config files.
