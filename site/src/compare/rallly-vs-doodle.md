### Rallly and Doodle pricing

As of November 2026, Doodle has four plans:

- **Free**: one group poll, one booking page, one one-to-one and one sign-up sheet, up to 20 time slots a poll, with ads.
- **Pro**: per seat, billed yearly or monthly, with unlimited polls and pages, deadlines, reminders and no ads.
- **Team**: USD 16 per seat a month billed yearly, adding an admin console, roles, co-hosting and custom branding.
- **Enterprise**: from USD 15k, billed yearly, for organisations with 50 or more employees.

Self-hosted Rallly is free for one signed-in organiser, with unlimited polls. A team of organisers needs a one-time licence key for Rallly version 4: Plus at $49 for up to 5 users, Organization at $499 for up to 50, and Enterprise from $999. The key works on trust, so Rallly keeps running past the limit and shows a reminder. Your other costs are the server, with 2 GB of memory, and an email sending service. Rallly's makers also run a hosted Rallly, free for up to 3 open polls, with Pro at $6 per seat a month billed yearly.

### Group polls

Both tools do the same core job. You propose dates or times, share one link, and people mark each option without an account. Rallly offers Yes, If need be and No, and so does Doodle. When you pick the winner, both send a calendar invite to the people who answered.

Rallly shows times in each voter's time zone, lets people discuss on the poll, and adds a Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Webex link. Doodle's paid plans add deadlines, automatic reminders and hidden participant details, and anyone with a Doodle account can connect a calendar to see conflicts.

### What Doodle has that Rallly lacks

- **Booking pages and one-to-ones.** People can pick a free slot from your calendar.
- **Sign-up sheets.** People claim a slot, such as a shift or a parent meeting.
- **Phone apps.** Doodle has iPhone and Android apps; Rallly runs in a phone's browser.
- **Extras.** Zapier, Stripe payments and custom branding.

### Ads, privacy and hosting

Doodle's free plan shows ads to organisers and participants, and every answer lives on Doodle's servers. Rallly has no ads, and on your own server the poll answers and email addresses stay with you. In return you look after the server, its updates and its email service.
