### Decidim and Go Vocal pricing

As of November 2026, Go Vocal's plans page shows no prices. It lists these plans:

- **Essential**: 1 active project and 1 admin seat, with an onboarding session and a dedicated success manager.
- **Standard**: unlimited projects, 4 admin seats and 5 project manager seats, with the participation tools, a report builder and two onboarding sessions.
- **Premium**: 8 admin seats and 25 project manager seats, adding advanced AI analysis, document annotation, auto-translation for up to 3 languages and priority support.
- **Enterprise**: unlimited admins, adding ArcGIS, single sign-on, a Power BI connector, webhooks and auto-translation for up to 10 languages.

Go Vocal says Enterprise features are also sold as add-ons for Standard and Premium.

Decidim has no licence fee. Your cost is the server, an email service and, if you want maps, a map provider. On Cloudgate, Decidim gets a server with 4 GB of memory.

### Participation tools

Both cover the core of online participation: proposals, surveys, voting and participatory budgets.

Decidim groups its tools as components inside spaces. Components include proposals, collaborative texts, surveys, meetings, debates, comments, voting, participatory budgeting, elections, accountability, newsletters, pages and blogs. Spaces are participatory processes with phases, assemblies for councils and working groups, citizen initiatives and conferences.

Go Vocal lists ideation, surveys, map surveys, voting and prioritisation, participatory budgeting and proposals, and says it has more than 16 participation methods, including voice recording and paper forms. Its AI sensemaking groups what residents say into themes and helps write reports.

### Hosting and source code

Go Vocal runs its platform for you and shows WCAG 2.2 AA, ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR badges on its site. It works open core: a Free Edition under AGPL-3.0 that you can host, and a commercial edition whose code is public but whose licence does not allow you to host it.

Decidim is AGPL-3.0 software you run on your own server or through one of the official Decidim service providers. One installation can host several organisations. You look after updates, backups and the email service yourself.

### Support and setup

Every Go Vocal plan includes onboarding sessions, and Go Vocal says each customer gets a dedicated participation expert from day one. Premium adds priority support.

Decidim is built by a public community around the non-profit Decidim association. It has documentation and community help, and service providers sell installation and support. Its admin panel is large, so plan time to learn it.
