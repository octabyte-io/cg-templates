### LinkStack and Linktree pricing

As of November 2026, Linktree's pricing page shows these plans in US dollars (prices vary by region):

- **Free**: unlimited basic links and email and phone sign-ups.
- **Starter**: $7 a month billed yearly ($84 a year), or $9 billed monthly. Adds a personalized page, unlimited featured links and basic analytics.
- **Pro**: $14 a month billed yearly ($168 a year), or $17 billed monthly. Adds branding removal, custom designs, selling with 0% Linktree fees and mailing-list integrations.
- **Premium**: $30 a month billed yearly ($360 a year), or $35 billed monthly. Adds link rules, AI integrations, all-time analytics, data export, social media scheduling and unlimited Instagram auto-replies.

Linktree says transaction and processing fees may still apply when you sell. Agencies and larger teams get a custom plan.

LinkStack has no plans. The software is free under the AGPL-3.0 licence, and your cost is a small server. If you would rather not host it, LinkStack's makers sell hosted pages from $1 a month.

### Link-in-bio page on your own domain or on linktr.ee

Both give you one page with your photo, name, a short bio and buttons to everything you share. LinkStack has ready-made buttons for dozens of sites, plus headings, text, an email address, a phone number and a contact card, with themes to change the look.

The main difference is where the page lives. A LinkStack page runs on your own server and web address, and one install can host pages for other people when you turn on sign-ups. A Linktree page lives on linktr.ee, and Linktree runs it for you.

### Analytics, selling and integrations

LinkStack counts clicks on each link and shows the totals on your dashboard. It has no deeper reports and no selling, sign-up forms or app integrations; you link out to your own shop or form instead.

Linktree builds much more into the page. Its paid plans add detailed analytics, selling digital products, courses and affiliate links, email and SMS sign-ups, mailing-list integrations, social post scheduling and Instagram auto-replies. It also has iPhone and Android apps for managing your page.

### Self-hosted LinkStack or managed Linktree

With LinkStack, you look after the server, its updates and its backups, and you turn off sign-ups unless you want others to join. LinkStack is light, so the smallest server is enough. Linktree needs no upkeep at all, which is a fair reason to choose it if you only want one page and don't mind the monthly fee.
