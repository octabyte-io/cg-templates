### Odoo and NetSuite pricing

As of November 2026, Oracle shows no public prices for NetSuite. Its pricing page says customers pay an annual licence fee made up of three parts: the core platform, optional modules and the number of users. There is also a one-time implementation fee. You get the price by asking NetSuite for a quote.

Odoo Community has no licence fee and no user limit; your cost is the server. Odoo the company also sells hosted plans priced per user a month, which include Odoo Enterprise. Its One App Free plan on Odoo Online is free for one app with unlimited users. This page compares the free Community edition, which is what Cloudgate installs.

### Accounting: the biggest difference

NetSuite includes accounting in its platform licence and is built around full financials, with more planning and reporting tools sold as modules.

Odoo Community's money app is Invoicing: customer invoices and reminders, vendor bills, credit notes, payments and several currencies. Bank reconciliation, financial reports, budgets and payroll are in the paid Odoo Enterprise. A business that needs a full general ledger either keeps its books in other software or buys Enterprise.

### Sales, stock and your website

Odoo's apps share one database, so a quote becomes an order, a delivery and an invoice without re-typing. Odoo Community has a CRM pipeline, quotations customers can sign and pay online, purchase orders, stock across warehouses with dropshipping, manufacturing with bills of materials, a point of sale till, and a website builder with an online shop, blog and live chat.

NetSuite covers CRM, order management, inventory and manufacturing, and sells ecommerce as SuiteCommerce.

### Hosting and phone apps

NetSuite runs only in Oracle's cloud, and Oracle publishes a NetSuite app for iPhone and Android. Odoo Community runs on a server you choose and works in a phone's web browser; the official Odoo phone apps are part of Enterprise. With Odoo Community you look after backups, and Odoo releases a new major version every year, so moving to it is up to you. Some extras, such as sending SMS, use paid Odoo credits.
