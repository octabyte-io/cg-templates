### Helfertool and SignUpGenius pricing

As of November 2026, SignUpGenius has five plans:

- **Free**: unlimited sign-up pages in many formats, confirmation and reminder emails, group tools, payment collection and email support.
- **Silver**: $8.99 a month billed yearly ($107.89 a year) or $11.99 monthly. One ad-free sign-up, 2 custom questions, 150 text invites or updates and 2,500 emails a month.
- **Gold**: $22.49 a month billed yearly ($269.89 a year) or $29.99 monthly. Ten ad-free sign-ups, 10 administrators, 500 texts and 10,000 emails a month, tabbed sign-ups and waitlists.
- **Platinum**: $44.99 a month billed yearly ($539.89 a year) or $59.99 monthly. Unlimited ad-free sign-ups, 20 administrators, 1,500 texts and 20,000 emails a month, custom URLs and phone support.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing.

Payments collected through SignUpGenius carry their own processing fees.

Helfertool has no fee, no ads and no plan. Your cost is a small server, which Cloudgate sets up with its database, and an email sending service for confirmations.

### Shifts at events, or every kind of sign-up

Helfertool does one job: staffing an event. You create jobs such as bar, entrance or set-up, and each job gets shifts with a start, an end and the number of helpers it needs. The overview shows at a glance which shifts are still short, and statistics cover filled and empty slots, T-shirt sizes and food preferences.

SignUpGenius covers far more ground. It handles volunteer slots, but also potlucks, parties, school events and conferences, with sign-ups duplicated for recurring events, calendar sync and payment collection.

### Running the event

Helfertool adds tools that event organisers use on the day. You can hide internal jobs and send a private link, give each team lead access to their own job's helpers, print badges for access control, note who got their T-shirt or thank-you gift, and lend out equipment with barcodes. Lists export to Excel or PDF, and you can email the helpers of one job or the whole event.

SignUpGenius focuses on reaching people: automatic confirmation and reminder emails on every plan, text messages on paid plans, custom questions and more administrators as you move up.

### Ads, data and hosting

SignUpGenius is a hosted service. Its paid plans are priced partly on how many of your sign-ups are free of ads.

Helfertool runs on your own server, so there are no ads, and your volunteers' names, emails and phone numbers stay with you. The trade-off is that you look after the server and connect an email service. Helfertool is made by volunteers in Germany and works in English and German.
