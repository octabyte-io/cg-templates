### Dossier and Teal pricing

As of November 2026, Teal's own pages list two levels:

- **Free**: no time limit, with unlimited resumes, unlimited job tracking, 10 resume templates, top-five keyword matching, basic resume analysis and limited AI credits.
- **Teal+**: $13 every 7 days, $29 every 30 days or $79 every 90 days, renewing until you cancel. It adds unlimited AI writing, full keyword lists, advanced resume analysis and the full template library.

Dossier has no plans. Every feature is free, and your cost is the server. The AI writing is billed only if you choose a hosted provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic or Mistral; a ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro subscription does not include that API use. A local model through Ollama or LM Studio costs nothing extra.

### AI writing and privacy

Both match your resume against a job posting and draft tailored resumes and cover letters. Teal's AI is part of its service. Dossier uses a model you choose, and with a local one your profile, postings and documents never leave your own hardware. A hosted model receives what each task needs.

Dossier drafts use only facts from your profile. When a posting asks for something you don't have, Dossier shows it as a gap instead of making it up. You can refine a draft in a chat, undo AI changes or go back to an earlier version.

### Saving and finding jobs

Teal's Chrome extension saves a job from LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and 40+ other job boards straight into its tracker, with the details filled in.

Dossier has no browser extension. You paste a posting's text, or use its job search, which looks through JSearch, Adzuna, FreeHire and startup lists such as YC job posts and Hacker News' monthly "Who is hiring?" thread once you add a source. An optional job agent runs up to three saved searches a day and never applies for you.

### Tracking and follow-up

Both track applications on a board. Teal's tracker is unlimited on the free plan. Dossier has a board, a table, a dashboard and a calendar, and it can connect to Gmail to send applications from your own address and read replies, and to Google Calendar for interviews.

### Who Dossier and Teal are for

Dossier is made for one person per install, with one password and no accounts, in a laptop or desktop browser, with an English interface. Teal is an online service where anyone can sign up for their own account and start at once.
