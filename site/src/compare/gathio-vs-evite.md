### Gathio and Evite pricing

As of November 2026, Evite's help pages list these prices, in dollars:

- **Free designs**: nothing to send, unless you add a Premium upgrade such as an envelope and stamp, a sticker, a background or ad removal.
- **Silver**: $17.99 for up to 12 guests.
- **Gold**: $36.99 for up to 30 guests.
- **Platinum**: $68.99 for up to 75 guests.
- **Diamond**: $99.99 for up to 750 guests.
- **Evite Pro**: $249.99 a year for unlimited invitations of up to 2,500 guests each.

The Silver to Diamond packages are for one event each.

Gathio has no fees, no paid edition and no limit on events or guests. Your cost is the server and, ideally, an email service, so Gathio can send hosts their editing link and keep guests updated. On Cloudgate, Gathio runs on a small server.

### Invitation design

Evite is built around the card. You choose from a library of free and Premium designs, add envelopes, stamps and stickers, or bring a design in from Canva. It also offers digital cards, sign-up sheets, potluck lists and gift registries.

Gathio makes a plain event page: a name, time, place, description and one cover picture. There are no card designs.

### Sending invites and reminders

Evite sends invitations by email, by link or by text message. Texts go only to US and Canada numbers. Evite tracks replies in real time, lets you follow up with guests who haven't answered, and can schedule reminders. Its apps let you pick guests from your phone's contacts or past guest lists.

Gathio gives you one link to share however you like. It sends no texts. Guests who leave an email hear when the time, place or details change, and can chat in the event's comments. People on Mastodon or Friendica can also follow an event and RSVP from there.

### Guests, privacy and control

On Gathio, nobody needs an account. Guests click Add me with a name and party size. You can cap numbers, ask your own questions with answers only you see, and hide the address until you approve each guest. You edit through a private link instead of an account, so keep it safe.

Each Gathio event is visible to anyone with its link, and by default it is deleted 7 days after it ends, with its guest list and comments. The guest list stays on your server, not with an invitation company.
