### Loomio and Doodle pricing

As of November 2026, Doodle has four plans:

- **Free**: one group poll, one booking page, one one-to-one and one sign-up sheet, up to 20 time slots a poll, with ads.
- **Pro**: per seat, billed yearly or monthly, with unlimited polls and pages, deadlines, reminders and no ads.
- **Team**: USD 16 per seat a month billed yearly, adding an admin console, roles, co-hosting and custom branding.
- **Enterprise**: from USD 15k, billed yearly, for organisations with 50 or more employees.

Self-hosted Loomio has no licence fee and no paid features. Your costs are a server with 2 GB of memory and an email sending service. Loomio's makers sell hosting from $399 a year ($39 monthly) on Starter for up to 30 members, with nonprofit prices from $299 a year.

### Two different jobs

Doodle is a scheduling tool. Its group polls, booking pages, one-to-one meetings and sign-up sheets all help people meet at a time that suits them.

Loomio is a decision tool. A group discusses a question, runs a proposal or poll, and records the outcome, so the reasons stay easy to find later. Its time poll is one of several poll types, next to consent proposals, dot votes, ranked choice and STV elections.

### Meeting polls side by side

In a Loomio time poll you enter dates and times and a meeting length. People mark each time as available, available if needed or unavailable, see times in their own time zone and can suggest others. When it closes, you pick a time and the outcome goes out with a calendar invitation.

A Doodle group poll works much the same, with yes or if need be answers and calendar invites when you confirm. The difference is who answers: anyone with a Doodle link can vote without an account, while Loomio's voters are group members or guests invited to that one poll by email.

### What Doodle has that Loomio lacks

- **Booking pages, one-to-ones and sign-up sheets.**
- **Phone apps** for iPhone and Android; Loomio runs in a phone's browser.
- **Calendar connections** for people with a Doodle account.
- **Zapier and Stripe payments.**
