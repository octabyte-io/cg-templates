### OmniTools and iLovePDF pricing

As of November 2026, iLovePDF has three plans:

- **Free**: essential tools with limited processing, and no OCR or apps.
- **Premium**: US$5 a month billed yearly (US$60 a year), or US$9 billed monthly, for 1 to 25 users, adding OCR, Workflows, apps, no ads and advanced e-signatures.
- **Business**: priced by quote for 25 or more users.

OmniTools has no fee, no paid edition and no ads. The server only serves web pages, so the smallest one is enough.

### PDF tools: basic or complete

OmniTools covers the basic PDF jobs: merge, split, rotate, compress, add a password, turn PDFs into images and images into a PDF, and make small edits. It has no Word, Excel or PowerPoint conversion, no OCR for scanned PDFs and no e-signatures.

iLovePDF is built for PDFs. It organizes, compresses, converts to and from Office formats, edits, protects, redacts and signs PDFs, and its paid plans add OCR, Workflows and AI tools such as a summarizer. Its free plan limits each task; Merge PDF, for example, takes 25 files of up to 100 MB.

### More than PDFs

OmniTools is a general toolbox of 128 tools. Next to the PDF tools it can:

- Resize, compress, crop and rotate images, remove a background, make QR codes and read text from an image.
- Trim, crop, speed up or loop a video, merge clips and turn clips into GIFs.
- Change text case, compare texts, sort lists and generate passwords.
- Tidy up and convert JSON, CSV, XML, YAML and TSV, and work out dates.

iLovePDF sticks to PDFs.

### Privacy and where the work happens

OmniTools runs every tool in your own browser, so files stay on your device, and there is no sign-in. A few tools download their engine from public websites the first time you use them. Big video and PDF jobs depend on your computer or phone, so they can be slow on an older device.

iLovePDF's web tools upload files to its servers and, it says, delete them automatically within two hours. Its desktop app, on paid plans, works offline.
