### Cypht and Mailbird pricing

As of November 2026, Mailbird's pricing page shows, in US dollars:

- **Free**: $0, for one email account.
- **Premium Yearly**: $4.03 per user a month, billed yearly, shown as 30% off $5.75.
- **Premium Pay Once**: $99.75 per user, paid once, shown as 75% off $399. Lifetime updates are a $69 add-on.

Premium adds unlimited accounts, the unified inbox, Exchange, the calendar, integrations, email tracking, templates and filters. Every plan has a 14-day money-back guarantee, and buying several licences brings volume discounts.

Cypht has no licence fee and no limit on accounts or users. Your cost is the server it runs on.

### Cypht's web inbox or Mailbird's app

Cypht is webmail. It runs on your server, and you open it in any browser. It does not hold your mail: it reads your existing accounts over IMAP, JMAP or Exchange and shows them in one combined view, with search across every account. Your account settings and saved passwords stay on your server. Several people can share one Cypht, each with their own login.

Mailbird is an app you install. Mailbird Next runs on Windows 10 and 11 and on Mac, it keeps your mail on your computer, and its iPhone and Android apps sync with the desktop.

### Features beyond the inbox

Cypht adds an address book, a calendar, tags, scheduled sending, Sieve filters on servers that support them, an RSS reader and modules for new features. Its design is plain and made for a computer screen.

Mailbird's Premium plan adds more than 45 apps in a side panel, a calendar, snooze, email tracking, templates and opt-in AI that connects to an AI app you already use. Its phone apps focus on sorting mail into To Read and To Do lists.

### Cypht or Mailbird: who each suits

Cypht suits someone with several accounts, or a small team, who wants one inbox in the browser at no cost and is fine with a plainer design and no phone app. Mailbird suits someone who wants a polished app on a computer and phone, with integrations, and is happy to pay for Premium.
