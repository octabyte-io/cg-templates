### Black Candy and Spotify pricing

As of November 2026, Spotify's US site shows these plans, each billed monthly:

- **Free**: Spotify's ad-supported service.
- **Premium Individual**: $12.99 a month, after a free month for new subscribers.
- **Premium Duo**: $18.99 a month for two people at the same address.
- **Premium Family**: $21.99 a month for up to six people at the same address.
- **Premium Student**: $6.99 a month for eligible students.

Premium removes ads, lets you download songs, play albums in any order and skip as often as you like, and streams at up to 24-bit/44.1 kHz.

Black Candy has no licence fee, no paid edition and no paid app. Your cost is the server and the storage your music needs; on Cloudgate it runs on a small server.

### Your own music or Spotify's catalogue

Spotify streams its catalogue: you play almost anything, but you rent it, and you lose access when you stop paying. Spotify's apps can also play music files stored on your device.

Black Candy streams only the music you upload. You hear your own files, and nothing disappears when a licence ends. It sorts them into albums, artists, genres and years, and it can fetch artist and album pictures from Discogs with a free token. Files a browser can't play are converted as they stream, and lossless files can be shrunk to MP3 to save mobile data.

### Black Candy and Spotify phone apps

This is where Black Candy is closest to Spotify. Both have their own official apps, so nobody has to choose an app made by someone else. Black Candy's apps are free and open source, on the App Store, Google Play and F-Droid, and its web player also works in a phone's browser.

Spotify reaches further: it says it works with over 2,000 devices, with one-tap switching between them, and Premium adds offline downloads.

Black Candy keeps to its own apps. It does not support Subsonic, so third-party music apps cannot connect.

### Discovery and features

Spotify builds playlists for you: its DJ, daylist, Blend with friends, and Jam for listening together in real time.

Black Candy has no catalogue to recommend from. It offers search across artists, albums, songs and playlists, filters by genre or year, playlists, favourites, a play queue you can reorder, and a list of recently played albums for each person. It has no smart playlists, share links or Last.fm scrobbling.
