### Snipe-IT and Asset Panda pricing

As of November 2026, Asset Panda's pricing page shows no prices. It lists Asset Panda Pro, with its Ursa AI assistant, and Asset Panda Classic, and gives a custom quote based on what you need, followed by a 7-day free trial.

Self-hosted Snipe-IT is free, with every feature and no limit on assets or users. Its maker, Grokability, also hosts it, in US dollars, with unlimited users and assets on every plan:

- **Basic Hosting**: $399.99 a year or $39.99 a month.
- **Small Business Hosting**: $999.99 a year or $99.99 a month.
- **Dedicated Hosting**: from $2,499.99 a year or $249.99 a month.

You don't need a hosted plan to use Snipe-IT; it only adds support, backups and upgrades done for you.

### Tracking assets and who has them

Snipe-IT is built around IT equipment. Each laptop, phone or monitor has a record with its serial number, model, cost, warranty, end-of-life date and status. You check items out to a person, a location or another asset in one click, and staff can accept your terms with a signature. Software licences, accessories, consumables and components sit alongside hardware, and Snipe-IT works out depreciation.

Asset Panda is a general platform that you shape with custom fields and forms, so it can track more than IT equipment. It handles check-in and check-out, assignments and maintenance scheduling.

### Phones and barcode scanning

Asset Panda has iPhone and Android apps that scan barcode or QR labels, open records, take photos and work offline for some tasks.

Snipe-IT has no native app. It prints barcode and QR labels, and you look items up in a phone's browser or with a handheld scanner.

### Integrations and sign-in

Asset Panda connects to Microsoft Intune, Jamf Pro, Microsoft Entra ID, ServiceNow and QuickBooks, and has an API, webhooks and SFTP.

Snipe-IT syncs users from LDAP or Active Directory, supports SAML single sign-on and has a REST API. It does not scan your network for devices; you add, import or sync them.

### Hosting and your data

Asset Panda runs in its own cloud. Self-hosted Snipe-IT runs on a server you choose, so your inventory and staff records stay with you. You look after the server, its updates and its backups, or pay Grokability to host it for you.
