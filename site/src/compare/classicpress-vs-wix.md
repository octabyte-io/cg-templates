### ClassicPress and Wix pricing

As of November 2026, Wix has a free plan and four paid plans, priced per month in US dollars and billed yearly; monthly billing costs more:

- **Free**: a wixsite.com address, Wix branding, 500 MB of storage and no payments.
- **Light**: $17 a month ($24 monthly), with 2 GB of storage.
- **Core**: $29 a month ($36 monthly), with 50 GB, payments, basic eCommerce and bookings.
- **Business**: $39 a month ($46 monthly), with 100 GB.
- **Business Elite**: $159 a month ($172 monthly), with unlimited storage.

Wix says prices and currency vary by location, and taxes are added on top.

ClassicPress has no licence fee, no plans and no paid edition. You pay for the server, a domain name and any paid themes or plugins. On Cloudgate, a small server with 1 GB of memory runs a typical business site or blog.

### Writing pages: classic editor or drag and drop

The biggest difference is how you build pages. Wix has a drag-and-drop editor, over 2,000 templates and an AI builder that creates a site from a prompt.

ClassicPress is a fork of WordPress without the block editor. You pick a theme for the design, then write pages and posts in the classic editor, much like a word processor. There is no drag-and-drop page builder in its core. It adds media categories and tags, a view of where each file is used, and tools to clear out old revisions.

### Themes, plugins and community

Wix sites get extra features from the Wix App Market. ClassicPress has its own directory of themes and plugins, and most WordPress themes and plugins built for WordPress 6.2 or earlier work too. Ones that need blocks or WordPress 6.3 and later may not.

ClassicPress is run by volunteers and directed by the ClassicPress Initiative, a US 501(c)(3) non-profit, so its community is much smaller than WordPress's. Wix is a company and includes 24/7 customer care with its paid plans.

### Hosting and ownership

Wix's help centre says a Wix site needs to be hosted on Wix's servers; you can connect your own domain but not move the site. A ClassicPress site's files and database are yours to copy to any host. In return, you keep ClassicPress, your theme and your plugins up to date yourself.
