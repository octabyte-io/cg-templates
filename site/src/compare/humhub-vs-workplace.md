### Workplace from Meta has closed

Meta's own Workplace page now says one thing: "Workplace has closed as of May 2026." Workplace was Meta's social network for companies, with a news feed, groups, chat and video in an app that looked like Facebook. It was used especially by organizations with many frontline staff. Every organization that relied on it has had to move.

So this is less a contest than a question of what to use instead. HumHub is one answer for teams that want the same feed-and-groups idea on a server they control.

### HumHub and Workplace pricing

Workplace is no longer sold, so it has no price to compare.

As of November 2026, HumHub has three editions:

- **Community Edition**: free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with no fee per member. You pay only for the server.
- **Professional Edition**: priced by number of users, in tiers from 50 to 10,000 users. HumHub's pricing page shows an example of up to 100 users, hosted by HumHub and billed monthly, at €144 a month excluding VAT. Hosting by HumHub adds €36 a month; running it on your own server adds nothing. Paying yearly takes 10% off, and onboarding packages cost €1,575 or €2,575 once.
- **Enterprise Edition**: priced on request.

The Professional Edition adds premium support, long-term support for the latest two major releases, and modules such as Advanced LDAP, SAML single sign-on and SOLR search.

### Hosting and control

Workplace ran only on Meta's cloud, which is why Meta could close it for everyone at once. HumHub runs on a server you choose, so your posts, files and member data stay with you and nobody can switch it off. The trade-off is that you look after the server, its updates and its backups, unless you pay HumHub GmbH to host it.

### Feed, spaces and profiles

HumHub covers the core of what Workplace did. Members get profiles, a people directory and a shared news feed. Work happens in spaces for teams, departments, projects or groups, with posts, comments, likes, mentions and files. Notifications arrive in the app and as email summaries, and full-text search finds posts, people and spaces.

Around 80 modules add a wiki, calendar, tasks, polls, photo galleries, direct messages and more; some of them are paid. HumHub is available in over 30 languages.

### Chat, video and phone apps

Workplace had chat and video calls. HumHub does not: it is a place for news and discussion, and its Mail module adds direct messages. If your team held meetings or chatted all day in Workplace, plan a separate tool for that.

HumHub's free official apps for iPhone, iPad and Android connect to your own HumHub. They add push notifications, open HumHub links straight in the app and let you share into HumHub from other apps.
