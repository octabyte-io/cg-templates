### Louez and Booqable pricing

As of November 2026, Booqable's pricing page shows three plans, in US dollars, or euros for visitors in the EU:

- **Start**: $29 a month billed yearly, or $35 billed monthly. Order and inventory management, quotes, contracts and invoices, a booking page and website integration.
- **Grow**: $69 a month billed yearly, or $87 billed monthly. Adds 5 extra users with permissions, e-signatures, downtime scheduling, advanced reports and API access.
- **Scale**: $149 a month billed yearly, or $187 billed monthly. Adds 10 extra users, activity logs, extra locations and enforced two-step sign-in.

Every plan has unlimited products and orders and no commission on bookings. Add-ons cost extra: the website builder is $19 a month billed yearly ($24 monthly), and the iPhone and Android point-of-sale app is $9 per user a month billed yearly ($12 monthly). There is a 14-day free trial.

Self-hosted Louez is free under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with no plan limits. You pay for the server and Stripe's fees if you take card payments. Louez also sells a hosted cloud: pay-as-you-go at 4% of rentals, or subscriptions from $49 a month with no commission.

### Online storefront and rental bookings

Both give customers a place to pick dates, see what is free and book. Louez gives each shop a branded storefront with its logo and colours, a cart, a checkout and a portal where customers track their bookings. Booqable offers a ready-made booking page and a widget for your existing website, and sells a website builder with templates.

### Inventory, contracts and documents

Louez covers products with photos, categories, quantities, pricing tiers, seasonal prices and promo codes, plus PDF contracts with the deposit you set, automated emails, staff roles and revenue statistics.

Booqable goes further for larger stock. It adds barcode and QR code scanning, maintenance downtime, product bundles, pull sheets, tax profiles, invoices and e-signatures, and lists more than 60 features on its higher plans.

### Hosting and support

Self-hosted Louez runs on your server, and you look after its updates and backups. It needs about 2 GB of memory with its database and image storage. Booqable runs everything for you, with chat and email support on every plan and onboarding sessions on Grow and Scale.
