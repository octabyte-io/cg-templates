Adobe XD is Adobe's tool for designing and prototyping websites and apps. Designers drew screens on artboards, linked them into clickable prototypes and shared them with developers and clients for review.

People look for an alternative because Adobe put XD in maintenance mode in 2023: it gets bug and security fixes but no new features, and it is no longer sold on its own. Teams that still have XD work want a tool that is actively developed. A self-hosted, open-source design tool such as Penpot does the same job in the browser, with no seat price, and keeps your files on your own server.
