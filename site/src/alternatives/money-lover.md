Money Lover is a phone app for tracking spending and income: you log each expense in a category, keep separate wallets for cash, cards and savings, and see where your money goes in charts, in several currencies. Your records are kept in the maker's cloud and synced between your devices.

People look for an alternative when they want those records on a server they control rather than in an app company's cloud, or when they want the same quick money diary on a computer as well as a phone, with no subscription. A self-hosted ezBookkeeping records spending from any phone or browser and keeps every transaction on your own server, though it has no budgets or bank sync.
