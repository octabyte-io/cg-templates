Salsify is a hosted product experience management platform for brands that sell through retailers, combining product information with syndication to retailers and digital-shelf analytics. It does not publish prices; plans are sold after a demo.

Smaller brands, wholesalers and shops look for an alternative when they mainly need one tidy product catalogue, not a full retail network. A self-hosted UnoPim keeps product data, images and translations in one place with no licence fee, and sends it to your channels by file, API or connector.
