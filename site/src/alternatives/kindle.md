Kindle is Amazon's e-book platform: a store, the Kindle e-readers, and Kindle apps that keep the books you buy in your Amazon library and sync your place between devices.

People look for an alternative to the Kindle library when their e-books come from many places, not only Amazon's store. Books bought from Kindle are copy-protected and can only be read in Amazon's apps and devices, and a household can share them only in the ways Amazon allows. Readers who own DRM-free e-books from other shops, public-domain collections or their own files want them all in one place they control. A self-hosted e-book library such as Calibre-Web keeps those books on your own server, readable from any browser and shareable with family or a book club, though it can't sell you books or open Kindle purchases.
