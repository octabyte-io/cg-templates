Confluence is Atlassian's team wiki, often used alongside Jira for documentation, meeting notes and project pages. Content is organised into spaces, with page trees, templates and a large marketplace of add-ons.

Teams look for an alternative for two main reasons. Atlassian ended support for Confluence Server in February 2024, so the only self-hosted option left is the Data Center edition, which is priced for large organisations. And Confluence Cloud charges per user once a team grows past the free plan, which covers up to 10 users. Many teams also find the editor and page trees heavier than they need. A self-hosted wiki such as Outline keeps your documents on your own server, with no price per user.
