Typefully is a writing and scheduling app for social posts, popular with creators who write threads, publishing to X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon and Instagram with analytics and an API.

People look for an alternative because Typefully is a paid subscription service and keeps your drafts on its platform. Shoutrrr covers the same writing loop, a live character count, automatic threads, a posting queue and analytics, on your own server with no fee per seat or account. In return you register your own developer app with X, LinkedIn and Meta's networks, and X charges for using its API.
