MindManager is business mind-mapping software with project features, such as Gantt charts, task data and links to Microsoft Office, licensed per user.

People look for an alternative when they mainly draw and share mind maps and a licence for every user is hard to justify, or when they want their maps on their own server. A self-hosted WiseMapping opens MindManager files and lets a whole team build and share maps with no fee per user.
