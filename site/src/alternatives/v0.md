v0 is Vercel's hosted AI tool for building user interfaces and web apps from a prompt, aimed at React apps and priced in credits.

People look for a v0 alternative when they mainly want quick designs and prototypes rather than a full app, want to choose the AI model, or want their projects and keys on their own server. Open Design makes web pages, prototypes and slide decks from a prompt on your own server, and you pay your AI provider directly.
