Deputy is workforce management software: scheduling, time and attendance, timesheets, labour-law rules for breaks and overtime, payroll links and team messaging for shift-based businesses. It is a paid subscription service.

People look for an alternative when they pay for a full workforce suite but mainly use the rota. A self-hosted BetterShift covers planning and sharing shifts with no subscription, on your own server. It is not a replacement for Deputy's time clock, timesheets or payroll, so it suits small teams that handle hours another way.
