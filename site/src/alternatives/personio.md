Personio is an HR platform for small and medium businesses, especially in Europe: employee files, absence management, recruiting, onboarding, performance reviews and payroll preparation, with workflows that connect them.

People look for an alternative when its subscription no longer fits their budget, or when they want staff records on a server they control rather than in a vendor's cloud. OrangeHRM Starter covers employee records, leave, timesheets, recruiting and reviews on your own server with no subscription or fee per employee; in return, it has no payroll preparation or onboarding and its screens are less polished.
