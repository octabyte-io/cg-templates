GoLinks is a hosted go links service: people in a company type go/ and a short name, such as go/payroll, in their browser to open a long address, with a browser extension, phone apps and integrations with workplace tools.

Teams look for an alternative because GoLinks charges per user each month for features many teams want, such as collections, tags and private links; when we checked its pricing page in November 2026, those came with the Pro plan at $4 per user a month. Some also prefer to keep the list of their internal links on their own server. A self-hosted Slash gives a small team short, memorable link names, tags, collections and visit counts for free, as long as the team is no larger than five accounts.
