Rebrandly is a branded link management service: it puts short links on your own domain and tracks how often they are clicked.

People look for an alternative because Rebrandly's plans limit how many links and branded domains you have, and when we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed 10 links a month on one custom domain. Paid plans raise those limits for a monthly fee. A self-hosted Kutt gives you branded short links on as many domains as you like, with no link limit, and keeps your link data on your own server.
