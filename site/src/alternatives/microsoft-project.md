Microsoft Project is Microsoft's project planning software, built around Gantt charts, task dependencies, resources and baselines, sold as per-user subscriptions alongside Microsoft Planner.

Organisations look for an alternative for cost and change. Every planner needs a paid licence, and Microsoft retired its Project Online service on 30 September 2026, pushing its customers to move their plans somewhere else. OpenProject plans projects with Gantt charts in the browser, with no fee per user in its Community edition, and keeps the plans on your own server.
