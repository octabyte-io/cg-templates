Mailbird is a paid email app for Windows and Mac that brings several email accounts into one unified inbox, with built-in connections to calendars and other apps.

People look for an alternative when they want that one inbox without a desktop app or a subscription, or want to read their mail from any browser on any computer. A self-hosted Cypht gives them a combined inbox for all their accounts in the browser, at no cost, and keeps their account settings and saved passwords on their own server.
