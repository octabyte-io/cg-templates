Thinkific is a hosted platform for creating and selling online courses, with a course builder, quizzes, certificates, a website builder and checkout.

People look for an alternative because their courses and student list stay on Thinkific's platform, under the plans and prices Thinkific sets. A self-hosted CourseLit builds and sells the same kind of course from your own website, takes no fee of its own and keeps students and content on your server.
