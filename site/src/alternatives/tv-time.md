TV Time is an app for tracking the TV shows and films you watch, episode by episode, with reminders for new episodes and a community that reacts to each one.

People look for an alternative because they want their watch history kept privately on their own server rather than in a company's app. A self-hosted Watcharr tracks shows, episodes and films with ratings and stats, for free, though it works in the browser with no phone app and has no TV Time import.
