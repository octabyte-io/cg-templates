Skylight Calendar is a touchscreen that hangs in the kitchen and shows the family's calendar, chores and lists. The screen is bought once, and an optional Skylight Plus subscription adds meal planning, rewards, a photo screensaver and more.

Families look for an alternative when they already own a tablet and don't want to buy a dedicated screen, or don't want a yearly subscription for features such as meal planning and rewards. A self-hosted Yuvomi turns a tablet you already have into the kitchen display, with the same calendar, chores and meals on everyone's phone, and keeps the family's plans on your own server.
