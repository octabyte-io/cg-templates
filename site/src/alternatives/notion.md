Notion is an all-in-one workspace: documents, wikis, databases, boards and calendars on pages you can nest and link together. Many teams use it as their company wiki.

Teams look for an alternative for a few reasons. Notion charges per member, so the bill grows with every hire, and its AI features have been an extra cost on many plans. Large workspaces can feel slow, and pages tend to sprawl when everything can be anything. And the whole workspace lives on Notion's servers. If what your team mostly needs is a tidy, searchable wiki, a self-hosted document tool such as Outline does that job on your own server with no price per member.
