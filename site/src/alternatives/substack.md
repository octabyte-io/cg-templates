Substack is a publishing platform for newsletters. Writers publish posts that go to a web page and to subscribers' inboxes, and can charge for paid subscriptions, with Substack handling the website, payments and email.

Writers look for an alternative mainly because of the fee: Substack keeps 10% of paid subscription revenue, which becomes a large sum as a newsletter grows. Others want their own design and domain, more control over their brand, or to keep their reader list somewhere they control. A self-hosted Ghost site does the same job of website, newsletter and paid memberships, with payments going straight to your own Stripe account and no share taken.
