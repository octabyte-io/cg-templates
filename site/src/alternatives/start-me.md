start.me is a browser start page: you collect bookmarks, news feeds and widgets on pages that open whenever you start your browser, and teams can share pages with everyone.

People look for an alternative because start.me's free plan shows ads and allows only a few pages, while unlimited pages and the extra widgets sit on paid plans. Some also want the list of tools their team uses kept off a third-party service. A self-hosted Dashy or Glance gives you an ad-free start page with no page limit, on your own server.
