Audible, from Amazon, is the best-known audiobook store and app. A monthly membership gives you credits to buy audiobooks and access to a catalogue of included titles, and the app keeps your place and plays them on your phone and computer.

People look for an alternative because the membership is a monthly cost and the books you buy are copy-protected, so they only play in Audible's own apps. Families with audiobooks from other shops, CDs or free sources also want them in one place. A self-hosted audiobook server such as Audiobookshelf plays your own DRM-free audiobooks on every device with no membership, though it has no store, so it complements a shop rather than replacing one.
