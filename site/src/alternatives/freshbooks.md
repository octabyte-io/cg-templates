FreshBooks is invoicing and accounting software aimed at freelancers, consultants and small service businesses. It is known for easy invoices, time tracking, projects and expense tracking, with phone apps.

People look for an alternative when the business grows. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, plans ran from $23 to $70 a month and each extra team member cost $11 a month. Businesses that sell products also need inventory and fuller accounting. A self-hosted Bigcapital adds stock tracking and complete double-entry books with no fee per person, and keeps your records on your own server.
