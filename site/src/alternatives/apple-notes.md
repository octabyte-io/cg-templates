moving: |
  There is no Apple Notes import. Open each note you want to keep, copy its text and paste it into a new note in flatnotes, or drag its pictures in. Instead of folders, add a hashtag to each note, such as #home or #work, and use tag search to see a group of notes together; pin the notes you open most to the home page. On your iPhone or Android phone, add flatnotes to the home screen from the browser so it opens like an app. Leave the old notes in Apple Notes as an archive until you are sure everything has moved. DumbPad has no import either: create a notepad for each topic you kept in a folder and paste the text in, then add it to your phone's home screen from the browser.

Apple Notes is the free notes app built into iPhone, iPad and Mac. It syncs through iCloud, and adds folders, checklists, sketches, scanned documents, locked notes and notes shared with other people.

People look for an alternative when they also use Windows or Android, where Apple Notes is only available through the iCloud website, or when they want their notes on a server they control rather than in iCloud. A self-hosted flatnotes opens in any browser on any device and keeps every note as a plain Markdown file you can move to another app at any time, in exchange for fewer features and no offline app.
