AccuRanker is a dedicated rank tracker for SEO teams and agencies, known for fast, on-demand ranking updates, SERP-feature tracking and share-of-voice reports, priced by the number of keywords you track.

People look for an alternative because the price rises with every keyword, so a large keyword list or many client sites quickly cost more each month. A self-hosted SerpBear tracks unlimited keywords on your own server, and you pay a scraping service only for the checks you run.
