SignUpGenius is a hosted service for online sign-up sheets: volunteer shifts, potlucks, school events, parent-teacher conferences and more, free with ads, with paid plans for more features.

Event organisers look for an alternative to get rid of the ads volunteers see, to avoid paying for features as an event grows, and to keep volunteers' names, emails and phone numbers on their own server. Helfertool, made by volunteers for festivals and clubs, runs shift sign-ups for events with no ads or limits, plus badges, gift tracking and coordinator access. It does one job: shifts at events, not every kind of sign-up sheet.
