Hotjar is a hosted tool for seeing how visitors use a website, best known for session recordings, heatmaps of clicks and scrolls, and on-site surveys and feedback.

People look for an alternative when they want recordings of their visitors kept on their own server rather than with an outside service, or want recordings and visitor stats in one tool instead of two. A self-hosted Rybbit records sessions with form entries hidden, adds funnels, journeys and cookie-free visitor stats, and stores it all on your server; in return, it has no heatmaps or surveys, and you run the server.
