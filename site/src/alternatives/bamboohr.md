BambooHR is one of the best-known HR systems for small and medium businesses: employee records, time off, hiring, onboarding and performance in one place, with payroll and benefits available in the US.

People look for an alternative when a subscription HR system no longer fits their budget, or when they want staff records, salaries and documents on a server they control. OrangeHRM Starter covers employee records, leave, timesheets, hiring and reviews on your own server with no fee per employee; in return, it has no payroll or onboarding.
