EngagementHQ is a hosted community engagement platform from Granicus, which bought its maker, Bang the Table, in 2021. Councils and agencies use it for surveys, forums, mapping and project pages, sold by subscription.

Organisations look for an alternative when the subscription is a large share of a small budget, or when they would rather keep residents' data on their own server. A self-hosted Decidim does consultations, proposals and participatory budgets with no licence fee, though you set it up and run it yourselves.
