Pinboard is a paid bookmarking service for people who want a fast, private place to save links. You tag what you save, mark it read or unread, and search your collection later; a higher-priced account adds an archived copy of each page and search inside those pages. Its code is not public.

People look for an alternative when they would rather not pay a yearly fee for a list of links, or would rather keep their bookmarks on a server they control. Others want an account for each person in a family or team. A self-hosted linkding keeps the same plain, tag-based way of working, with notes and a read-later flag, adds user accounts and shared bookmarks, and keeps everything on your own server.
