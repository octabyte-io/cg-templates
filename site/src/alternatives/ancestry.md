Ancestry is a subscription genealogy service: people build family trees on it, search its historical record collections, get record hints, and take its DNA tests.

People look for an alternative when they want to keep their tree, photos and family stories without a subscription, or want their family's data on their own server rather than a genealogy company's. A self-hosted Gramps Web keeps the tree private to the relatives you invite; in return, it has no record collections or DNA tests, so you bring your own research.
