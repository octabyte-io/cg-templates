Intercom is a customer messaging platform: a chat widget for your website, a shared inbox, a help centre and an AI agent called Fin that answers customers.

Businesses look for an alternative because of cost. Intercom charges per support seat, and Fin charges for each conversation it resolves, so the bill grows with both your team and your customers. Smaller teams often use only the chat widget and inbox. A self-hosted app such as Chatwoot covers those with no price per agent, and keeps customer conversations on your own server.
