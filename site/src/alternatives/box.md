Box is cloud file sharing for businesses: shared folders, permissions, links for people outside the company, and integrations with many other tools.

Businesses look for an alternative because Box is charged per user, with at least three users on its business plans and a file size limit on each plan. Some also want their files on a server they control. Seafile syncs and shares files for a team on your own server, with no price per user.
