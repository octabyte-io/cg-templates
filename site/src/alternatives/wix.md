Wix is a hosted website builder: you pick a template, drag elements onto the page and Wix runs the site, with add-ons for shops, bookings and blogs.

People look for an alternative when the monthly plan gets expensive, especially once they need a custom domain without Wix ads, a shop or more storage. The bigger concern is lock-in: a Wix site cannot be exported, so leaving later means rebuilding it. And you can only use the features and apps Wix offers. Self-hosted WordPress, with WooCommerce for a shop, has no platform fee, works with any theme or plugin, and can be moved to another host whenever you like. Joomla and Drupal are options for sites in several languages, Drupal for large, structured sites with many editors, and Grav a lighter one for small sites written in Markdown.
