Wix is a hosted website builder: you pick a template, drag elements onto the page and Wix runs the site, with add-ons for shops, bookings and blogs.

People look for an alternative when the monthly plan gets expensive, especially once they need a custom domain without Wix ads, a shop or more storage. The bigger concern is lock-in: a Wix site cannot be exported, so leaving later means rebuilding it. And you can only use the features and apps Wix offers. Self-hosted WordPress has no platform fee, works with any theme or plugin, and can be moved to another host whenever you like.
