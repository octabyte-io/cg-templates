iLovePDF is a popular online PDF toolbox. On its website you can merge, split, compress, convert, rotate, watermark, unlock and sign PDFs, one tool per task, and it also has desktop and phone apps and a paid plan.

People look for an alternative because the free plan caps file sizes and how many files go into one task, the paid plan charges per user, and every document is uploaded to iLovePDF's servers. For contracts, invoices or personal records many businesses would rather keep files in-house. A self-hosted toolbox such as Stirling PDF offers the same tools in the browser, on a server you control.
