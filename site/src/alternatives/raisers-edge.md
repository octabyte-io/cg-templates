Raiser's Edge NXT is Blackbaud's fundraising and donor management system for larger nonprofits, with constituent records, gifts, campaigns, prospect research and analytics, and links to the rest of Blackbaud's software.

Nonprofits look for an alternative because Raiser's Edge NXT is sold on quote-based subscriptions that can be costly for a mid-sized organisation, and because the data lives in Blackbaud's cloud. CiviCRM is an open-source CRM for nonprofits that keeps donors, gifts, memberships, events and email on your own server with no licence fee. It has less built-in prospect research and analytics.
