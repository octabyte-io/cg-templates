AssoConnect is French all-in-one software for associations: members, fees, accounting, online payments, emails and a website, as a hosted service with a free plan and paid plans.

Associations look for an alternative to stop paying a subscription as they grow and to keep their members' details on a server they control. Paheko, developed in the open since 2012 for associations, does members, fees, email reminders and accounting in one free tool. It asks for a little more of the treasurer, who keeps double-entry books, and online payments go through the HelloAsso extension instead of being built in.
