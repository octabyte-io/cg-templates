Vimeo is a video hosting service for creators and businesses: an ad-free player, privacy settings, embeds, analytics, live streaming and tools for marketing and selling videos.

People look for an alternative because Vimeo's business features come with paid plans, and because their videos, especially internal training or teaching videos, sit on a service they do not control. A self-hosted MediaCMS gives an organisation its own video site with private and group-only videos and embeds, with no fee per viewer, seat or upload; in return, you provide the server, its disk and its bandwidth.
