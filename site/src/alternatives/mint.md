Mint was a free personal finance app from Intuit. It connected to your bank and card accounts, sorted spending into categories, and showed budgets, bills and your credit score in one place. Intuit shut Mint down in 2024 and moved its users to Credit Karma.

People who used Mint look for a replacement that keeps the same everyday view of their money: every account, where the money went this month, and how each budget is doing. Many also want that history kept somewhere it cannot be switched off. Budget Board was written as a self-hosted Mint replacement and keeps your accounts and transactions on your own server. The trade-off is that bank sync is not built in: you connect a paid sync service or import files.
