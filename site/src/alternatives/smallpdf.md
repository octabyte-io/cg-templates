Smallpdf is an online PDF service: you open its website, choose a tool such as compress, merge or convert to Word, drop in a file and download the result. It also offers e-signatures, phone apps and a paid plan for teams.

People look for an alternative because the free plan limits how many tasks you can run, and the paid plan is charged per user. Every file is also uploaded to Smallpdf's servers, which many businesses don't want for contracts, payslips or medical records. A self-hosted PDF toolbox works the same way, but the files are processed on your own server and there is no cap on how many you work on by hand.
