Convertio is an online file converter that works in the browser, with a free tier and paid plans for bigger files and more conversions.

People look for an alternative because of its plan limits. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, paid plans started at $11.99 a month for 1,000 credits and files up to 1 GB, and larger files needed higher plans. Every file is uploaded to Convertio's servers. A self-hosted ConvertX has no credits or file size plans, and your files stay on your own server.
