AnyList is a grocery list and recipe app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and the web. A household shares lists that update as items are added or ticked off, and recipes can add their ingredients to a list. Its meal-planning calendar is part of the paid AnyList Complete plan.

People look for an alternative when they would rather not pay a subscription for planning meals, or want the household's lists and recipes on a server of their own. A self-hosted KitchenOwl keeps a shared grocery list, recipes, a meal plan and shared expenses on your server for free, with phone apps for everyone in the house, though it is younger software than AnyList.
