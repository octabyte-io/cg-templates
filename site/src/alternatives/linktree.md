Linktree is a link-in-bio service: one page with buttons to everything you share, for the single link that Instagram, TikTok and other profiles allow.

People look for an alternative because its useful extras sit on paid plans. When we checked Linktree's pricing page in November 2026, it had Free, Starter, Pro and Premium plans; advanced analytics, commerce and marketing integrations were on the higher plans, and removing the Linktree branding needed Premium. Others want the page on their own domain. A self-hosted LinkStack gives you the page, buttons and themes for free on your own address.
