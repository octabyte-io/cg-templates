Dropbox file requests let you send someone a link so they can upload files straight into a folder in your Dropbox.

People look for an alternative when they collect documents from clients often but do not use Dropbox for anything else, or want those documents on their own server. A self-hosted Sharry gives each request its own upload page, and the same app sends files back out by expiring link.
