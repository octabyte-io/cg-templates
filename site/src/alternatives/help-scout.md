Help Scout is customer support software built around a shared inbox. Customer emails arrive as conversations, and the team assigns, answers and notes them together. Plans add a knowledge base, live chat and reports.

Teams look for an alternative because Help Scout charges per user each month once you pass its free plan, which covers 5 users and one inbox. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, paid plans started at $25 per user a month, so the bill grows with every person who answers customers. FreeScout was built to work the same way on your own server, with no price per user; you pay once for only the extra modules you need.
