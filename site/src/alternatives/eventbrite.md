Eventbrite is a ticketing platform and event marketplace: organisers build an event page and sell tickets, and buyers can also find events by browsing Eventbrite itself. It handles payment and sends buyers their tickets.

Organisers look for an alternative mostly because of the fees. When we checked its US pricing page in November 2026, Eventbrite added a service fee of 3.7% plus $1.79 to each paid ticket, with 2.9% payment processing on top, so the platform's cut grows with every sale. Free events carry no fee. Many organisers also want the attendee list and the buyer relationship to stay with them rather than on a marketplace that shows buyers other events. A self-hosted Hi.Events sells tickets from your own pages, pays out to your own Stripe account and charges no platform fee, though you bring the audience yourself.
