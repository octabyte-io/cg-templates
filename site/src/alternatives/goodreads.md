Goodreads, owned by Amazon, is the best-known site for keeping track of books: you shelve what you have read and want to read, rate and review books, set a yearly reading challenge and see what friends are reading.

Readers look for an alternative because their reading history sits in an Amazon-owned account. A self-hosted Ryot keeps a private record of every book you read on your own server and brings your Goodreads history with it; BookOrbit keeps that record next to the e-books and audiobooks you own. Both replace the private tracking side of Goodreads, not the social network.
