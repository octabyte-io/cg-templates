Goodreads, owned by Amazon, is the best-known site for keeping track of books: you shelve what you have read and want to read, rate and review books, set a yearly reading challenge and see what friends are reading.

Readers look for an alternative because their reading history sits in an Amazon-owned account, and many want it next to the books they actually own. A self-hosted BookOrbit keeps your library, reading progress, ratings, goals and statistics together on your own server. It replaces the private tracking side of Goodreads, not the social network.
