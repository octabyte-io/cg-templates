Semrush is an all-in-one SEO and marketing suite: keyword research, backlink analysis, site audits, competitor research, content tools and Position Tracking, which checks where your pages rank for chosen keywords.

Many people look for an alternative because they mainly use it to follow their rankings, yet pay a monthly plan for the whole suite, with the number of tracked keywords and projects capped by plan. A self-hosted SerpBear does the rank-tracking part for as many sites and keywords as you like, and you pay a scraping service only for the checks you run.
