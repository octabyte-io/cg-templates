Miro is a hosted online whiteboard where teams brainstorm with sticky notes, run workshops from templates, map ideas and plan projects together on an endless canvas.

People look for an alternative because of its limits and price. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the free plan kept only the three most recent boards editable, and paid plans were priced per member, from $8 a month on Starter billed yearly. A teacher, tutor or small team that mainly needs to sketch and explain ideas together does not need all of that. A self-hosted WBO gives everyone a shared board in the browser with no board limit and no fee per person; in return, it is far simpler than Miro, with drawing tools only.
