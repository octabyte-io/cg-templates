Editorial Manager, from Aries Systems, is a web-based system for tracking manuscripts through submission and peer review, used by many publishers and societies and priced by quote.

Journals look for an alternative when they publish on their own and want to stop paying for an enterprise system, or want the editorial workflow and the published journal in one place. Open Journal Systems does both on your own server, with no licence fee.
