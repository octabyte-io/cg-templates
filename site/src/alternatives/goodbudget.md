Goodbudget is a hosted envelope budgeting app for individuals and couples: you divide your income into virtual envelopes and take each purchase out of one, with phone apps that keep a household's budget in sync.

People look for an alternative because Goodbudget's free plan limits how many envelopes and accounts you have, and the rest comes with its paid Plus subscription. Some also want their budget on their own server. A self-hosted OpenBudgeteer uses the same envelope method with no limits and no subscription, though you use it in a browser and import your bank's CSV files instead of using a phone app.
