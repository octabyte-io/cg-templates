Invision Community, formerly IP.Board, is a paid community suite: forums plus blogs, galleries, commerce and more, sold as hosted plans or a self-hosted licence renewed each year.

People look for an alternative when they mainly use the forums and the yearly cost no longer makes sense for a club or hobby community. MyBB is free, open-source forum software with boards, threads, private messages and moderation, on your own server and with no licence fee, though it is a forum only.
