Invision Community is a paid community suite: forums at its centre, plus blogs, galleries, file downloads, a store and paid memberships, either hosted by Invision or licensed to run on your own server.

People look for an alternative when they mainly use the forums and don't want to keep paying for the whole suite. phpBB is free, open-source forum software that covers the forum part with no licence fee, on your own domain, though everything around the forum needs other tools.
