Front is a shared inbox for teams: customer email, chat, WhatsApp and other channels arrive in one place, where people assign, discuss and answer them together.

Teams look for an alternative because Front charges per seat, so the bill grows with every person who answers customers. Libredesk gives a support team the same kind of shared inbox for email, chat and WhatsApp on your own server, with no fee per agent and no paid tiers.
