Cozi Family Organizer is a family app with a shared calendar, shopping lists, to-do lists and a meal planner with recipes, used on phones and in the browser. The free version shows ads, and Cozi Gold, a paid yearly plan, removes them and adds features such as month view and calendar search.

Families look for a Cozi alternative to get rid of the ads without a subscription, or to keep the household's plans on a server of their own rather than in someone else's cloud. A self-hosted Yuvomi does the same everyday jobs in one private app and adds what Cozi does not have: chores that earn children reward points, a household budget with shared-cost splitting, a pantry with best-before reminders and a kitchen wall display.
