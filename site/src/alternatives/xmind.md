faq:
  - q: "Can WiseMapping open XMind files?"
    a: "Yes. WiseMapping imports .xmind files from its map list, as well as MindManager, FreeMind, Freeplane and OPML files, and it exports maps to PDF, PNG, SVG, Markdown or text."
  - q: "Does WiseMapping work offline like the XMind app?"
    a: "Not the self-hosted version, which runs in the browser on computers, tablets and phones. A separate WiseMapping Desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux keeps maps offline on one computer."
  - q: "Is WiseMapping open source?"
    a: "Not strictly: it is source-available. Its code is public under the WiseMapping Public License, which is the Apache 2.0 licence plus one rule: the powered by WiseMapping notice stays on every screen. It is free to use, with every feature included and no limit on maps or users."

XMind is mind-mapping software for computers and phones, with many map types, such as fishbone, timeline and matrix diagrams, and themes, sold as a subscription.

People look for an alternative when they want maps a team opens in the browser and shares, rather than files on one computer, or when they don't want another subscription. A self-hosted WiseMapping opens your XMind files and keeps the maps on your own server, shared with the people who need them.
