XMind is mind-mapping software for computers and phones, with many map types, such as fishbone, timeline and matrix diagrams, and themes, sold as a subscription.

People look for an alternative when they want maps a team opens in the browser and shares, rather than files on one computer, or when they don't want another subscription. A self-hosted WiseMapping opens your XMind files and keeps the maps on your own server, shared with the people who need them. Drawnix suits people who make mind maps now and then: it makes mind maps and flowcharts on one free whiteboard in any browser, with nothing to install, though it is a much simpler mind-mapping tool than XMind and cannot open XMind files.
