Fathom is an AI note taker for video calls: it records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams meetings with a bot that joins for you, then writes a transcript, a summary and action items.

People look for an alternative when they would rather not have a bot record client and team calls onto someone else's servers, or want the notes in a place they control. A self-hosted Speakr keeps recordings, transcripts and summaries on your own server, though you start the recording yourself.
