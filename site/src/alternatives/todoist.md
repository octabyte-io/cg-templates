Todoist is a popular to-do list app for personal and team tasks, with projects, due dates, labels and quick task entry on every device.

People look for an alternative because many features sit behind its paid plans. When we checked Todoist's pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed five personal projects and three filters and left out the calendar layout and custom reminders. Others want their task list on their own server. Vikunja has every view and feature in its free app, with no project limit.
