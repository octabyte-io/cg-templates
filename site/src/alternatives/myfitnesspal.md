MyFitnessPal is a calorie counter and nutrition tracker. You log meals against a large food database, follow calorie and macro goals and track your weight, on its phone apps and website, with more features in its paid Premium plan.

People look for an alternative because the free version shows ads and keeps features for Premium, and because a daily food diary and body measurements are personal data held by someone else. A self-hosted wger logs meals, nutrition plans and weight on your own server with no ads or subscription, and adds workout planning, though its food database is smaller.
