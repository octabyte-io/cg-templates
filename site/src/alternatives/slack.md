Slack is the best-known team chat app: channels for topics and projects, threads, direct messages, huddles for calls and a large directory of integrations.

Teams look for an alternative mostly because of price and history. The free plan only shows the last 90 days of messages and files, so older decisions and documents disappear from view unless you pay. Paid plans are charged per active user each month, which adds up as a team grows. Some organisations also need their conversations kept on their own servers for privacy or compliance. A self-hosted chat app such as Mattermost, Zulip, Rocket.Chat, a Matrix server with Element, Campfire, Chatto or the source-available Nebulynk keeps your full history, has no price per user for the server, and stores every message on your own server.
