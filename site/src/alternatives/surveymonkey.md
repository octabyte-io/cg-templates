SurveyMonkey is one of the best-known online survey tools. You build a survey from templates, send it by link or email, and read the results in charts; it adds paid features such as a panel of people you can pay to answer, and benchmarks against other companies.

People look for an alternative because many of SurveyMonkey's features sit behind paid plans and its team plans are charged per user, while every answer is stored on its servers. A self-hosted survey tool such as Formbricks has no per-user or per-response price and keeps the answers on your own server; in return, it has no panel or benchmark data and you run the server yourself.
