Brandfolder, owned by Smartsheet, is a hosted digital asset management (DAM) platform for brand assets. Teams use it to store and share logos, photos, videos and documents, with brand guidelines, embeds served from a delivery network and analytics on how assets are used, and it is sold by quote.

Teams look for an alternative when they mainly need a well-organised media library and don't want a quoted subscription for it, or when their files must stay on their own server. A self-hosted Phraseanet catalogues photos, videos, audio and documents with detailed metadata and fast search, controls who sees what, and shares files for review and download, at no licence fee. It has fewer marketing features than Brandfolder and an older interface.
