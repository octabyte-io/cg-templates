Splashtop is remote access and support software with fast native apps for desktops and phones, sold as subscriptions for remote work, IT support and classrooms.

People look for an alternative because Splashtop is priced per user or technician on monthly or yearly plans, and connections go through its service. A self-hosted MeshCentral handles remote control, terminal and file tasks for as many machines as you like on your own server, with no licence fee.
