Axiell Collections is paid collections management software for archives and museums, and the successor to Axiell CALM, which is no longer sold to new customers.

Archives look for an alternative when the move from CALM forces a new system anyway, or to avoid licence and hosting fees. ArchivesSpace is free, open-source archives management built by and for archivists, with no licence fee, though it covers archival collections only.
