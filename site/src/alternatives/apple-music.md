Apple Music is Apple's music streaming service: for a monthly fee you play songs from its catalogue on Apple devices, Android, the web and speakers, and it can also keep your own song files in the same library.

People look for an alternative for a few reasons. The subscription never ends, the catalogue is rented, so songs can disappear when a licence runs out, and the music you own sits next to it in Apple's service. Collectors and families with their own files want to hear them in the quality they ripped or bought them, on any device. A self-hosted music server such as Koel or Black Candy streams that collection to every browser and phone with no subscription, though it can't replace Apple Music's catalogue or its recommendations.
