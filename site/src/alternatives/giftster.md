Giftster is a wish list app for families and friend groups. Members keep lists in private groups, reserve gifts without the list owner seeing, and can run a Secret Santa draw, on the web and in its phone apps.

People look for an alternative because they would rather keep their family's lists, names and email addresses on their own server than on another company's service, and because they want lists that take items from any shop. Wishlist does the core job, lists, hidden claims and suggestions within a group, free and on your own server. It does not run a gift exchange draw.
