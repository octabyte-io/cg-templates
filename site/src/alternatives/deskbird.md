deskbird is a desk booking service for hybrid offices with shared, hot desks: people book desks, rooms and parking spots in its phone apps or from Slack and Microsoft Teams, and see which colleagues are in the office. It is a subscription priced per user each month.

Companies look for an alternative because the per-user price grows with the team, while the core job, booking a desk for a day, is simple. Some also prefer to keep who is in the office on which day on their own server. A self-hosted desk booking app such as Seatsurfing does that booking on your own floor plans with no per-user fee, though it has no native phone apps.
