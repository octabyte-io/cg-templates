Skilljar is a hosted customer-education platform: companies use it to run an academy that teaches customers and partners how to use their product, sold by quote.

People look for an alternative because Skilljar is a paid platform sold by quote, and learner data sits on Skilljar's service. A self-hosted ClassroomIO runs a customer academy on your own domain with no fee per learner, though it has fewer integrations and single sign-on is a paid extra.
