Hevy is a workout tracker app for the gym. You log sets, reps and weights as you train, follow routines, see your progress and records, and share workouts with friends in its social feed. Hevy Pro is its paid plan.

People look for an alternative because the free plan limits how many routines and custom exercises you can create, and their training history lives in someone else's app. A self-hosted Ryot logs workouts on your own server with no subscription, imports your Hevy history, and keeps your measurements and progress graphs in one place.
