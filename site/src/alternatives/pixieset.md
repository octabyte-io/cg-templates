Pixieset is a hosted client-gallery service for photographers: proofing where clients mark favourites, downloads with PINs and watermarks, a print store it fulfils, and branded invites, with a free tier and paid upgrades.

Photographers look for an alternative when they want galleries on their own web address with no storage plan, and mainly need to send clients clean albums to view and download. A self-hosted Lychee does that with password-protected or account-only albums and full-resolution downloads; in return, it has no proofing or print fulfilment, and you run the server.
