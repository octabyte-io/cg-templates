Xero is online accounting software for small businesses. It handles invoicing, bills, bank feeds and reconciliation and reporting, with payroll as an add-on, and it connects to a large marketplace of apps.

Businesses look for an alternative because Xero is a monthly subscription and its plans limit what you can do. When we checked its US pricing in November 2026, the entry plan cost $27 a month and allowed 20 invoices and 5 bills a month, with higher plans from $59. Payroll and some features cost extra. A self-hosted Bigcapital has no monthly fee and no limits on invoices or bills, and your books stay on your own server, though it has fewer automatic bank feeds and integrations.
