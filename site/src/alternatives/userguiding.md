UserGuiding is a hosted no-code onboarding tool for web apps: product tours, checklists, hotspots, announcements, surveys and a resource center.

People look for an alternative because UserGuiding is priced by monthly active users, and their users' data is kept on its servers. A self-hosted Usertour covers the same in-app onboarding with no user limit, on your own server, though a developer must add its snippet once and you look after the server.
