Partiful is a party invitation app: you make a themed event page, and guests are invited and reminded by text message to their phone numbers.

People look for an alternative because they would rather not hand every guest's phone number to an app, or want an invitation that works from a plain link. A self-hosted Gathio needs no phone number, app or account from guests, and keeps the guest list on your own server.
