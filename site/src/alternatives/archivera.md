ArchivEra, from Lucidea, is paid, hosted software for managing archives: describing collections, tracking accessions and publishing them online.

Archives look for an alternative to avoid the yearly subscription or to keep their catalogue on their own server. ArchivesSpace is free, open-source archives management used by many archives, built by and for archivists, with no licence fee.
