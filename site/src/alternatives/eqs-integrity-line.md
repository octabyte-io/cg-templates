EQS Integrity Line is a hosted whistleblowing system from EQS Group, used by many European companies to meet the EU Whistleblower Directive. Reporters submit concerns online, stay anonymous if they choose, and talk with case handlers through a secure mailbox.

Companies look for an alternative because it is a subscription service and the reports are stored in the vendor's cloud. A self-hosted GlobaLeaks provides the same kind of confidential channel, with anonymous reports, two-way follow-up and case management, on your own server with no licence fee. It keeps a "Powered by GlobaLeaks" line on every screen, as its licence requires.
