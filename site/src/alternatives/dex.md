Dex is a personal CRM: an app for keeping in touch with friends, family and professional contacts. It gathers contacts from your email, calendar and LinkedIn into one timeline, reminds you when to reach out, and has phone apps as well as the web app. It is a paid subscription.

People look for an alternative because of that subscription and because of what it connects to. A personal CRM holds private notes about the people closest to you, and Dex reads your email, calendar and social accounts to build it. Some people would rather keep those notes on their own server, and only need reminders for birthdays and catching up. A self-hosted personal CRM such as Monica does that for free, though everything is entered by hand.
