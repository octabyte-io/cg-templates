Micro.blog is a paid hosted service for short posts on your own domain, with a social timeline of the people you follow, phone apps and cross-posting to other networks.

People look for a Micro.blog alternative when they want their microblog on a server they control, with no subscription. Ech0 is a free, open-source microblog for one person or one business: post short updates with photos and video to your own timeline and let readers follow by RSS. It covers the publishing side only, with no social timeline, apps or cross-posting.
