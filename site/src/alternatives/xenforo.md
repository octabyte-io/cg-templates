XenForo is paid forum software, sold as a licence to run on your own server or as a hosted cloud plan, and known for a polished, modern forum out of the box.

Community owners look for an alternative when they would rather not pay for a licence and its renewals to run a club or hobby forum. MyBB is free, open-source forum software that has been developed by volunteers since 2002: it covers boards, threads, private messages and moderation, and keeps your members and posts on your own server, though it looks older and has a smaller team behind it.
