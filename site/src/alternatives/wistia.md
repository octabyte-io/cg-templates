Wistia is a hosted video platform for marketing: businesses upload videos, embed them on their websites, and use its lead capture, calls to action and marketing analytics.

People look for an alternative when they mainly need to host and embed videos and want them on their own server, with no plan limits on videos or viewers. A self-hosted PeerTube gives you embeds, private and password-protected videos and per-video statistics; in return, it has no marketing tools, and videos stream from your server rather than a worldwide delivery network.
