Mentimeter is a web app for interactive presentations: you build slides with polls, quizzes, word clouds and Q&A, and the audience answers on their phones while results appear on screen. It is used in classrooms, training sessions and team meetings.

People look for an alternative because Mentimeter's free plan limits how many people can respond each month, and paid plans are charged per presenter. A self-hosted Claper puts the same kinds of questions on your existing slides, with no attendee limit and no per-presenter fee, and the answers stay on your server.
