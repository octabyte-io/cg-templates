Sunsama is a daily planner: each morning it walks you through choosing the day's tasks, pulls them in from email, Slack and work tools, and lays them out on your calendar as time blocks.

People look for an alternative because Sunsama has no free plan; after a 14-day trial it costs a monthly subscription. Some also want their task list and work history kept privately. Super Productivity is a free, open-source planner with a day and week schedule, timeboxing and time tracking, and the self-hosted version syncs your data through your own server.
