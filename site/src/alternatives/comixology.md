ComiXology is Amazon's digital comics store, where the comics and manga you buy are copy-protected and read in Amazon's own apps.

Readers look for an alternative when their comics come from many places and they want one library they control. ComiXology purchases are copy-protected and live in Amazon's apps, while DRM-free comics, manga and webtoons from other shops end up scattered across folders and devices. A self-hosted Kavita brings those files into one private library that everyone in the household can read in the browser, though it has no store and cannot open ComiXology purchases.
