Drivvo is a car expense app for Android, iPhone and the web: you log fuel, services and other costs, and it charts what each vehicle costs. It is free for personal use, with a paid fleet plan for five or more vehicles.

People look for an alternative when they track several vehicles, for a family or a small business, and want the history somewhere they control. A self-hosted LubeLogger keeps every vehicle’s fuel, service and repair records, receipts and reminders on your own server, with no limit on vehicles and no fleet plan to pay for.
