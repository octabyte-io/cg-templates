Teachable is a hosted platform for selling online courses and coaching: you build lessons and sales pages, and Teachable runs the school, the checkout and the student apps.

Creators look for an alternative because their school, students and earnings all live on someone else's platform, with prices and plan limits that Teachable sets. A self-hosted CourseLit runs the courses, pages and checkout on your own server, takes no fee of its own and sends payments straight to your own Stripe, Razorpay or Lemon Squeezy account.
