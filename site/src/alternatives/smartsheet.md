Smartsheet is spreadsheet-style work management: sheets of rows and columns with Gantt charts, project timelines, forms, dashboards and automations, priced per user.

Teams look for an alternative when the per-user price grows with the team, or when they mainly use Smartsheet as a shared tracker rather than for detailed project plans. Teable gives a team spreadsheet-style tables with kanban, calendar and form views on its own server, with no row limit and no fee per user in its open-source edition. It is a partial replacement: it has no Gantt chart or timeline.
