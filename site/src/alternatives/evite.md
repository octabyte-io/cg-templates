Evite is an online invitation service: you pick a design, send the invitation, and track who has replied.

People look for an alternative because Evite's free invitations carry ads and its premium designs cost money, and every guest's reply sits with Evite. A self-hosted Gathio makes a plain event page with RSVPs and no ads, and keeps the guest list on your own server.
