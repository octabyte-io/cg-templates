Viva Engage, formerly Yammer, is Microsoft's internal social network: communities, a feed, posts, questions and announcements, built into Teams and Outlook as part of Microsoft 365.

People look for an alternative when they are not on Microsoft 365, want to leave it, or want their internal network on a server they control. HumHub runs a social intranet with spaces, feeds and profiles on your own server, with no fee per member in its free edition.
