Profound is an AI visibility platform for marketing teams at larger companies: it tracks how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Copilot and Google's AI answers mention and cite a brand, sold on custom enterprise pricing.

Teams look for an alternative because Profound is priced for enterprises and its software is closed, so smaller teams and agencies can't simply try it. A self-hosted Elmo tracks the same core numbers on your own server, and you pay only the AI providers for each check.
