Facebook is Meta's social network: profiles, a news feed, photos, groups, events and Messenger, used by family, friends, clubs and local communities.

People look for an alternative because Facebook is paid for by ads, decides what appears in your feed, and keeps everything you and your family post on Meta's servers. A self-hosted Friendica gives a group, a family or a community a Facebook-like place of its own, with no ads, no algorithm choosing what members see, and your own rules, and it connects to Mastodon and the wider fediverse. The trade-off is reach: far fewer people use it, so friends have to join to follow you.
