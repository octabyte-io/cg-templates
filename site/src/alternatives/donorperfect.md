DonorPerfect is hosted donor management software for nonprofits, with donor and gift records, online forms, reports and email, sold in plans that are priced by features and by how many records you keep.

Nonprofits look for an alternative when a growing donor list pushes them into a higher plan, or when they want donor data on their own server. CiviCRM is a free, open-source CRM built for nonprofits that holds donors, gifts, memberships, events and email in one database with no record limit; in return, you run the server and spend more time on setup.
