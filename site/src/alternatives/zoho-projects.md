Zoho Projects is cloud project management from Zoho, with task lists, Gantt charts, timesheets, issue tracking and links to Zoho's other business apps.

Teams look for an alternative because Zoho Projects runs only in Zoho's cloud, its free plan stops at 5 users and 3 projects, and paid plans are charged per user. Redmine is a long-established, open-source tracker that runs on your own server with no user or project limit, and handles issues, Gantt charts, time tracking and wikis for many projects at once. Its screens are plainer than Zoho's.
