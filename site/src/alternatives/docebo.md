Docebo is an enterprise learning platform for employee, partner and customer training, sold by quote, with AI features, a content marketplace and many integrations.

People look for an alternative because Docebo is priced for large companies and keeps training records on its own service. A self-hosted ILIAS is a full learning management system with no licence fee, used by companies, public bodies and universities, though it takes more setting up and comes with no ready-made course library.
