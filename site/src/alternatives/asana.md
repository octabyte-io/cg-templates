Asana is a work management app for teams across a company. Projects are lists, boards, timelines or calendars of tasks, and paid plans add rules for automation, forms, goals, portfolios and reporting.

Teams look for an alternative because Asana is charged per seat and keeps rules, forms and portfolios for paid plans, so costs grow with the team, and all project data is stored on Asana's servers. A self-hosted tracker such as Plane covers tasks on boards and timelines on your own server, with no seat price in its Community Edition.
