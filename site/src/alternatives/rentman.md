Rentman is rental management software for AV, event and production companies: it plans equipment and crew for each project, makes quotes and invoices, and tracks stock in the warehouse, sold as a subscription.

Smaller rental businesses look for an alternative when they pay for planning features they don't use, or want customers to book online by themselves. A self-hosted Louez covers the rental side, with an online storefront, real-time availability, a booking calendar, stock and contracts, and no subscription; companies that schedule crews for events still need a planning tool.
