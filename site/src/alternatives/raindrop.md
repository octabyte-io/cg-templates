Raindrop.io is a hosted bookmark manager: you save links from your browser or phone into collections, tag them and find them again, with a free plan and a paid Pro plan.

People look for an alternative because Raindrop.io keeps some of its most useful features for Pro. When we checked its plans page in November 2026, full-text search, permanent copies of saved pages, AI suggestions and annotations were all Pro features. Others want their saved links on a server they control. A self-hosted Karakeep includes full-text search and page archiving at no cost and keeps your bookmarks on your own server.
