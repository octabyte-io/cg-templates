HackMD is a hosted editor for writing Markdown notes together in real time, with free and paid team plans charged per seat, plus GitHub sync, book mode and team workspaces. Self-hosting is offered only on its Enterprise plan, priced by quote.

Teams look for an alternative because each seat adds to the bill and every note is stored on HackMD's servers. A self-hosted HedgeDoc gives the same real-time Markdown notes, slides and publishing on your own server, with no price per seat.
