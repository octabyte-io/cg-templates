faq:
  - q: "Can I embed the pretalx schedule on my own website?"
    a: "Yes. pretalx has a schedule widget you add to your site with a short piece of code, and the public schedule and speaker pages are on its own web address too. You can also export a static HTML copy of the schedule."
  - q: "Is there an attendee app for pretalx?"
    a: "Not from pretalx. Attendees use the public schedule in their phone's browser and can add starred sessions to their calendar. The schedule also exports as frab-compatible XML, XCal, JSON and iCal."
  - q: "Does pretalx send emails to speakers?"
    a: "Yes, once you add your email server. Acceptance, rejection and schedule-change emails use templates with placeholders, in several languages, and wait in an outbox so you can check them before they go."

Sessionize is a hosted service for event content: speakers submit sessions to a call for papers, organisers evaluate them, and the service builds the schedule, speaker pages and an attendee app.

Organisers look for an alternative because Sessionize is free only for free community events, and charges a fee per event otherwise, and because every proposal and speaker's details sit on its platform. A self-hosted pretalx runs the call for papers, review and schedule for any number of events with no per-event fee, on your own server.
