Zendesk is a customer service platform built around tickets: emails, chats and calls become tickets that agents assign, answer and track, with a help centre and many integrations.

Teams look for an alternative because Zendesk is priced per agent each month, with features spread across several plans, so a growing team pays more each year. Smaller teams often find it heavier than they need. A self-hosted app such as Chatwoot or FreeScout gives you a shared support inbox with no price per agent, and keeps customer data on your own server; Chatwoot also includes a help centre.
