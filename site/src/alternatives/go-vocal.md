Go Vocal, formerly CitizenLab, is a hosted platform that councils and public bodies use to engage residents online, with ideas, surveys, maps, voting and participatory budgets. It is sold by subscription.

Councils and community groups look for an alternative when the subscription is more than they can spend, or when they want the platform and residents' data under their own control. A self-hosted Decidim covers the same kind of participation with no licence fee, though you run it yourself and plan time to configure it.
