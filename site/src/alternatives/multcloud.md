MultCloud is a web service that connects many cloud drives in one place, so you can browse them together and copy, sync or back up files between them.

People look for an alternative because MultCloud's free plan allows only 5 GB of transfers a month, and its paid plans are priced by data. Others don't want to give a third party the logins to every drive. A self-hosted OpenList shows all your drives in one place, copies between them with no traffic cap, and keeps the drive logins on your own server.
