Clockify is a cloud time tracker with a free plan for unlimited users. Teams track time with timers or timesheets against clients and projects, and paid plans add invoicing, expenses, scheduling and more controls.

People look for an alternative because the features many businesses need, such as invoicing and stronger admin controls, are on paid plans charged per user, and all timesheets live on Clockify's servers. A self-hosted tracker such as Kimai includes invoicing in its free core and keeps the data on your own server.
