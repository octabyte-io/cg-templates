MyAnimeList is the best-known database and community for anime and manga: you keep a list of what you have watched and read with scores and progress, and read reviews, recommendations and forums from other fans.

People look for an alternative when they want their list on their own server, or one place for everything they watch, read and play instead of a separate site for anime. A self-hosted Yamtrack imports your MyAnimeList list, keeps it private, and tracks films, series, games and books next to it. It replaces your personal list, not the community.
