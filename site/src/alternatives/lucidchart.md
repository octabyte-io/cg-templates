Lucidchart, from Lucid Software, is an online diagramming app for flowcharts, process maps, org charts and technical diagrams, with real-time collaboration and a shared workspace for teams.

Teams look for an alternative because Lucidchart sells its team plans per user and keeps every diagram in its cloud. draw.io draws the same diagrams in the browser on your own server, with no plans or user fees, and your diagrams stay as files you keep.
