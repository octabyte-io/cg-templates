Paprika is a recipe manager with apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows. It saves recipes from websites, plans meals, builds grocery lists and syncs them between your devices. The apps are paid, and they work offline.

People look for an alternative because a recipe collection is something you keep for years, and they would rather not pay for it on every device or keep it in one company's app. Households also want one shared collection that everyone can open in a browser. A self-hosted recipe manager such as Mealie keeps the recipes, meal plan and shopping list on your own server for free, though it has no app-store app and no offline mode.
