Kubera is a paid net-worth tracker. It brings bank accounts, investments, crypto, property and other assets into one balance sheet, connecting to financial institutions to update balances, and can pass access to chosen people if something happens to you.

People look for an alternative because Kubera is a paid subscription, and it works by linking your financial accounts to its service. A self-hosted Wealthfolio shows your investments, property and debts in one private view on your own server, with no subscription and no telemetry, in return for entering or importing your figures yourself.
