Maze is a hosted user research service: teams run unmoderated tests on prototypes, often made in Figma, and get heatmaps, task success rates and survey answers, with options to recruit testers and run other kinds of studies.

Teams look for an alternative because Maze is a paid service and their prototypes and test results sit on its servers. A self-hosted Quant-UX does the core of the job, clickable prototypes, test links, recordings, heatmaps and task analytics, with no fee and with the results on your own server. It suits teams that can bring their own testers and don't need Maze's wider range of study types.
