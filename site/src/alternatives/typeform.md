Typeform is an online form and survey builder known for its conversational style: one question at a time, with smooth transitions, logic that skips questions, and forms you share as a link or embed on a website.

People look for an alternative because Typeform's plans limit how many responses you collect each month, so a popular survey pushes you onto a higher plan, and every answer is stored on Typeform's servers. A self-hosted survey tool such as Formbricks has no response limit and keeps the answers on your own server; in return, its forms are less focused on conversational design and you run the server yourself.
