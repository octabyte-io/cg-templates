Fathom Analytics is a paid, hosted website analytics service known for simple, privacy-friendly stats without cookies, shown on a single dashboard.

People look for an alternative when they want the same kind of cookie-free stats with their visitors' data on their own server, or want more than visitor counts, such as recordings and funnels, without adding a second tool. A self-hosted Rybbit gives you cookie-free stats plus session replay, funnels and error tracking with no subscription; in return, you run the server and it needs more memory than the lightest stats tools.
