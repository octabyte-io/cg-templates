OurGroceries is a shared grocery list app for iPhone, Android, Apple Watch, Amazon Echo and the web. Everyone in a household sees the same list as items are added or crossed off, and recipes can add their ingredients to a list.

People look for an alternative when a list is no longer enough: they want to know what is already in the cupboards, which food is about to go off, and which meals they can cook with what they have, and they want that household data on a server of their own. A self-hosted Grocy keeps track of your stock and due dates and builds the shopping list from it, though it takes more setup than a list app.
