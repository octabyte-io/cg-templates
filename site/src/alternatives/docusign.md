DocuSign is the best-known e-signature service. You upload a document, mark where each person signs, and send it as an envelope; signers sign in the browser and everyone gets a completed copy with a certificate of who signed and when. It is used for sales contracts, HR paperwork, leases and approvals.

People look for an alternative mainly because of price. Plans are charged per user and limit how many envelopes each user can send, so a small business that signs a lot of documents pays more each year, and features such as bulk sending and payments sit on higher plans. Every contract is also stored on DocuSign's servers. A self-hosted signing app such as DocuSeal handles the same send-sign-store flow with no per-user or per-envelope fee, and keeps the signed documents on your own server.
