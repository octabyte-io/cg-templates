Lightspeed sells cloud point-of-sale systems for retail shops and restaurants, with inventory, purchasing, customer loyalty, e-commerce, payments and reporting across several locations.

People look for an alternative because Lightspeed is a monthly subscription, which is a lot for a small shop or café that mainly needs a reliable till and stock list. Open Source Point of Sale covers that core on your own server with no subscription or fee per register, and your sales and stock data stay with you. In return, you keep a separate card terminal and run the server yourself.
