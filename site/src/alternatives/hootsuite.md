Hootsuite is a social media management platform for businesses and agencies. Teams schedule posts to many accounts, reply to comments and messages from one inbox, monitor what people say about a brand, and report on results.

People look for an alternative because Hootsuite has no free plan and charges per user, so a growing team or agency pays more for every person who logs in. Many teams mainly need to plan, approve and schedule posts. A self-hosted scheduler such as Postiz or TryPost covers that job for any number of users, at the cost of setting up each network's developer app yourself.
