Hootsuite is a social media management platform for businesses and agencies. Teams schedule posts to many accounts, reply to comments and messages from one inbox, monitor what people say about a brand, and report on results.

People look for an alternative because Hootsuite has no free plan and charges per user, so a growing team or agency pays more for every person who logs in. Many teams mainly need to plan, approve and schedule posts. A self-hosted scheduler such as Postiz, TryPost or Shoutrrr covers that job for any number of users, at the cost of setting up each network's developer app yourself, and Shoutrrr also gathers replies to your posts in one inbox.
