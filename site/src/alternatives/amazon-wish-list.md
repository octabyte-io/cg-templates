Amazon Wish List, or Amazon Lists, lets you save products from Amazon to a list and share it, so family and friends can buy from it for a birthday, the holidays or a registry.

People look for an alternative because an Amazon list is built around Amazon's store and an Amazon account, while the gifts people want often come from other shops. Wishlist is a self-hosted gift list app for a family or friend group: items from any shop, hidden claims so nobody buys the same present twice, and suggestions from others, with everyone's lists on your own server.
