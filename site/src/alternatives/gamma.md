Gamma is a hosted AI tool that turns a prompt or document into a presentation, document or simple web page made of cards, with AI credits, sharing links, analytics on who viewed it and export to PowerPoint or PDF.

People look for an alternative because Gamma is a monthly subscription for each person, its AI use is metered in credits, and every deck and the documents behind it live on Gamma's servers. A self-hosted Presenton generates and edits decks on your own server with no seat price, using the AI provider you choose and pay for directly.
