Ticket Tailor is an online box office for independent organisers. It sells tickets from its own pages or a widget on your site and charges a flat fee for every ticket rather than a percentage. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the pay-as-you-go fee was £0.60 plus VAT per ticket, lower if you buy ticket credits in advance.

Organisers look for an alternative when ticket volumes grow and even a small fee per ticket adds up, or when they want the event pages, attendee list and payment setup fully under their own control. A self-hosted Hi.Events sells tickets with no per-ticket fee at all and keeps every order on your own server.
