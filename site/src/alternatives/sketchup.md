SketchUp is a 3D modelling tool, sold by subscription, used by architects, builders, interior designers and homeowners to model buildings, interiors and objects, with a huge library of ready-made models and many extensions.

People look for a SketchUp alternative when they mainly draw buildings and floor plans and don't want a yearly subscription, or want their projects on their own server. Pascal Editor is narrower and simpler: it draws walls, rooms and roofs as building parts that know their size and area, rather than shapes you push and pull, and runs in the browser.
