When I Work is employee scheduling software for hourly teams: managers build the schedule, staff see their shifts, swap them and ask for time off, and a time clock records hours for payroll. It is priced per user each month.

People look for an alternative when per-user pricing adds up for a team that mainly needs to plan shifts and tell people when they work. A self-hosted BetterShift does that part with no per-user fee: shift templates, open shifts, sharing and calendar feeds, on your own server. Teams that rely on When I Work's time clock, messaging or payroll will need other tools for those.
