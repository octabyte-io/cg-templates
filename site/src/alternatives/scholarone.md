ScholarOne Manuscripts, from Clarivate, is a web-based submission and peer review system used by many large publishers and societies, priced by quote.

Journals look for an alternative when they publish independently and want to stop paying an enterprise licence, or want submissions, review and the published journal in one system they control. Open Journal Systems runs the editorial workflow and the journal website on your own server with no licence fee.
