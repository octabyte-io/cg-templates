Adobe Acrobat is Adobe's PDF software. Acrobat Pro, the paid edition, edits text and images in PDFs, converts them to and from Office files, reads scans with OCR, redacts, protects, compares and signs documents, and links to Adobe's cloud and e-signature service.

Offices look for an alternative because Acrobat Pro is a subscription for every person who uses it, and most people only need a handful of its tools: merge, compress, convert, OCR and sign. Online tools are cheaper but mean uploading contracts and personal records to someone else's website. A self-hosted PDF toolbox such as Stirling PDF does those everyday jobs in the browser, on your own server, with no fee per seat for a small team.
