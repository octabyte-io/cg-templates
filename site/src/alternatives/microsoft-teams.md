Microsoft Teams is Microsoft's chat and meetings app, part of Microsoft 365. It combines chat channels, video meetings, calling and file sharing through SharePoint and OneDrive.

Teams look for an alternative when they want chat without the rest of the Microsoft 365 package, when they find Teams heavy and slow for everyday messaging, or when they want conversations kept on a server they control rather than in Microsoft's cloud. A self-hosted chat app such as Mattermost gives you Slack-style channels and threads on your own server, with no price per user in its free edition.
