Kahoot! is a hosted quiz game for classrooms, training sessions and events: the host shows questions on a big screen and everyone answers on their own device against the clock.

People look for an alternative because Kahoot!'s plans limit how many players can join a game and charge each host, so a bigger room or a second trainer means a higher plan. Some also want the quizzes and results kept on their own server. A self-hosted Razzia, formerly called Rahoot, runs the same PIN, timer and leaderboard game with no player tiers and no fee per host.
