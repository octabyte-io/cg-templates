Planning Center is a cloud suite of church management apps: People for member records, Services for worship planning and volunteer scheduling, Check-Ins, Giving, Groups and more, each with its own subscription.

Churches look for an alternative because the bill grows as they add apps and people, and because member and giving records are kept in the vendor's cloud. A self-hosted ChurchCRM keeps people, groups, attendance, volunteers and giving records on your own server with no subscription. It suits churches that can plan worship and take online gifts with other tools.
