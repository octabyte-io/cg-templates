Squarespace is a hosted website builder known for polished templates, used for portfolios, small business sites, blogs and online shops.

People look for an alternative when the yearly plan becomes a significant cost, when they need features Squarespace does not offer, or when they want a site they can move. Squarespace keeps you on its own hosting, and its export covers only part of a site. Self-hosted WordPress or Ghost has no platform fee, can be extended without limits, and runs on any server you choose.
