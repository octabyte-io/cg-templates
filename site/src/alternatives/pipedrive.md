Pipedrive is a sales CRM built around the deal pipeline. Each deal sits on a board by stage, and salespeople log calls, emails and meetings against it; it adds reports, automations, phone apps and a marketplace of integrations.

Teams look for an alternative because Pipedrive charges for every seat, and features such as automations and fuller reporting depend on the plan. A self-hosted CRM such as Twenty gives the same pipeline board, contacts and email sync with no per-user price, and the customer data stays on your own server.
