Coggle is a web app for drawing mind maps together, shared by link, with a free plan and paid plans for more private diagrams.

People look for an alternative because the free plan limits how many diagrams you can keep private, and schools and companies may want maps to stay on their own server. TeamMapper does the shared mind-map job with no accounts or map limit, on a server you control.
