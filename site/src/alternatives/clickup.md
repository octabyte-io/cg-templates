ClickUp is an all-in-one work app: tasks, docs, goals, whiteboards, time tracking and chat in one place, with many views and automations.

Teams look for an alternative because ClickUp charges per user and its free plan is tight. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the free plan had 60 MB of storage and offered Gantt charts only as a trial. Many teams also find it does far more than they need. Leantime covers tasks, milestones, goals and time tracking in a simpler app on your own server, with no user fee.
