Microsoft Visio is Microsoft's diagramming app, used for flowcharts, org charts, floor plans and network diagrams, sold as per-user plans in Microsoft 365.

People look for an alternative because every person who draws needs a paid Visio plan, and the full desktop app runs only on Windows. draw.io draws the same kinds of diagrams in any browser, opens Visio files, and has no licence fee.
