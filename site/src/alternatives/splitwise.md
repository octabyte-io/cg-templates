Splitwise is a popular app for sharing costs with friends, housemates, partners and travel groups. Everyone adds what they paid, and Splitwise keeps a running balance of who owes whom, with apps for iPhone and Android.

People look for an alternative because many features sit behind Splitwise Pro. Its own Pro page lists unlimited expenses each day, search, charts, currency conversion, receipt scanning and an ad-free app among the paid extras, so free users meet a daily limit and ads. Spliit includes search, charts and currency conversion for everyone, with no daily limit and no ads, and a self-hosted copy keeps your group's spending on your own server. In return, it has no accounts and no bank or card import. I Hate Money, another self-hosted option, works without accounts too.
