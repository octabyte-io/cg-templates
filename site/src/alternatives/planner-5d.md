Planner 5D is a home design app for drawing floor plans and furnishing them in 3D, in the browser and on phones. It is freemium, with a free version and paid plans, a large furniture catalogue and realistic renders.

People look for a Planner 5D alternative when they want to plan a house, flat, extension or shop without a subscription or project limits, or want the drawings on their own server where a whole team can open them. Pascal Editor draws rooms, walls, roofs and stairs as real building parts, shows them in 3D and 2D, and exports formats that architects and 3D printers use.
