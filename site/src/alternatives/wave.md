Wave is free accounting and invoicing software for small businesses, with paid extras such as its Pro plan and online payments.

People look for an alternative when they need recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders, which on Wave need the paid Pro plan, or when they work outside the US and Canada, where Wave’s payments and bank connections are mostly limited. A self-hosted SolidInvoice sends recurring invoices and reminders at no cost and takes payments through Stripe or PayPal wherever they operate, though it does no bookkeeping.
