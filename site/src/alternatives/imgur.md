Imgur is a free image host and community: you upload a picture, get a link to share or embed in a forum or chat, and browse what others post. It is paid for by ads and runs on Imgur's servers under Imgur's rules.

People look for an alternative when they want images to stay where they put them, on their own domain, without ads, and under their own rules about what is kept and for how long. A self-hosted Chevereto gives you your own upload-and-share site for you, a team or a community, with direct links and embed codes. It has no audience of its own, and the free edition leaves abuse controls to the paid editions.
