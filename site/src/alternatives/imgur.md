Imgur is one of the best-known image hosts: you upload a picture, get a link and share it, and public posts appear in its community gallery. It is free to use and paid for by adverts.

People look for an alternative because Imgur decides what stays online. In 2023 it began deleting old images not tied to an account and banned explicit images, so many old links can stop working. A self-hosted Slink keeps your images on your own server for as long as you choose, private by default, with passwords and expiry dates when you need them.
