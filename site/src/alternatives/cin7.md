Cin7 sells inventory and order management for product businesses as two products, Cin7 Core and Cin7 Omni, linking stock to online stores, marketplaces, point of sale, a B2B portal and accounting apps.

Businesses look for an alternative because Cin7 Core's plans are priced by number of users with a yearly cap on sales orders, and Omni is sold by quote, so costs rise as the team and order volume grow. A self-hosted OpenBoxes tracks stock and orders across warehouses with no user or order limits. It suits organisations that mainly move stock between their own sites rather than sell through many channels.
