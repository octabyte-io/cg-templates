Mem is an AI note-taking app: you write or paste notes, and its AI organises them, finds related notes and answers questions from everything you have saved. It runs only in Mem's cloud, with paid plans.

People look for an alternative because their notes, often personal or about work, sit on Mem's servers and are read by its AI there, and because a cloud-only app can change its plans or features at any time. Blinko gives you quick notes with optional AI search and chat on your own server, using the AI provider or local model you choose.
