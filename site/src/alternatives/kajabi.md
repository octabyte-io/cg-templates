Kajabi is an all-in-one platform for online course businesses: courses, coaching, communities, email marketing, sales funnels and podcasts, with a mobile app.

People look for an alternative because of the monthly price of Kajabi's plans, and because the business, its customers and its content all live on Kajabi's platform. A self-hosted CourseLit runs the courses, downloads, pages, email and communities from your own server with no fee of its own, as long as you can live without funnels, podcasts and a mobile app. Didacta, a fair-code platform in early beta, puts courses, memberships and a member community with live classes in one app.
