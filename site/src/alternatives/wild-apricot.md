Wild Apricot is hosted membership software for associations, clubs and nonprofits. It combines a member database, a website, online dues payments, event registration and email, priced by the number of contacts.

Clubs look for an alternative because the price rises as the membership list grows, and many small groups mainly need a tidy member database, events and email. A self-hosted Admidio covers those for free on your own server, with no price per contact, as long as you collect dues and run your public website some other way.
