Slido, owned by Cisco, is an audience interaction tool for meetings, conferences and classes. People join from their phones to ask and upvote questions and answer polls, and it plugs into PowerPoint, Google Slides, Webex and Microsoft Teams.

People look for an alternative because the free plan is limited, and larger events need a one-time or yearly paid plan. Questions and answers from staff meetings or customer events are also stored on Slido's servers. A self-hosted Claper runs Q&A, polls and quizzes on your own server for as many attendees as it can handle, at no cost per event.
