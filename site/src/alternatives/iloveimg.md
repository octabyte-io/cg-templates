iLoveIMG is a website with a set of online image tools: compress, resize, crop, convert, rotate, watermark, a photo editor, upscaling and background removal. It has a free plan with limits on how many images each tool processes and a paid Premium plan.

People look for an alternative because the free plan limits how many images each tool handles, and because images are uploaded to iLoveIMG's servers to be processed. For the everyday jobs, making images smaller, resizing them and changing their format, MAZANOKE is open source and does the work in your own browser, with no limits, no account and no uploads. It covers only those jobs, not iLoveIMG's editing tools. SnapOtter covers those too, on your own server, with crop, watermarks, a photo editor and AI upscaling and background removal.
