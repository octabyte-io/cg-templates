Attio is a hosted CRM with a modern, flexible design: records and lists you shape yourself, custom objects, data enrichment, email and calendar sync, automations and AI features, priced per seat.

Teams look for an alternative when they want the same kind of modern CRM on their own server, with customer data kept there and no price for each seat. A self-hosted Relaticle covers the core of a sales CRM, works with the AI tools a team already uses, and adds no seat limit, though its data model is smaller than Attio's.
