HubSpot is a customer platform for growing businesses. Its free CRM tracks contacts, companies and deals, and paid hubs add marketing (forms, email campaigns, landing pages), sales tools, a help desk and more.

Teams look for an alternative because the price climbs quickly once the free CRM isn't enough: paid seats, higher tiers for automation and reporting, and marketing plans priced by the number of contacts. For a team that mainly needs a sales CRM, a self-hosted app such as Twenty covers contacts, companies and a deal pipeline with no per-user price, and keeps customer data on your own server.
