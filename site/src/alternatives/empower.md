Empower Personal Dashboard, formerly Personal Capital, is a free online dashboard from Empower, a US retirement and wealth company. You link your bank, credit card and investment accounts, and it shows your net worth, budgeting and cash flow, a retirement planner and portfolio analysis.

People look for an alternative because the dashboard works best once you hand over the logins to all your accounts, and because it sits alongside Empower's paid advisory services. Investors who mainly want to follow their portfolio can do that with a self-hosted Ghostfolio, which links to no accounts and keeps every holding on their own server.
