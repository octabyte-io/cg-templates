Habitify is a habit-tracking app for iPhone, Android, Mac and the web. It has a free version and a paid premium plan, with goals, reminders, timers and mood tracking.

People look for an alternative when they want a simple, private record of their habits, without a subscription and without being nudged by goals and notifications. A self-hosted Beaver Habit Tracker keeps that record on your own server, with no limit on habits, and works in any browser.
