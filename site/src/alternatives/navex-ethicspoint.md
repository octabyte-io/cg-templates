NAVEX EthicsPoint is a hosted ethics hotline and case management service: employees and others report misconduct online or by phone, and compliance teams investigate and track the cases. NAVEX also owns WhistleB.

Organisations look for an alternative because the service is a running subscription, and because the most sensitive reports in the company are stored by a third party. A self-hosted GlobaLeaks gives you an encrypted reporting site and case handling on your own server, with no licence fee and no paid features.
