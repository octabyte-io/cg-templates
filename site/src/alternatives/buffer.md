Buffer is a social media scheduler. You connect your accounts, write posts, and Buffer publishes them at the times you set from one queue and calendar; it adds simple analytics, AI writing help and a link-in-bio page.

People look for an alternative because Buffer charges for each social channel, so a business or agency with many accounts pays more every time it adds one. A self-hosted scheduler such as Postiz has no channel or post limits; in return, you set up a developer app with each network yourself.
