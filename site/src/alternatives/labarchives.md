LabArchives is a cloud electronic lab notebook, widely licensed by universities and research institutes, where researchers record experiments, attach data and share notebooks with their group.

Labs look for an alternative because the licence is paid per user, so costs grow with every student and postdoc, and because research records are kept in the vendor's cloud. A self-hosted eLabFTW gives a whole department a notebook, inventory and booking calendar on its own server, with no fee per user and no feature behind a paywall.
