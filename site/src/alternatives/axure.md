Axure RP is a long-established paid app for building interactive prototypes, known for logic-heavy work: conditions, variables and form validation that make a prototype behave close to the real product.

Teams look for an alternative because Axure RP is paid software, and testing a prototype with real people needs a separate tool. A self-hosted Quant-UX builds interactive prototypes with logic in the browser and tests them in the same place, with recordings, heatmaps and task analytics, with no licence fee and the work kept on your own server.
