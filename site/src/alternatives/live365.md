Live365 is an internet radio hosting service that bundles streaming with music licensing for the US, UK and Canada, so a station can play licensed music without arranging royalties itself. It is a paid service.

Station owners look for a Live365 alternative when they play their own or royalty-free music and don't need licensing included, or want their station on their own server. AzuraCast runs the station, with the AutoDJ, schedules, requests and listener reports, with no monthly fee; the music licences and the server's bandwidth become your responsibility.
