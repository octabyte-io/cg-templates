Jotform is an online form builder used for everything from contact and order forms to applications, registrations and surveys, with a large template gallery, widgets, payment fields, PDF tools and many integrations.

People look for an alternative because of its limits. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed 5 forms and 100 submissions a month, and paid plans ran from $34 to $99 a month billed yearly. A busy form soon pushes you onto a higher plan, and every submission is stored on Jotform's servers. Self-hosted HeyForm and OpnForm have no limit on forms or responses and keep the answers on your own server.
