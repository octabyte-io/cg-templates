Zoho One is a bundle of more than 45 Zoho business apps, from CRM, books and inventory to helpdesk, HR and marketing, sold under one licence for each employee per month.

Businesses look for an alternative because the price grows with every employee, whether or not they use the apps, and all the company's records sit in Zoho's cloud. A self-hosted Odoo Community puts the business core in one system on your own server with no per-user fee, in return for fewer apps and no phone apps. AureusERP, a younger system whose modules and screens follow Odoo closely, does the same with accounting included.
