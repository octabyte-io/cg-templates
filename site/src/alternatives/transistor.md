Transistor is a hosted podcast platform for creators and companies, with unlimited shows on every plan, private podcasts for subscribers, a website for each show and analytics.

People look for an alternative because its plans are priced by downloads. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the Starter plan cost $19 a month for 20,000 downloads a month and 50 private podcast subscribers, and Professional cost $49 a month for 100,000 downloads. Castopod hosts unlimited shows and subscriber-only episodes on your own server, with no download cap other than your server's bandwidth.
