MyHeritage is a subscription genealogy service: families build trees on it, search historical records, get Smart Matches with other people's trees, and take its DNA tests.

People look for an alternative when they want a private family tree without a subscription, kept on their own server and downloadable in full at any time. A self-hosted Gramps Web gives relatives a shared tree with photos, maps and DNA match data; in return, it has no record search, matches or DNA tests.
