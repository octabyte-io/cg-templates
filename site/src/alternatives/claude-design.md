Claude Design is Anthropic's AI design tool: you describe what you need, and it makes prototypes, slide decks, one-pagers and other visual work with Claude. It is hosted by Anthropic, included in Claude subscriptions and works only with Anthropic's models.

People look for a Claude Design alternative when they want to choose the AI model, keep their projects and brand files on their own server, or pay an AI provider directly for what they use instead of a subscription. Open Design is an open-source design studio for the same kinds of work that runs on your own server with the provider you connect.
