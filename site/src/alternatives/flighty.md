Flighty is an iPhone app for following flights live, with delay alerts, gate changes and predictions, and a Passport that keeps a record of your past flights with statistics. Its basic features are free; unlimited Passport history, extra statistics and importing from email, calendar and TripIt are part of the paid Flighty Pro plan.

People look for an alternative when what they value most is the record of every flight they have taken, and they want it to last without a subscription or a single phone app. A self-hosted AirTrail keeps that log on your own server, with a map, history and statistics, and several people in a family can each keep a private log on the same install.
