Rundown Studio is hosted run-of-show software for show callers, producers and broadcast teams: a rundown whose start times update as cues change, live tracking of the current cue, guests who follow or edit the show, and a built-in teleprompter.

Teams look for an alternative because the free plan is meant for testing, with limited rundowns, and regular use means buying an event pass or a yearly team plan. Ontime runs the rundown, delays, cuesheets and stage timers on your own server with no plan or event fee. It has no teleprompter and no per-person accounts.
