Whimsical is an online workspace for flowcharts, mind maps, wireframes and docs, where teams draw and comment together. Its free plan is limited, and paid plans are charged per editor.

People look for an alternative when they mostly draw flowcharts and mind maps on their own and would rather not pay per editor, or want their diagrams kept off someone else's cloud. A self-hosted Drawnix puts flowcharts and mind maps on one free board in the browser, with boards kept on your device, though it has none of Whimsical's teamwork features.
