Quicken is personal finance software for tracking bank accounts, cards, investments and bills, with budgets and reports. It comes as Quicken Classic for desktop and Quicken Simplifi on the web and phones.

People look for an alternative because Quicken is a subscription that renews every year. When we checked its comparison page in November 2026, Quicken Simplifi was billed annually and no free plan was offered. Others want their financial records on their own server rather than a company's. Firefly III tracks every account in detail with no subscription; the trade-off is that you bring transactions in yourself or set up bank sync separately.
