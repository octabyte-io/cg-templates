QR TIGER is a hosted QR code generator that focuses on dynamic codes: codes whose destination you can change after printing, with scan tracking and hosted landing pages such as digital business cards. Its paid plans are built around those dynamic codes.

People look for an alternative when they only need good-looking codes that point somewhere fixed, such as a menu, a Wi-Fi login or a contact card, and don't want a subscription or an account for that. A self-hosted Mini QR makes those codes with your logo, colours and a frame, free and with no limit, though it does no tracking and its codes can't be changed after printing.
