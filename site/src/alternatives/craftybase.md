Craftybase is inventory, costing and production software for small-batch makers such as candle, cosmetics and food businesses. It works out what each product costs to make, keeps stock of materials and syncs sales from Shopify, Amazon and Faire. On 1 July 2026 it was renamed Stocksmith, with the same team and software.

People look for an alternative because it is a paid subscription after a 14-day trial, and the recipes and costs that make up a small business's know-how are kept in someone else's cloud. Craftplan does recipe costing, stock with lot numbers and production batches on your own server for free, and adds order planning and recall tracing, though you give up the automatic shop sync.
