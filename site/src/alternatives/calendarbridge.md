CalendarBridge is a paid service that syncs busy times between calendars and also offers booking pages for scheduling meetings.

People look for an alternative when they only need the sync part, or want their calendar logins kept on their own server rather than paying a subscription for every account. A self-hosted Keeper.sh syncs Google, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail and CalDAV calendars for free, though it has no booking pages.
