Pocket was Mozilla's read-it-later service: you saved articles from your browser or phone and read them later in a clean view, with a free plan and a paid Premium plan.

Mozilla shut Pocket down on 8 July 2025 and let users export their saves until 8 October 2025, after which the data was deleted. Anyone who relied on it needs a new home for their reading list, and many would rather not depend on a service that can close again. A self-hosted Karakeep saves articles, links and notes on your own server, keeps a copy of each page if you want one, and stays available for as long as you run it.
