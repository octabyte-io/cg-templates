monday.com is a work management platform. Teams build boards of items with columns for status, owners and dates, and use them for projects, CRM, marketing and HR, with automations, integrations and dashboards on top.

Teams look for an alternative because monday.com sells seats in fixed bundles and keeps more automations, integrations and dashboards for higher plans, so a growing team pays more each year. A self-hosted tracker such as Plane covers boards, spreadsheets and timelines for software and product work on your own server, with no seat price in its Community Edition.
