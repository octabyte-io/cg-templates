Sweepy is a home cleaning schedule app for iPhone and Android. It splits the home into rooms, shows how clean each one is, and shares chores between household members, with some features in a paid premium subscription.

People look for an alternative when they want every feature for the whole household without a subscription, or want their home's chores on a server of their own. A self-hosted Donetick has no paid tier and no member limit: rotation, points, subtasks and analytics are all in the free build on your server.
