Spendee is a phone app for tracking spending and income: you log expenses in categories, keep separate wallets, can share a wallet with others, and see where your money goes in charts. Your records are kept in Spendee's cloud.

People look for an alternative when they want those records on a server they control rather than in an app company's cloud, or when they want a money diary that works as well on a computer as on a phone, with no subscription. A self-hosted ezBookkeeping records spending from any phone or browser and keeps every transaction on your own server, though it has no budgets, bank sync or shared wallets.
