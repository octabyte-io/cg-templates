DrChrono is a cloud EHR and practice-management service known for its iPad and phone apps, with charting, scheduling and billing.

Practices look for an alternative when they want patient records kept on their own server under their own control, or want to stop paying a cloud subscription. A self-hosted OpenEMR covers records, scheduling, billing and a patient portal; in return, it has no tablet app, and setup and compliance are your practice's job.
