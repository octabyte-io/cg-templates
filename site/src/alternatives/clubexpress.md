ClubExpress is hosted club management software for clubs and associations, with a club website, member database, dues collection, event registration and email in one subscription.

Clubs look for an alternative because the subscription is an ongoing cost and their member records live in the vendor's cloud. A self-hosted Admidio keeps the member database, roles, events and email on your own server for free. It suits clubs that can collect dues and keep a public website elsewhere.
