Flickr is one of the oldest photo-sharing communities: photographers upload photos to albums, tag them, and find and follow each other's work.

People look for an alternative when they want a photo site under their own name rather than a profile on someone else's platform. A self-hosted Piwigo shows your albums on your own web address, with fine control over who sees what and no storage plan; in return, it brings no community of other photographers and you run the server.
