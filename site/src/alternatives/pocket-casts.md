Pocket Casts is a popular podcast app that syncs your subscriptions and progress across devices through its own cloud account. Its paid Plus plan adds the desktop and web apps and cloud storage for your own files.

People look for an alternative when they want that cross-device sync without a subscription or an account with a third-party service. A self-hosted PinePods keeps every listener's podcasts, queue and progress on your own server and syncs them to its web, desktop and phone apps, at no cost.
