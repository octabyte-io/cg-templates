Strong is a workout tracker app for the gym. You build routines, log sets, reps and weights as you train, and see your history and records, with more features in its paid Pro plan.

People look for an alternative because the useful extras sit behind a subscription and their training history lives in someone else's app. A self-hosted wger plans routines and logs workouts on your own server with no subscription, and the same app also tracks nutrition and body measurements for you, your family or your gym. Ryot logs workouts too and can import your Strong history.
