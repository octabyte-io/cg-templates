Bynder is a hosted digital asset management (DAM) platform built around brands. Marketing teams use it to store and share logos, photos, videos and documents, with brand guidelines, templating and many marketing integrations, and it is sold on custom quotes.

Teams look for an alternative when the quote is more than they want to pay for what is mostly a shared media library, or when they want their files on their own server. A self-hosted Phraseanet keeps photos, videos, audio and documents in one searchable library, with detailed metadata, rights by collection and user, and an approval process, at no licence fee. It suits archives, press and communication teams that catalogue a lot of media; marketing teams that rely on Bynder's brand portal and templates will miss them.
