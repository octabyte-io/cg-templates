SmugMug is a photo hosting and gallery service for photographers: unlimited photo storage, customisable gallery websites, client galleries and print sales.

People look for an alternative because the subscription keeps running for as long as the photos are online, which is a lot for a club, school or organisation that mainly needs to share a large collection. A self-hosted Piwigo or Lychee shows your galleries on your own web address with private albums and no storage plan; in return, it has no print sales or client proofing, and you run the server.
