LingQ is a language-learning service built around reading: you read lessons from its library or your own imports, unknown words are highlighted, and the words you save, called LingQs, come back for review. Its free plan limits how many words you can save, and the full features need a subscription.

People look for an alternative because they mostly read their own material and don't want to pay a subscription for it, or want their reading and vocabulary kept on their own server. A self-hosted LinguaCafe offers the same read, look up and review loop for free, though you bring your own texts and dictionaries and use it in the browser.
