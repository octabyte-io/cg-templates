Linear is a fast, minimal issue tracker popular with software startups. Teams plan work in cycles, track issues and projects with keyboard shortcuts, and connect it to GitHub and Slack, with desktop and phone apps.

Teams look for an alternative because Linear is cloud-only and charged per user, so the bill grows with the team and all project data sits on Linear's servers. A self-hosted tracker such as Plane works in a similar way and runs on your own server, with no per-user fee in its Community Edition.
