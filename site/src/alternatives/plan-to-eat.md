Plan to Eat is a meal planning service: you save recipes from websites into one collection, add them to a weekly planner, and it builds the shopping list, on the web and in its phone apps. It is a paid subscription.

People look for an alternative because it is a subscription for something many households use every week for years, and because their recipe collection lives in Plan to Eat's account. A self-hosted meal planner such as Mealie does the same save, plan and shop routine for free on your own server, and imports the recipes you already have there.
