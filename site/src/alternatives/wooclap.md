Wooclap is an interactive teaching platform used by schools, universities and trainers. Students answer polls, quizzes and open questions on their phones during a lesson, and it connects to learning platforms such as Moodle.

People look for an alternative because Wooclap's free plan allows only a few questions a month, and paid plans are billed yearly per presenter. Schools also prefer to keep students' answers on their own servers. A self-hosted Claper gives every teacher polls, quizzes and questions on their own slides at no cost per person, and its Moodle link sends quiz scores to the gradebook.
