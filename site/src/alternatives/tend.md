Tend is cloud farm software for vegetable, flower, orchard and nursery growers. It plans crops from a crop library, organises tasks and labour, and tracks sales, inventory and traceability, with a phone app that works offline.

Growers look for an alternative because the software is a subscription and their records live in its cloud. A self-hosted farmOS keeps planting, field and harvest records on your own server for free. It asks more of you than a guided crop planner, so it suits growers who want full records they control more than ready-made plans.
