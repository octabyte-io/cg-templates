ChatGPT Plus is OpenAI's paid personal plan for ChatGPT. For a monthly fee it gives one person more use of OpenAI's latest models than the free plan, with extras such as memory, custom GPTs, image generation, voice and apps for phones and computers.

People look for an alternative for a few reasons. The subscription costs the same whether you chat every day or once a week, and it only covers OpenAI's models, while many people now want to try Claude, Gemini or open models too. Some also prefer that their chat history is not kept in a chat company's account. big-AGI is open source and uses your own API keys, so you pay each provider only for what you use, switch between many companies' models in one window, and keep your chats in your own browser. It gives up ChatGPT's polished apps and memory in return.
