Cvent is enterprise event management software used by companies and associations for conferences, meetings and trade shows. It covers registration, abstract management, attendee apps, event marketing and venue sourcing, and is priced by quote.

Organisers look for an alternative when Cvent's broad platform and its pricing are more than their events need, or when they want registrations and attendee data on their own server. A self-hosted Indico covers registration, abstracts, the timetable and badges for conferences and recurring meetings with no licence fee, though it does not replace Cvent's marketing, venue sourcing or attendee app.
