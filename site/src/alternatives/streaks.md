Streaks is a paid habit-tracking app for Apple devices. It reminds you of your daily tasks and works with Apple Health and the Apple Watch.

People look for an alternative when they also use Android or a Windows computer, or want their habit history on their own server rather than in one company's app. A self-hosted Beaver Habit Tracker works in any browser on any device and keeps that history with you, though it has no reminders or Health integration. OpenHabitTracker has native apps with reminders on Apple and other devices, syncing with your own server.
