QR Code Generator, sold as QR Code Generator PRO, is a hosted service for making QR codes for websites, menus, Wi-Fi, contact cards and more. Its paid plans add logos and design options, dynamic codes whose link you can change after printing, and scan statistics.

People look for an alternative because the features they want most, such as a logo on the code, sit behind a subscription, and dynamic codes route every scan through the service, so they depend on staying subscribed. A self-hosted Mini QR makes styled static codes with your logo, colours and a frame, for free and with no account, and those codes keep working on their own, though it cannot redirect a printed code or count scans.
