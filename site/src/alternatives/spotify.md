Spotify is a music streaming service: for a monthly fee you can play almost any song from its catalogue on your phone, computer, speakers or car, with playlists and recommendations made for you.

People look for an alternative for a few reasons. The subscription never ends, and the songs you listen to are only rented, so albums can disappear when a licence runs out. Some collectors want to hear their own files, in the quality they bought or ripped them, rather than a streamed copy. And a family with a large music collection already owns the music it wants. A self-hosted music server such as Navidrome or Koel streams that collection to every device with no subscription, though it can't replace Spotify's catalogue or its discovery features.
