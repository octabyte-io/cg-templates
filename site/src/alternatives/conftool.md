ConfTool is conference management software for academic and scientific conferences, handling abstract submission, reviewing, registration and the programme. Its free Standard edition is for small, non-commercial conferences, installed on your own server, without support; its Pro edition is hosted and priced by quote.

Organisers look for an alternative when their conference outgrows the free edition's participant limit or is not eligible for it, and they would rather not pay for Pro. A self-hosted Indico covers abstracts, reviewing, registration, the timetable and badges with no participant limit and no licence fee, though it takes time to set up.
