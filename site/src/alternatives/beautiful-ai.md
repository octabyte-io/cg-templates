Beautiful.ai is a hosted presentation app built around smart slides, layouts that arrange themselves as you add content, with AI to draft decks, team themes and brand controls, and export to PowerPoint.

Teams look for an alternative because Beautiful.ai is priced per user, so every person who makes slides adds to the bill, and the decks live on its servers. A self-hosted Presenton drafts and edits decks on your own server with no seat price, from your own templates and the AI provider you choose.
