OurHome is a family app for chores and rewards, with a shared grocery list and calendar, on iPhone and Android. Parents assign chores, children earn points for finishing them, and points can be swapped for rewards.

People look for an alternative when they want the family's chores on a server of their own, or need more control over how chores repeat and rotate. A self-hosted Donetick covers the chores side for free: repeating tasks, turns between family members, points and a wall-tablet view for children, though it has no grocery list or calendar.
