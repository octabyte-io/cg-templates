MindMeister is an online mind-mapping tool for individuals and teams, with shared folders, templates, comments, a presentation mode and phone apps.

People look for an alternative because its free plan limits how many mind maps you can keep, and paid plans are priced per user. Many only need a quick map that a group builds together in a meeting, workshop or class. TeamMapper does that on your own server, with no accounts and no limit on maps. WiseMapping suits teams that want to keep their maps in accounts, with sharing, version history and embedding.
