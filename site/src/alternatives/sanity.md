In Directus, sign in with your admin email and the password Cloudgate created, choose the Core plan unless you have a licence key, and build your collections in Settings, Data Model.

---

Sanity is a hosted content platform: content lives in its Content Lake, and teams edit it in Sanity Studio, an open-source React editor that developers customise in code. Websites and apps read the content through its query language, GROQ, or GraphQL, and the hosted service is sold by plan.

Teams look for a Sanity alternative when they want their content in a database they run rather than a hosted service, or a ready-made editor that needs no code to set up. Squidex gives editors a finished web app for structured content, with REST and GraphQL APIs, on your own server. Cockpit does the same on a much smaller server, Enonic XP keeps Sanity's content-as-code approach and adds a visual page editor, and the source-available Directus keeps your content in a SQL database you can query and back up yourself.
