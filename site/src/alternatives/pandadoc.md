PandaDoc is a document platform for sales teams. It builds proposals, quotes and contracts from templates and a content library, adds pricing tables, tracks when clients open a document, collects e-signatures and payments, and connects to CRMs.

Teams look for an alternative because PandaDoc is charged per seat, and many only use it to send documents for signature. If your proposals are already written in a word processor, you are paying for an editor you don't need. A self-hosted signing app such as DocuSeal covers sending, signing and storing those documents with no per-user fee, and keeps them on your own server.
