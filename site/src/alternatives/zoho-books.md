Zoho Books is online accounting software from Zoho, the company behind a large suite of business apps. It covers invoicing, bills, inventory, banking and reports, and works closely with the rest of the Zoho suite.

Businesses look for an alternative when they outgrow its free plan, which is limited to one user plus an accountant and to small businesses by revenue, and paid plans limit users and features by tier. Others want their financial records off a large cloud suite. A self-hosted Bigcapital offers a very similar set of accounting tools with no user limit and no subscription, and keeps your books on your own server.
