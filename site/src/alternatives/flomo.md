flomo is a card-style notes app, popular in China, for writing down short thoughts quickly and organising them with tags rather than folders, then looking back through them over time. It runs in flomo's own cloud, with a paid Pro plan for its fuller features.

People look for an alternative because every note sits on flomo's servers and some features need a Pro membership, or because they want the same quick-card habit in an app they control. Blinko keeps the timeline of short cards with tags, adds optional AI search over your own notes, and stores everything on your own server with no paid features.
