CLZ, from Collectorz.com, makes cataloguing apps for collectors, one for each type of collection: books, movies, music, comics and games. They scan barcodes, fill in details from CLZ's online database and sync between devices, and each is sold as a subscription.

Collectors look for an alternative when they pay for several CLZ apps, collect something CLZ has no app for, such as figures, stamps or cards, or want their catalogue on their own server. A self-hosted Koillection handles any kind of collection in one free app, though you enter the details yourself.
