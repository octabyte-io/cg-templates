Zoho Creator is Zoho’s low-code platform for building business apps from forms, reports, pages and workflows, with native phone apps and ties to the rest of the Zoho suite. It is priced per user every month, and its Standard plan allows one app.

Teams look for an alternative when they need more apps or more users than their plan allows, or want the records on their own server rather than in Zoho’s cloud. A self-hosted Corteza builds the same kind of record, workflow and approval apps with no user or app limit, though it has no phone apps and asks more of the person building the apps.
