Readlang is a web reader and browser extension for language learners: click a word on any page to translate it, and the words you look up become flash cards. A paid Premium plan lifts its limits.

People look for an alternative because they read a lot and want no limits or subscription, or want their vocabulary kept on their own server. A self-hosted LinguaCafe offers reading with word lookups and flash-card reviews for free, though you import texts into it rather than reading on any website.
