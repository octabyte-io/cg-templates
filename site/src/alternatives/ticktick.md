TickTick is a to-do list and planner for phones, computers and the web, with lists, reminders, a calendar, a habit tracker and a focus timer. Some features, such as its calendar views, are part of its paid Premium plan.

People look for an alternative when they want those planning features without a subscription, or want their tasks and habits on a server of their own. A self-hosted tududi keeps tasks, projects, habits and a daily plan on your server, free for everyone in the household.
