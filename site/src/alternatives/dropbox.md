Dropbox is the original file-syncing service: a folder on every device that stays in sync, with links for sharing and tools for teams.

People look for an alternative because the free Basic plan has only 2 GB of space and works on up to three devices, and paid plans are charged per user for teams. Others prefer to keep files on their own server rather than a US cloud provider. A self-hosted app such as Nextcloud does the same syncing and sharing, with no device limit, no price per user and storage as big as your server's disk.
