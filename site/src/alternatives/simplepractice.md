SimplePractice is a cloud practice-management subscription built for therapists and other private practices, with scheduling, notes, billing and a client portal.

Practices look for an alternative when they want client records on a server they control or want to stop paying a monthly subscription. A self-hosted OpenEMR covers scheduling, records, billing and a patient portal; in return, it is broader and more complex to set up, and compliance stays your practice's responsibility.
