Toggl Track is a cloud time tracker known for its simple one-click timer. Freelancers and teams track time across browser, desktop and phone apps, and see reports by client and project.

People look for an alternative because the free plan stops at 5 users and paid plans charge per user each month, with billable rates and fuller reporting on higher tiers. Timesheets also live on Toggl's servers. A self-hosted tracker such as Kimai offers timers, reports and invoicing for any number of users, with no per-user fee.
