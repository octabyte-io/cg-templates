Buzzsprout is a hosted podcast platform: you upload episodes, and it publishes your feed to Apple Podcasts, Spotify and other apps, with a podcast website, stats and optional video.

People look for an alternative because Buzzsprout is priced by how many hours of audio you upload. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed 2 hours of uploads a month and kept episodes for 90 days, and paid plans started at $15 a month, billed yearly, for 72 hours of uploads a year. Castopod publishes as many podcasts and episodes as your server can hold, with no upload limits and your listener stats on your own server.
