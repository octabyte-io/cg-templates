Sapling, now sold as Kallidus Sapling, is a paid HR platform for growing companies, best known for onboarding and offboarding workflows that run through Slack and connect to other HR and IT tools, alongside its employee records.

People look for a Sapling alternative when they mainly use it for onboarding and want to stop paying for a full HR platform, or want new hires' details kept on their own server. A self-hosted ChiefOnboarding covers the onboarding and offboarding workflows with no subscription or fee per new hire, and works next to whichever HR system holds your employee records.
