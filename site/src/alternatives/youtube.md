YouTube is the world's largest video site: anyone can upload, and its recommendations bring viewers to channels of every kind. Organisations use it to share training, lectures and events because it is free to upload and easy to embed.

People look for an alternative when YouTube's way of working does not suit their videos. Ads can play before and during them, recommendations put other channels' videos beside theirs, and private or internal videos depend on Google's settings and rules. A self-hosted MediaCMS gives an organisation its own video site with its own branding and no ads, and keeps private videos on its own server; in return, it brings no audience and you pay for the server's disk and bandwidth.
