RoomSketcher is a subscription service for drawing floor plans and showing homes in 3D, used by homeowners, estate agents and interior designers, with polished 2D plans and 3D photos.

People look for a RoomSketcher alternative when they draw plans now and then and don't want a subscription, or want the plans on their own server. Pascal Editor draws floor plans in the browser, shows them in 3D and exports them as a PDF or as 3D files, with no project limit.
