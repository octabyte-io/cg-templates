Google Classroom is Google's online classroom for schools: teachers post assignments and materials, learners hand in work, and teachers grade it, all tied to Google accounts and Google Drive. It is free for schools, with paid Google Workspace for Education tiers that add more.

Schools, libraries and learning centres look for an alternative when their internet connection is slow, costly or missing, or when they would rather keep learners' names and progress on their own server than in Google's. A self-hosted Kolibri brings a ready-made library of lessons that works offline, with classes, lessons, quizzes and progress reports; in return, it has no file hand-in or grading of written work.
