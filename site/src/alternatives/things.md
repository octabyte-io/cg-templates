Things, from Cultured Code, is a to-do app for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, with areas, projects and Today, Upcoming and Someday lists. It is a one-time purchase, bought separately for Mac and for iPhone and iPad, and syncs through its own Things Cloud.

People look for an alternative when they use a Windows computer or an Android phone, share tasks with others, or want their to-do list on a server of their own. A self-hosted tududi organises work the same way, with areas, projects and a Today list, and opens in any browser on any system.
