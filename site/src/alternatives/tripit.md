TripIt builds a travel itinerary from the booking confirmations you forward to it, so flights, trains and hotels appear in one place. TripIt Pro, a paid subscription, adds flight status alerts, seat and fare tracking and check-in reminders.

People look for an alternative because TripIt reads every travel email you send it and keeps your plans in its cloud, and the most useful alerts are paid. A self-hosted TREK keeps bookings and plans on your own server and is made for travelling together: everyone on the trip sees the same itinerary, map, costs and packing list.
