Farmbrite is cloud farm management software for livestock, crop and mixed farms. It keeps records, tasks and plans alongside accounting, inventory, sales and an online store, for a monthly or yearly subscription.

Farmers look for an alternative because the subscription is an ongoing cost, and because years of farm records sit in someone else's cloud. A self-hosted farmOS keeps detailed field, crop and livestock records on your own server, with every feature free, and lets you export everything as CSV or through its API. It suits farms that want thorough records and handle the money side in another tool.
