Booqable is online rental software for businesses that rent out equipment such as cameras, bikes, party supplies and tools. It combines an online store where customers book items for their dates with inventory, orders, payments and documents, and it is sold on monthly subscription plans.

Rental shops look for an alternative when the monthly plan weighs on a small business, or when they want their storefront, customers and bookings on a server of their own. A self-hosted Louez gives a small shop an online storefront with real-time availability, a booking calendar, stock, contracts and customer records, with no subscription and no fee per booking.
