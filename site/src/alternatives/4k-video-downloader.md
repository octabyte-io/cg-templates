4K Video Downloader Plus is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux that saves videos, playlists, channels and subtitles from YouTube and other sites. It has a free Starter plan with basic features and paid licences that unlock more.

People look for an alternative when they want one place for the whole team or household instead of an app on every computer, or no plan limits. MeTube runs on your own server as a web page that works from any browser or phone, keeps a shared queue of downloads, and follows channels for you. Use it for videos you are allowed to save, such as your own channel's uploads or a recorded webinar.
