Doodle is best known for its group polls: an organiser suggests a few times, everyone marks when they can make it, and the best time wins. It also offers booking pages, one-to-one meetings and sign-up sheets, with calendar connections.

People look for an alternative because Doodle's free plan shows ads to organisers and participants and limits how many group polls you can run, while removing the ads and limits needs a paid plan priced per seat. A self-hosted Rallly runs group polls with no ads and no account needed to vote, and keeps the responses on your own server. It is free for one organiser; more signed-in users need a one-time licence key.
