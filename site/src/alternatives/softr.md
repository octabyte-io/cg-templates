Softr is a hosted no-code builder for internal tools and client portals on top of data in Airtable, Google Sheets and other sources.

Teams look for an alternative when they want internal tools running on their own server, next to their own data, rather than on a hosted plan. A self-hosted ToolJet builds internal tools with no seat limit, though it is more technical and is not made for public portals.
