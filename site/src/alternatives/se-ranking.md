SE Ranking is an SEO platform that started as a rank tracker and now adds site audits, backlink checks, competitor research and client reports for agencies. Its plans are priced by how many keywords you track and how often they are checked.

People look for an alternative because the price climbs with each keyword and with daily checks, so tracking many keywords for many sites gets expensive. A self-hosted SerpBear has no keyword cap: you pay a scraping service per check and keep the ranking history on your own server.
