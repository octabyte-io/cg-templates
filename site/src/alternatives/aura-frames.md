Aura makes Wi-Fi digital photo frames that family members fill from Aura's phone app, with the photos kept in Aura's cloud. The frames are polished and quick to set up.

People look for an alternative when they want family photos kept in their own photo library rather than a frame maker's cloud, or want to use screens they already own instead of buying a frame for each room. A self-hosted frame app shows albums from your own Immich server on a tablet, TV or spare screen, though it needs Immich and more setup than a boxed frame.
