Google Drive is Google's cloud storage, with Docs, Sheets and Slides for editing in the browser. Every Google account gets 15 GB free, shared between Drive, Gmail and Google Photos; more space comes with a Google One or Workspace plan.

People look for an alternative for privacy, cost and control. Your files sit on Google's servers under Google's terms, Workspace is charged per user, and storage plans have fixed sizes to upgrade through. A self-hosted file app such as Nextcloud keeps every file on your own server, has no price per user, and its only storage limit is your server's disk.
