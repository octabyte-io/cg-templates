Microsoft OneNote is a digital notebook: pages in sections and notebooks where you type, draw, paste and clip anywhere on the page, synced through your Microsoft account and OneDrive.

People look for an alternative when they want their notes outside Microsoft's cloud, or in plain files they can open anywhere. A self-hosted Poznote keeps notes, tasks and a diary on your own server as plain HTML and Markdown files, shared with the people you choose. It suits typed notes better than freeform pages and handwriting.
