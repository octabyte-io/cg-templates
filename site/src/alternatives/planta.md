Planta is a plant-care app for iPhone and Android. You add your plants and it gives you a care schedule for each one, with reminders, and it can identify plants and measure light. Many of its features come with a paid Premium subscription.

People look for an alternative when they want a free tool the whole household shares, or their plant records on their own server rather than in an app's cloud. A self-hosted HortusFox tracks every plant by location, with its care history, tasks and supplies, for everyone at home. In return you decide the care routine yourself, since it does not suggest one.
