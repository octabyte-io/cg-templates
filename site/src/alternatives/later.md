Later is a social media planner known for visual planning of Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest posts. You drag photos and videos onto a calendar, preview how a feed will look, and Later publishes them on time, with analytics and a link-in-bio page.

People look for an alternative because Later's plans limit how many social profiles and posts you get, and adding users or profiles costs more. A self-hosted scheduler such as Postiz has no such limits, though you register a developer app with each network yourself.
