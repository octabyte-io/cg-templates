Overcast is a podcast app for iPhone, known for audio features such as Smart Speed and Voice Boost, with a paid tier that removes adverts.

People look for an alternative when they also listen on Android, a computer or the web, or want their podcasts and listening progress on their own server. A self-hosted PinePods syncs subscriptions and progress across every platform and every person in the household, with no subscription.
