Obsidian is a note-taking app that keeps your notes as Markdown files on your own device, with links between notes, a graph view, a large collection of community plugins and official apps for computers and phones.

People look for an alternative because Obsidian is proprietary and its sync is a paid add-on. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the app was free, Obsidian Sync cost $4 per user a month and Publish $8 per site a month, both billed yearly. Trilium Notes is open source and has sync and web access built in: its desktop apps sync with your own server, and you can open your notes in any browser.
