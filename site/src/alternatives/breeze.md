Breeze ChMS, now Tithely Church Management, is cloud church management software known for being simple: people and families, tags, attendance, contributions, online giving and email, for a monthly fee.

Churches look for an alternative because the fee is ongoing and their member and giving records are kept in the vendor's cloud. A self-hosted ChurchCRM covers the same kind of records on your own server, free and with no subscription. The trade-off is online giving, which you would handle with a separate payment service.
