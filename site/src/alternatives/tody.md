Tody is a cleaning schedule app for iPhone, iPad and Android. You list the cleaning tasks for each room, and it shows which areas need attention most, based on how often you want each one done. Sharing the plan with others in your home is part of its premium version.

People look for an alternative when the whole household should see and share the chores without paying for it, or when they want to rotate the work fairly and keep their home's data on their own server. A self-hosted Donetick lets the whole household share the plan for free: everyone joins one group, each chore repeats on the schedule you type, and Donetick rotates who does it.
