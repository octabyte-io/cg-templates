Google Keep is Google's free note app for quick notes, checklists, photos, voice notes and reminders, with labels, colours, shared notes and apps for phones and the web.

People look for an alternative because Keep stores every note in a Google account, and they want their notes on a server they control. Others want Markdown, a timeline they can look back through, or a way to publish a note on the web. Memos is a small self-hosted app for quick notes and checklists that keeps everything on your own server.
