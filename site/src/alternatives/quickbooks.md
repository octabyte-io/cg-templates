QuickBooks Online, from Intuit, is accounting software for small businesses: invoices, bills, expenses, bank feeds, reports and, as an add-on, payroll. Many accountants and bookkeepers use it, and it connects to a large number of other apps.

Businesses look for an alternative mainly because of the price. QuickBooks is a monthly subscription that steps up from plan to plan, and payroll costs extra. When we checked its US pricing page in November 2026, plans ran from $38 to $340 a month. Your financial records also sit on Intuit's servers. A self-hosted Bigcapital does the core double-entry bookkeeping with no subscription and keeps your books on your own server, as long as you can do without payroll and tax filing in the same app.
