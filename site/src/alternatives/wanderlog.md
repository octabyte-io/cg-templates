Wanderlog is a trip planner in the cloud: you build an itinerary for each day on a map, collect places and bookings, and plan with friends who can edit the same trip. It is free to start, with a Pro subscription for extras.

People look for an alternative because every trip, booking and travel date is kept on Wanderlog's servers, and the extras sit behind a subscription. A self-hosted TREK keeps the shared map, day plans, bookings, costs and packing lists on your own server, with every feature free, so a family or group of friends can plan every trip in one place.
