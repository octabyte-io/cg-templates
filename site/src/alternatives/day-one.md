Day One is a journaling app for iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and the web. You write entries with photos, and paid Silver and Gold plans add sync on any number of devices, up to 30 photos per entry and video, with end-to-end encryption on every plan.

People look for an alternative because the features that make Day One work across devices are on a subscription, and the journal lives in Day One's cloud. Some want a diary that stays on a server they control. DailyTxT is free, keeps one encrypted entry per day with photos and files on your own server, and lets several people in a family keep their own diaries, each with their own key. In return, it has no native apps and cannot bring your Day One entries across.
