LabWare LIMS is an enterprise laboratory information management system from LabWare, offered cloud-hosted, as a service or installed on your own servers, with an electronic lab notebook and modules for many industries. Prices are given only on request.

Labs look for an alternative because an enterprise LIMS is a large purchase, sold by quote, and more than many testing labs need. SENAITE is free and open source, runs on your own server and covers the work of a testing lab from sample intake to the published report, with no licence fee. It does much less than LabWare and you set it up yourself, so it suits labs that want to own a focused LIMS.
