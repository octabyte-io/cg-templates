Trello, from Atlassian, is a Kanban board app: cards move across lists, and Power-Ups add calendars, automations and integrations.

Teams look for an alternative because of its limits and per-user price. When we checked Trello's pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed 10 boards and 10 collaborators per workspace, and paid plans were charged per user. Others want their boards on their own server. A self-hosted app such as Vikunja or Leantime gives you boards with no board or user limits.
