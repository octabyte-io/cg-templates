moving: |
  Vikunja and Kan both import Trello boards: whoever runs the server adds a Trello app key to the app once, then each person connects their Trello account and picks the boards to bring in. Leantime has no Trello importer; bring your cards in through its CSV import of to-dos, or recreate the active boards by hand. Power-Ups and automations do not carry over in any of them, so note the ones your team depends on.

Trello, from Atlassian, is a Kanban board app: cards move across lists, and Power-Ups add calendars, automations and integrations.

Teams look for an alternative because of its limits and per-user price. When we checked Trello's pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed 10 boards and 10 collaborators per workspace, and paid plans were charged per user. Others want their boards on their own server. A self-hosted app such as Vikunja, Kan or Leantime gives you boards with no board or user limits.
