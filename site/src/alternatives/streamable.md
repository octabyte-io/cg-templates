Streamable is a hosted service for uploading short videos and sharing them by link, popular for game clips and sports highlights. The free plan caps uploads at 250 MB and 10 minutes and deletes videos after 90 days; paid plans keep them longer.

People look for an alternative when their clips keep disappearing, when they hit the size limit, or when they would rather keep a library of clips on their own server. A self-hosted Fireshare shares clips by link with no time limit, though it has no phone app and does not record clips for you.
