Workplace from Meta was Meta's social network for companies: a news feed, groups, chat and video in an app that looked like Facebook, used especially by organizations with many frontline staff.

Meta has closed Workplace, so every organization that relied on it has had to move. Many want the same familiar feed-and-groups idea without depending again on a vendor that can switch it off. HumHub gives you a private social network with spaces, feeds, profiles and phone apps on your own server, with no fee per member in the free Community Edition.
