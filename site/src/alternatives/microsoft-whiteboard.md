Microsoft Whiteboard is Microsoft's freeform digital whiteboard, part of Microsoft 365 and Teams, where people draw, write and add sticky notes together, with boards stored in the owner's OneDrive.

People look for an alternative because Microsoft has narrowed who can use it. Its support pages say the standalone Whiteboard apps for Windows, iPhone and Android retired on October 16, 2026, and Whiteboard now continues only for work or school accounts, on the web and in Teams; people with personal Microsoft accounts lost it. Teachers, tutors and small teams without a work Microsoft 365 plan, or who simply want boards on their own server, can run WBO: a shared whiteboard in any browser with no Microsoft account. In return, it has fewer tools than Whiteboard.
