Bitly is a link shortener and link management platform: it turns long web addresses into short ones, tracks clicks, makes QR codes and offers branded domains, campaigns and link-in-bio pages on paid plans.

People look for an alternative because Bitly's free plan is very limited and branded links cost a monthly fee. When we checked its pricing page in November 2026, the free plan allowed 5 links a month with no custom domain, and branded domains started on the Growth plan at $29 a month. A self-hosted Kutt gives you short links on your own domain with no link or domain limits, and keeps the click data on your server.
