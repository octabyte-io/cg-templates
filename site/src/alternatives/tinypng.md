TinyPNG is a popular website for making PNG, JPG and WebP images smaller without a visible loss in quality, from the company Tinify, which also sells an API and a WordPress plugin. Its free web tool takes a limited number of images at a time, and paid Web Pro and Web Ultra plans raise the limits.

People look for an alternative because of those limits and because every image is uploaded to TinyPNG's servers, where it is kept for up to 48 hours. That is a concern for private photos, client work and screenshots with personal details. MAZANOKE is open source and does the same everyday job, making images smaller, resizing them and converting formats, entirely in your browser, with no limits, no sign-up and no uploads.
