Canto is a hosted digital asset management (DAM) platform where teams store, find and share photos, videos and documents, with AI tagging, portals and many integrations. It is priced by quote.

Teams look for an alternative when the quote grows with every user and every gigabyte of storage, or when they want their media library on their own server. A self-hosted Phraseanet has no price per user or for storage, and is strong at cataloguing: metadata fields, a thesaurus and faceted search across photos, videos, audio and documents. In return it has no AI tagging, an older interface and admin screens that take time to learn, so it suits teams with someone to look after it.
