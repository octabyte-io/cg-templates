Medium is a blogging platform where anyone can publish stories, and members pay a monthly fee to read beyond a few free articles. Writers in its Partner Program earn a share of membership revenue based on reading time.

Writers look for an alternative to own their work and their readers. On Medium your stories sit on Medium's domain and design, the paywall and its earnings follow Medium's rules, and changes to recommendations or payouts can cut your reach or income overnight. You cannot set your own subscription price. A self-hosted blog such as Ghost or WordPress puts your writing on your own domain, lets you build an email list you keep, and lets you charge readers directly if you want to.
