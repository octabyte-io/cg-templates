RadioKing is a hosted web radio builder: you upload music, plan your programming and share a player, with statistics on your listeners, on a monthly plan.

People look for a RadioKing alternative when they want to stop paying monthly for a small or hobby station, or want their music and listener data on their own server. AzuraCast does the same station management on your own server with no monthly fee, though you take care of the server, its bandwidth and your music licences.
