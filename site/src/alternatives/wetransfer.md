WeTransfer sends large files by link: you upload files, add an email address or copy a link, and the person you send them to gets a download link that works for a limited time. Paid plans send bigger transfers, keep them longer and add branded pages.

People look for an alternative because the free plan limits how much you can send and how long it stays, and every file passes through and sits on WeTransfer's servers. A self-hosted PsiTransfer sends files by link from your own server, with no adverts and no account for anyone. FileBrowser Quantum does the same with share links that last as long as you choose, and it can also collect files from others through upload-only links. PicoShare sends one file per link, with links that can last a day or forever and guest links for collecting files, and ProjectSend gives regular clients a private page. With these four, the only size limit is your disk. Gokapi sends links that expire after a set number of downloads, with accounts for your team and file requests to receive files. Plik adds a login for each colleague, one-time downloads and optional end-to-end encryption, with up to 10 GB per file as Cloudgate sets it up. Sharry adds sign-in for senders and upload pages for collecting files from clients. Erugo adds what WeTransfer's paid plans offer: emailed links, download alerts and your own branding. Enclosed is for small files that must stay confidential, encrypted before they leave your browser. copyparty is a full web file server that also sends share links, resumes interrupted uploads and keeps files in folders after they are sent. Send adds end-to-end encryption on every file, with links that delete themselves after the last allowed download, up to 1 GB per upload. WulfVault keeps a download history for every file, with the recipient's email on sign-in downloads, plus teams with storage quotas and an audit log. With any of them, the limits are set on your own server, not by a plan.
