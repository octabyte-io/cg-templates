Huntr is an online job search tracker with AI tools: you save jobs from the web with its Chrome extension, track them on a board, create tailored resumes and cover letters, and fill in application forms automatically.

People look for an alternative because Huntr's free plan caps how many jobs you track and keeps unlimited AI resumes and tailoring for its paid Pro plan. Dossier tracks your applications and drafts tailored resumes and cover letters on your own server, with no plan or limits, and keeps your job search private. It is built for one person and has no browser extension or autofill.
