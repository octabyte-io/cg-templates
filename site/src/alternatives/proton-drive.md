Proton Drive is Proton's end-to-end encrypted cloud storage, with Proton Docs and Sheets for editing, desktop and phone apps, and 5 GB free before paid plans.

People look for an alternative when they want end-to-end encryption without trusting a hosted service with their storage, or need more space, more people or more kinds of documents than their plan covers. CryptPad is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted office suite you run on your own server: documents, spreadsheets, presentations, forms and kanban boards that several people edit at once, which the server itself cannot read. It does not sync the files on your computer.
