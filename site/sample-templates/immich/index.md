Immich backs up the photos and videos from your phone automatically and lets you browse them on any device, much like Google Photos or iCloud Photos. Once the app is set up, every new picture uploads in the background, in its original quality.

Finding a picture is quick. Immich recognises faces so you can see every photo of one person, shows your photos on a map, and lets you search in plain words such as "dog on the beach". You can make shared albums and send links to friends or clients. A wedding photographer, for example, could back up every shoot and share a private album with each couple.

Cloudgate installs Immich on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign in becomes the admin and can add more people, each with their own private library. Immich has apps for iPhone and Android and works in any web browser.
