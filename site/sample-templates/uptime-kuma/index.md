Uptime Kuma keeps an eye on your websites and online services and tells you the moment one stops working, so you hear about it before your customers do. It checks each address as often as you like and sends you a message if it does not answer.

Alerts can go to email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Telegram, Discord, SMS and many other channels. You can also publish a status page that shows customers which services are working and which are being fixed. An online shop, for example, can watch its storefront, payment page and booking system, and get a phone alert within a minute if checkout breaks at night.

Cloudgate installs Uptime Kuma on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open it creates the admin account. For the most reliable alerts, run it on a different server from the websites it watches.
