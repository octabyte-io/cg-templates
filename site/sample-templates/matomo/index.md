Matomo is complete website analytics, much like Google Analytics, but with your visitors' data kept on your own server. It shows who visits your website, where they came from, which pages they read and what they do before they buy or sign up.

You can set goals, follow visitors through a checkout funnel, track online shop sales and compare campaigns. It can also run without cookies, which helps you follow privacy rules such as GDPR. A marketing team, for example, can see which of last month's newsletters and ads brought in paying customers, not just clicks.

Cloudgate installs Matomo on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to open it runs a short setup wizard, creates the admin account and adds the first website. Matomo has plugins for WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce and other platforms, and can import your history from Google Analytics.
