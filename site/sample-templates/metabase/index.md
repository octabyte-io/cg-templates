Metabase helps you understand how your business is doing by turning the data you already collect into charts and dashboards. You pick what you want to see, such as orders per week or revenue by region, and Metabase draws it. No spreadsheets to update by hand.

It suits teams that have their data in a database, for example from an online shop, a booking system or an app, and want everyone to see the same numbers. An operations manager, for example, can build a dashboard of daily orders, returns and delivery times, and have it emailed to the team every Monday.

Cloudgate installs Metabase on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign in becomes the admin, connects your data and invites colleagues. Metabase reads common business databases, including PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, BigQuery and Snowflake.
