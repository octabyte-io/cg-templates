n8n lets you connect the online tools your business already uses and have them work together on their own. When a customer fills in a form, n8n can add them to a spreadsheet, send your team a chat message and email the customer a welcome note, without anyone copying and pasting.

You build these workflows on a visual board by dragging blocks and joining them with lines. It works with more than 400 services, including Google Sheets, Gmail, Slack, Shopify, HubSpot and Stripe. A workflow can start on a schedule, when an email arrives, or when another app sends it a message.

Cloudgate installs n8n on a server you connect and gives it its own secure web address, so other services can reach your workflows from the internet. The first person to sign in becomes the owner and can invite the rest of the team. Because it runs on your server, there is no limit on how many workflows you run or how often they run.
