Outline is a wiki for your team: one place for the handbook, how-to guides, policies and meeting notes. Documents are grouped into collections, such as Sales or Onboarding, and anyone can search across all of them in a second.

The editor is quick and uncluttered. Several people can work on the same page at once, add images and tables, and link pages to each other. An office manager, for example, can write the onboarding checklist once, keep it up to date, and send every new starter a single link instead of a stack of attachments.

Cloudgate installs Outline on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. People sign in with the work accounts they already have, through a sign-in service such as Google Workspace, Microsoft or Okta that you connect during setup. The first person to sign in becomes the admin. Outline imports documents from Notion, Confluence, Word and Markdown.
