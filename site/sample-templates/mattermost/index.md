Mattermost gives your team one place to talk. Conversations are organised in channels for each team, project or topic, and people can message each other directly, reply in threads, share files and start a quick call. If your team has used Slack, they will know their way around within minutes.

Because Mattermost runs on your own server, your whole message history is kept and searchable, without paying extra to see older messages. A growing company, for example, can create a channel for every client project so that decisions are easy to find months later, even after people have moved on.

Cloudgate installs Mattermost on a server you connect and gives it a secure web address. The first person to sign up becomes the admin and invites the team. There are apps for Windows, Mac, Linux, iPhone and Android, and Mattermost connects to tools such as GitHub, Jira and Zoom.
